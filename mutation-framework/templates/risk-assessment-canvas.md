# Risk Assessment Canvas

**Version 1.0-draft** · Use with: any proposed experiment or initiative before funding

A one-page canvas to make the risk of an innovation bet explicit *before*
money and reputation are attached. Fill it in one sitting, as a group, in
under an hour. If you can't fill a box, that gap is your first risk.

### 4. Blast radius

### 5. Mutation exposure (the risk of NOT doing it)
*What does it cost to be wrong in the other direction? If the signal behind this bet is real and we sit still, what happens in 12–24 months? This box exists because boards systematically price action risk and ignore inaction risk.*

## Facilitation notes

- Fill **section 2 before section 1 is polished** — kill criteria written after a pitch is loved are theater.
- Section 5 is the canvas's reason to exist. An initiative with moderate blast radius and severe mutation exposure should beat a safe initiative with zero upside. Make that comparison visible at the portfolio level (see [innovation-kpi-scorecard.md](innovation-kpi-scorecard.md)).
- Revisit the canvas at every check date. A canvas filed and forgotten is a risk register, and risk registers don't change decisions.
