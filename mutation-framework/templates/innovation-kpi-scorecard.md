# Innovation KPI Scorecard

**Version 1.0-draft** · Use with: quarterly portfolio review (Framework Guide §7)

Most innovation KPIs measure activity (ideas submitted, workshops held).
This scorecard measures **capability and flow** — the things that predict
whether the next signal becomes a response in time. Twelve KPIs, three per
quadrant. Review quarterly; trend matters more than absolute value.

## Quadrant 1 — Sensing

| KPI | Definition | Healthy direction |
|---|---|---|
| Signal lead time | Days between a signal first being externally visible and it appearing in your SignalNet | ↓ |
| Source diversity | Distinct source types contributing signals this quarter (target ≥ 5) | ↑ |
| Miss audit rate | Of surprises this quarter, % that were in the log but ignored vs. never logged | track both |

## Quadrant 2 — Deciding

| KPI | Definition | Healthy direction |
|---|---|---|
| Signal-to-decision time | Median days from "signal escalated" to a funded yes/no | ↓ |
| Reallocation ratio | % of innovation budget that moved between initiatives this quarter | ↑ (0% means locked budgets) |
| Kill latency | Median days an initiative survives past its own kill criteria | → 0 |

## Quadrant 3 — Experimenting

| KPI | Definition | Healthy direction |
|---|---|---|
| Experiment throughput | Experiments started AND concluded this quarter | ↑ |
| Cost per learning | Median spend per concluded experiment (cheaper experiments = more shots) | ↓ |
| Learning capture rate | % of concluded experiments (incl. failures) with a written, findable result | → 100% |

## Quadrant 4 — Scaling

| KPI | Definition | Healthy direction |
|---|---|---|
| Graduation rate | % of successful experiments that reached production/rollout within 2 quarters | ↑ |
| Time to scale | Median days from "experiment validated" to "available to all intended users" | ↓ |
| Regression check | % of scaled initiatives still healthy 2 quarters later | ↑ |

## Anti-gaming notes

- **Never set targets on throughput alone** — you'll get 50 trivial experiments. Pair it with graduation rate.
- **Kill latency is the most honest KPI on this page.** It is also the one most likely to be quietly dropped. If it disappears from your review deck, ask why.
- Three consecutive quarters of 0% reallocation ratio means your "rolling budget" is annual budgeting with extra meetings.
