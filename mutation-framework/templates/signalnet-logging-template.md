# SignalNet Logging Template

**Version 1.0-draft** · Use with: weekly signal review (see Framework Guide §7)

A SignalNet is a shared, living log of weak signals — early evidence of
technological, market, or behavioral change that could matter to your
organization — distributed and semi-anonymous, so any employee can log
without political consequence (Operating Manual, Appendix E). It is the operational backbone of the **Signal Sensitivity**
dimension. One log, one owner, one weekly cadence.

## The log

Copy this table into your team's wiki, spreadsheet, or tracker. One row per
signal. Never delete rows — resolved signals are your calibration history.

| Field | What goes here |
|---|---|
| **ID** | Sequential (S-001, S-002 …) |
| **Date logged** | When it entered the net |
| **Signal** | One sentence: what was observed, concretely |
| **Source** | Where it came from (customer call, paper, competitor move, frontline report, regulator, …) |
| **Spotted by** | Who logged it — track this; your best sensors deserve recognition |
| **Category** | Technology / Market / Customer behavior / Regulatory / Talent / Other |
| **Strength** | Weak (1 source, speculative) / Medium (2–3 independent sources) / Strong (converging evidence) |
| **Potential impact** | If this signal is real, what does it threaten or enable for us? One sentence. |
| **Status** | New / Watching / Investigating / Acted / Faded |
| **Next review** | Date this row must be looked at again |
| **Disposition** | Filled when status reaches Acted or Faded: what we did, or why we let it go |

## Weekly signal review (30 minutes, fixed agenda)

1. **New signals (10 min)** — each new row gets 60 seconds from its spotter. No debate yet; just understanding.
2. **Status changes (10 min)** — which Watching signals strengthened or faded? Promote, demote, or hold.
3. **One decision (10 min)** — pick at most one signal to escalate to an investigation or experiment this week. One. Forced prioritization is the point.

## Rules that keep a SignalNet alive

- **Logging is cheap.** A row takes two minutes. If logging feels like paperwork, cut fields, not the habit.
- **No signal is too small** to log; a signal *is* too small to escalate. The net filters — the mesh shouldn't.
- **Faded is a success state.** A signal that faded after being watched cost you almost nothing. The expensive failure is the signal never logged.
- **Review the misses quarterly.** When something surprises you, check the log: was it there and ignored (process failure) or never logged (sensing failure)? They have different fixes.
