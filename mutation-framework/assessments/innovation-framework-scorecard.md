# Innovation Framework Scorecard

**Version 1.0-draft** · 16 questions · 4 layers · ~8 minutes

> Machine-readable source of truth: [`innovation-framework-scorecard.yaml`](innovation-framework-scorecard.yaml)

## What this measures

The companion instrument to the [Mutation Readiness Scorecard](mutation-readiness-scorecard.md).
That scorecard measures your organization's *general capacity to adapt*; this
one measures maturity against the **four layers of the Innovation Framework**
for organizations actively implementing it:

1. **AI Enablement** — AI in real workflows with measured impact, not pilots.
2. **Technical Backbone** — the platform substrate that lets experiments graduate to production.
3. **Lean Portfolio & Value Streams** — funding that flows and reallocates, replacing annual project budgets.
4. **Human Mutation** — leadership behavior, psychological safety, and learning culture. Without this layer the other three regress within 18 months.

## How to score

Each question is rated 1–5 on a maturity scale:
1 = Not started · 2 = Isolated pockets · 3 = Established in places · 4 = Standard practice · 5 = Continuously improving.

Points per question = (rating − 1). Total range 0–64.

| Band | Score | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **Forming** | 0–26 | The framework exists on paper; adoption is sponsor-driven and fragile. |
| **Performing** | 27–47 | Two or more layers genuinely operational; watch for strong layers masking weak ones. |
| **Transforming** | 48–64 | All four layers reinforce each other and survive leadership rotation. |

**Sequencing rule:** always invest in your *weakest* layer first. The layers
compound — a 4-4-4-1 profile performs worse over time than a 3-3-3-3 one,
because Human Mutation decay drags the rest down.

## The questions

### Layer 1 — AI Enablement
1. **AE1** — AI-assisted workflows are in production in at least three distinct functions.
2. **AE2** — We measure before/after cycle time or quality for each AI-assisted workflow.
3. **AE3** — Model and tool selection follows a documented evaluation process, not vendor enthusiasm.
4. **AE4** — Every AI deployment has a named owner responsible for drift, quality, and guardrails.

### Layer 2 — Technical Backbone
5. **TB1** — A new experiment can reach a production-like environment in under a week.
6. **TB2** — Observability is good enough that we detect regressions before customers report them.
7. **TB3** — Core systems expose APIs that internal teams can build against without negotiation.
8. **TB4** — Technical debt is tracked and budgeted against, not just lamented.

### Layer 3 — Lean Portfolio & Value Streams
9. **LP1** — Funding flows to value streams, not to projects with fixed annual budgets.
10. **LP2** — Portfolio review happens at least quarterly with real reallocation, not rubber-stamping.
11. **LP3** — Every initiative has explicit leading indicators reviewed on a cadence.
12. **LP4** — Stopping an initiative releases its people and budget within one cycle.

### Layer 4 — Human Mutation
13. **HM1** — Leaders run and visibly learn from their own experiments.
14. **HM2** — Communities of practice meet regularly and influence real decisions.
15. **HM3** — Career progression rewards people who adapt, teach, and de-risk change.
16. **HM4** — Retrospectives at every level produce changes that stick.
