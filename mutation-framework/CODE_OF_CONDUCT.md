# Code of Conduct

This code applies to all framework spaces: this repository (issues, PRs,
discussions), the community Discord, community calls, and any event run
under the framework's name.

## Our standard

We are practitioners helping organizations change, which means we discuss
real companies, real failures, and real careers. That demands a higher bar
than ordinary open-source courtesy:

1. **Be direct about ideas, generous with people.** "This question won't
   survive contact with a hostile CFO" is great feedback. Commentary about
   the person who wrote the question is not.
2. **Protect the subjects of case studies.** Never de-anonymize a case, in
   any channel, even speculatively. Naming a company "because it's
   obviously them" is treated as seriously as doxxing a person.
3. **No harassment.** No personal attacks, discriminatory language, sexual
   attention, sustained disruption, or publishing others' private
   information. The full list of behaviors we mean is the standard one; if
   you'd need a lawyer to tell you whether something counts, don't do it.
4. **No covert selling.** Consultants and facilitators are core members of
   this community and may identify their affiliation openly. Astroturfing,
   DM cold-pitching people who asked a question, and disguising marketing
   as advice will get you removed faster than rudeness will.
5. **Honest numbers.** Fabricating field results or scorecard data in a
   contribution is grounds for permanent removal.

## Enforcement

Reports go to the maintainer at **[conduct contact email — to be set by
owner before community launch]**. All reports are read by a human, kept
confidential, and acknowledged within 72 hours.

Consequences scale: private warning → public warning → temporary ban →
permanent ban. Case-study de-anonymization, harassment, and fabricated
data may skip steps. Enforcement decisions can be appealed once, in
writing, to the same contact.
