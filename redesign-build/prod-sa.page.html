None. The course suits executives, managers, and change agents leading an Agile transformation — no prior SAFe experience required.

Yes — SAFe Agilist is one of the most in-demand Agile credentials, with strong salary and role growth (see the demand stats above). Your first exam attempt and a money-back pass guarantee are included.
