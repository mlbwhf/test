AI-Generated Content Statistics 2026

AI-generated output surged again in 2025–26 — but at very different speeds across text, code, images, audio and video. Every figure is linked to its primary source, dated, and rated for confidence.

By The AI Index · Updated July 2026 · 9 min read · 12 sourced figures

Key takeaways

  • Code — fastest sustained climb. AI now writes 30%+ of new code at Google, up from 25%. (Alphabet earnings)
  • Audio — fastest surge. 44% of tracks uploaded to Deezer daily are fully AI (up from 18%) — though most is upload-side spam. (Deezer)
  • Images — industrial scale. Adobe Firefly has produced 24 billion+ images; nearly half of Adobe Stock is now AI. (Adobe / PetaPixel)
  • Text plateaued, video fragile. AI-written web articles crossed ~50% then flattened; OpenAI discontinued Sora on unsustainable economics. (Graphite / OpenAI)

AI-generated text

AI-written articles first surpassed human-written ones on the open web in November 2024, reaching 51.7% of sampled articles by May 2025 (from 2.2% in January 2020) — but the share has been roughly flat near parity since mid-2024, an important nuance against “AI will swallow the web” narratives (Graphite, detector study of ~65,000 URLs). Other detectors measuring different samples diverge: Ahrefs found 74.2% of new pages contain some AI content (Apr 2025), while Originality.ai found only ~17% of top-20 Google results are AI (Sep 2025). Consumer demand is the engine: writing is the #1 work use of ChatGPT, and non-work usage rose from 53% to 73% of messages in a year (OpenAI / NBER).

AI-generated code

Code is the steepest, most consistent climb. Google’s share of AI-generated new code went from “more than a quarter” (Q3 2024 earnings) to “well over 30%” (Q1 2025 earnings) (Alphabet 8-K). Microsoft’s Satya Nadella put AI-written code in Microsoft repos at 20–30% (Apr 2025). GitHub Copilot grew from 15M to 20M+ users in one quarter. The counter-trend: developer trust fell even as adoption rose — distrust of AI accuracy went from 31% to 46% (Stack Overflow 2025).

AI-generated images

The cleanest primary-sourced image series is Adobe Firefly: from 1 billion generations within three months of its March 2023 launch, to 13 billion by October 2024, to 24 billion+ by June 2025 — growth running well over a billion new generations a month (Adobe Newsroom). The most striking measured share comes from stock libraries: an independent count found Adobe Stock went from 2.5% AI-generated (May 2023) to 47.85% (April 2025) — roughly a 40× rise in two years (Kneschke count via PetaPixel). Everypixel’s landmark industry figure was 15 billion+ AI images by mid-2023 (Everypixel). Firefly cumulative generations climbed from 1B (Jun 2023) to 13B (Oct 2024) to 24B+ (Jun 2025).

AI-generated audio & music

Audio shows the most aggressive trajectory of all. Deezer’s share of fully AI-generated daily uploads rose from ~10% (Jan 2025) to 18% (Apr 2025) to 28% (Sep 2025) to 44% (Apr 2026) — with daily volume climbing from ~10,000 to ~75,000 tracks (Deezer / TechCrunch). Crucially, AI tracks are only 1–3% of actual streams and ~85% are flagged fraudulent and demonetized — i.e., this is upload-side spam, not listener demand. Capital is pouring in regardless: Suno went from a $2.45B to a $5.4B valuation in ~7 months; ElevenLabs from $3.3B to $11B (and $330M+ ARR) in ~13 months.

AI-generated video

Video is the newest frontier — and the first to show a major reversal. OpenAI’s Sora 2 app launched Sept 30 2025 and crossed 1 million downloads in under five days (faster than ChatGPT), then OpenAI discontinued Sora in 2026 on “completely unsustainable” per-day compute economics. The durable traction sits in the embedded-in-suite and enterprise model: Google Veo / Flow reported hundreds of millions of generated videos within months, and Runway raised to a $5.3B valuation (Feb 2026). Marketer adoption roughly doubled off a low base — Wyzowl reports AI-video use rising from ~1-in-3 (2024) to 51% (2025) to 63% (2026), though those survey figures could not be confirmed to the primary report.

“% AI-generated” figures for text and music are detector estimates with error bars, not ground truth, and vendors disagree (Graphite ~52% vs Originality.ai ~17%). Company figures (Google, Adobe, Deezer) are self-reported. Wyzowl survey figures unconfirmed to primary.

Frequently asked

How much content is AI-generated in 2026?

It varies sharply by type. AI writes 30%+ of new code at Google, accounts for 44% of daily music uploads on Deezer, and roughly half of new web articles (a detector estimate). Adobe Firefly alone has generated 24 billion+ images, and nearly half of Adobe Stock’s library is now AI-generated.

Which type of AI content is growing fastest?

By share growth, AI-generated music uploads grew fastest — Deezer’s fully-AI share more than doubled year-over-year (18% to 44%). AI code is the fastest sustained enterprise climb. AI text, by contrast, has plateaued near 50% of new web articles.

Are these “% AI-generated” numbers reliable?

Treat them as estimates. Text and music shares come from AI detectors with documented error rates, and different vendors disagree (Graphite ~52% of articles vs Originality.ai ~17% of top Google results). Company figures (Google, Adobe, Deezer) are self-reported internal metrics.
