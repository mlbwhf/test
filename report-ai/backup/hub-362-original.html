State of AI in 2022 — ChatGPT Arrives

~50% org adoption. ChatGPT hits 100M users in two months — the fastest consumer app launch on record.

State of AI in 2023 — The Breakout Year

33% using generative AI. GPT-4, Claude 2, Llama 2 and Gemini all ship within a single year.

State of AI in 2024 — The Inflection Year

78% org use. $252.3B invested. SWE-bench surges from 4.4% to 71.7% as benchmarks break open.

88% org use. $37B GenAI spend. 61% of global VC. Near-universal adoption with value concentrating at the frontier.
