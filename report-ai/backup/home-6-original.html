Straight answers, sourced

Is "everyone's using AI" just hype?

Not really — 88% of organizations now use AI in at least one function. The catch: only a slim minority see real profit yet. (McKinsey)

Is the world spending trillions on AI?

Yes — about $2.5 trillion in 2026, more than the GDP of all but a handful of countries. (Gartner)

Is AI getting cheaper or more expensive?

Both. Running yesterday's models got ~280× cheaper in two years. (Stanford HAI)
