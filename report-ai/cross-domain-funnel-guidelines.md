# Cross-Domain Funnel & Linking — De-Risked Guidelines
# This is your Section 4, corrected to avoid a PBN / link-scheme footprint while keeping the lead flow.

## The problem with the original "exact-match anchors to the hub" idea
One owner + multiple interlinked domains + exact-match commercial anchors all pointing at one money
site (agile-agilist.com) is the textbook footprint Google classifies as a Private Blog Network (PBN).
Google can connect ownership via registrant, shared hosting/IP, shared GA4/GTM, and reciprocal schema.
Result: links devalued or the network penalized. So we keep the FUNNEL, drop the FOOTPRINT.

## The 6 rules

1. **Funnel by UX, not by link juice.** Move people with buttons, CTAs, and diagnostic-result routing —
   not with keyword-stuffed anchor links engineered to pass PageRank.

2. **Vary anchor text; never exact-match commercial.**
   - ❌ "Agile Agilist SPCT-Led Lean Portfolio Management (LPM) programs"
   - ✅ "talk to the team at Agile Agilist", "book an alignment session", "see the enterprise programs"

3. **Mark commercial cross-links appropriately.** For money-anchor links between your own properties,
   use `rel="nofollow"` (or `sponsored`). You still get the click and the lead; you don't manufacture
   a ranking signal. Editorial/citation links (see rule 4) can stay followed.

4. **Citations are the ONE place to link naturally + followed.** When implementing-safe.com or
   agile-agilist.com cites real report-ai.org data, that's a genuine editorial reference — link it
   naturally: "...per the 2026 Report AI Enterprise AI Index" → report-ai.org. Real data earns real links.
   (Requires the data to actually exist — see the data-integrity warning in the cert hub file.)

5. **Don't reciprocate every link.** A clean research→tactics→execution flow is natural. Every site
   linking to every other site with commercial anchors is not. Keep most flow one-directional and sparse.

6. **Uniform Organization schema = transparency, not manipulation.** Declaring report-ai.org as the
   research arm and agile-agilist.com as the execution provider (see ecosystem schema file) HELPS Google
   map you accurately. That's allowed and good. The risk is never the schema — it's the anchor-text spam.

## Direction of flow (intended funnel)
```
scaledagile101.com ─(informational)→ safe-spc.com ─(commercial)→ agile-agilist.com (hub)
report-ai.org ─(research/citation)→ implementing-safe.com ─(tactical)→ agile-agilist.com (hub)
report-ai.org ─(diagnostic result)→ agile-agilist.com (premium) / implementing-safe.com (playbook)
```
Note: the two bottom-left/right nodes (safe-spc.com, scaledagile101.com) remain GATED on the
Gold-Partner trademark clearance — build them only after written approval, else keep as redirects.

## Quick compliance checklist before publishing any cross-link
- [ ] Anchor text is natural/varied, not exact-match commercial
- [ ] Commercial cross-links are rel="nofollow"/"sponsored"
- [ ] Any followed link to a "report" is backed by REAL published data
- [ ] Flow is mostly one-directional, not all-reciprocal
- [ ] Ecosystem Organization schema present on the page
