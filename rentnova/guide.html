Guide

From one home
to three working units.

A full walk-through of the six steps we run for every owner — what happens, what it costs, what we need from you. Read it end-to-end or jump to a step.

01
Feasibility

Figure out what your lot allows.

Time1–2 weeks
Cost$0 to you
Deliverable1-page memo

What happens

We pull your lot's zoning, bylaw setbacks, parking minimums and floor-area ratios. Then we walk the lot, mark what's physically buildable, and put dollar ranges to each scenario that fits — main-only refresh, plus a basement suite, plus a garden suite, full triplex conversion.

You leave Step 01 with concrete numbers for your specific address, not generic ranges off a calculator.

What you give us

  • Your address
  • A few photos of the lot, or a 30-min window to walk it
  • Your goals: live in one unit, rent them all, mix
  • Any survey or floor plan you already have
02
Design

Architectural drawings, ready for permit.

Time4–8 weeks
CostFixed at sign-off
DeliverablePermit-ready set

What happens

A 3-D massing study first — one or two cheap, fast options before any drafting time goes in. Once you pick a direction we lock the scope and produce the permit package: site plan, floor plans, elevations, sections, schedules, energy and structural notes.

We hand the package to the city. You don't have to learn how building department portals work.

What you give us

  • Feedback on the massing options (1–2 rounds)
  • Sign-off on the final layout
  • Decisions on the half-dozen finishes the design needs to lock
03
Build

Permits, demo, build, inspections.

Time3–6 months
CostPer fixed scope
DeliverableInspected, habitable units

What happens

Vetted GCs we run jobs with weekly — not a one-off subcontractor we found for your project. The scope is locked at design sign-off, so the build doesn't drift; change orders are explicit, signed, and itemised.

You get a private dashboard with weekly site photos, the change-order log, and the run-rate against your budget.

What you give us

  • Visit the site whenever you want (just text first)
  • Respond to spec questions within 48 hours
  • Sign off on rough-in inspections when they pass
04
Furnish

Turn-key fit and finish.

Time2–3 weeks
Cost$8k–$25k / unit
DeliverableGuest-ready unit

What happens

Furniture, mattresses, linens, kitchen, art, signage. Each unit gets a documented spec sheet — so when something breaks a year from now the replacement matches what was there.

We bias to durable mid-tier pieces that read better on camera than they cost. Guests notice; you don't pay for it twice.

What you give us

  • A few style nudges if you have strong preferences
  • Otherwise — we choose. You'll see the spec before we order.
05
List

Photograph, write, price, publish.

Time1 week
CostIncluded
DeliverableLive listings + direct site

What happens

Professional photo shoot. Listing copy written by someone who's read enough comps to know what converts. Dynamic pricing wired up on Airbnb, Booking.com, and a direct-book page on your own subdomain.

Your unit goes live across every channel at the same time, with a co-ordinated launch promo to seed early reviews.

What you give us

  • Sign the management agreement
  • That's it
06
Manage

Run it. Forever.

TimeOngoing
CostRevenue-share
DeliverableMonthly statement

What happens

24/7 guest comms, turnovers, restocking, maintenance triage. We send a monthly statement with revenue, fees, occupancy, ADR and a year-end summary you can hand to your accountant without re-typing anything.

The revenue-share means we win when your unit performs — there's no flat fee that ignores whether occupancy is 90% or 40%.

What you give us

  • A credit card on file for guest damage holds
  • Otherwise — nothing

Common questions.

If yours isn't here, send it over — we'll answer in the feasibility call.

How much does the whole thing usually cost?

Lot-dependent. A basement suite alone runs $80k–$140k all-in, including design and furnishing. Adding a garden suite typically lands $300k–$500k. The Step 01 feasibility memo gives you the numbers for your specific lot — that's the whole point of starting there.

What's the revenue-share percentage?

Depends on scope. Pure management is in the 18–24% range. Build-plus-manage gets a different split because we have more skin in the game. Both are spelled out in your engagement letter — no surprises mid-stream.

Do I need to move out during construction?

For a basement-only build, no — the work happens below grade and the main floor stays habitable. Garden-suite builds happen entirely outside your home. Full multi-unit conversions usually need a 4–8 week stretch where you're either travelling or in a furnished short-stay; we'll help arrange one.

What if I want to use one of the units myself?

Common. We design and run the rest, and you keep the keys to "yours." The revenue-share only applies to the units we actually manage. Owners who keep the main residence and short-stay the basement + garden suite are our most common setup.

What if it doesn't pencil out?

The feasibility memo will say so. We'd rather tell you "your lot doesn't justify the build" in week two than two years in. About 1 in 5 lots we look at don't move past Step 01 — and that's a useful answer too.

Can you do this on a property I don't own yet?

Yes — sometimes we run the feasibility before close so you know what you're buying. Useful when the listing photos hide whether the basement can legally become a suite.

Who owns the listings?

You. The direct-book URL, the Airbnb and Booking.com accounts, the reviews — all in your name. If you ever leave us, it stays with the property.

What's the minimum timeline from "yes" to "first guest"?

Basement suite: ~4 months. Garden suite: ~7 months. Full multiplex conversion: 12–18 months depending on city processing. Permit timelines vary more than build timelines.

Do you work outside the GTA?

No. We stay inside a 1-hour radius from Mississauga so we can actually be on-site when something breaks. Saying no a lot is how we keep saying yes well.
