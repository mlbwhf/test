# Agile Agilist

> Agile Agilist is a SAFe® Gold Partner delivering SPCT-led Scaled Agile Framework (SAFe) certification, enterprise Agile transformation, innovation, and AI-Native leadership training across Toronto, Canada, and North America. The practice combines official SAFe certification courses with an AI-Native operating model and assessment tooling to help enterprises move from framework adoption to measurable business agility.

## SAFe Certification Training — Advanced
- [Advanced SAFe® Practice Consultant (ASPC)](https://agile-agilist.com/training/adv-safe/aspc/): Advanced certification for senior change agents leading large-scale, multi-train, AI-native SAFe transformations.
- [Implementing SAFe® — SPC Certification](https://agile-agilist.com/training/adv-safe/spc/): SAFe Practice Consultant certification authorizing you to train and coach Agile Release Trains and lead enterprise transformations.
- [SAFe® Release Train Engineer (RTE)](https://agile-agilist.com/training/adv-safe/rte/): Certification for facilitating Agile Release Trains, PI Planning, and program-level flow.
- [SAFe® Lean Portfolio Management (LPM)](https://agile-agilist.com/training/adv-safe/lpm/): Lean budgeting, governance, and portfolio strategy for connecting strategy to execution.
- [SAFe® Agile Product Management (APM)](https://agile-agilist.com/training/adv-safe/apm/): Design Thinking and customer-centric product management aligned to Scaled Agile Framework 6.0.

## SAFe Certification Training — Core
- [Leading SAFe® (SAFe Agilist / SA)](https://agile-agilist.com/training/safe/sa/): The foundational SAFe Agilist certification for executives, leaders, and change agents.
- [SAFe® Product Owner / Product Manager (POPM)](https://agile-agilist.com/training/safe/popm/): Role-based certification for product owners and managers in a SAFe enterprise.
- [SAFe® Business Owner (BO)](https://agile-agilist.com/training/safe/bo/): Workshop for business owners accountable for value in an Agile Release Train.
- [SAFe® Foundations Micro-Credential](https://agile-agilist.com/training/safe-found/): Entry-level SAFe foundations credential.
- [All SAFe Training](https://agile-agilist.com/training/): Full catalog of SAFe certification courses and learning paths.
