## One line (under 100 characters)

EnrollClass is the enrollment and payments platform for professional training businesses.

## Short description (Stripe "What does your business do", 2 to 3 sentences)

EnrollClass is a software platform that lets professional training providers (certification
trainers, bootcamps, corporate learning teams) publish course cohorts, take seat registrations,
and collect payment by card or invoice. Learners pay EnrollClass at checkout; funds are held in
a ledger and released to the training provider's connected Stripe account after the course is
delivered. EnrollClass charges the provider a per-transaction platform fee and does not deliver
training itself.

## Long description (website About, investor and partner pages)

EnrollClass is an enrollment and payments platform built for the people who run professional
training: SAFe and Scrum trainers, PMI and cloud certification instructors, bootcamps, and
corporate learning teams. It replaces the patchwork of ticketing tools, invoicing software,
spreadsheets and manual payouts that training businesses use today.

Organizers publish courses and cohorts, embed a checkout on their own website, and sell seats
by card, Apple Pay, Google Pay, or corporate invoice with purchase order. Learners manage their
own seats: substitute a colleague, move to another date, or cancel under the organizer's policy,
without emailing anyone. Attendance is captured from Zoom or Microsoft Teams, certificates are
issued automatically with a public verification code, and follow-up emails run on a schedule.

Underneath is a double-entry ledger. Every payment is split at the moment it lands: the
organizer's share, instructor fees, venue or partner shares, taxes, and the platform fee.
Organizer funds are held until the cohort is delivered, then released automatically, which
protects learners and lets organizers offer guaranteed-to-run and money-back terms with
confidence. Payouts go to the organizer's own Stripe account; EnrollClass never holds funds
outside regulated payment rails.

EnrollClass is operated from Toronto with an EU entity in Lithuania, serves organizers in
Canada, the United States, the United Kingdom, the European Union, South Africa and the Gulf,
and prices in CAD, USD, GBP, EUR, AED, SAR, ZAR and INR.

## Positioning in three sentences

Eventbrite sells tickets; Stripe moves money; neither understands a cohort. EnrollClass is built
around the cohort: minimum enrollment, held funds released on delivery, substitutions and
transfers, instructor and partner splits, attendance and certificates. For a training business
it is the money and operations layer in one product, priced per transaction, with no monthly fee.
