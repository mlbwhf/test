By mid-2026 the frontier is a photo-finish. On the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index (July 2026), OpenAI's GPT-5.6 Sol holds the top composite score at 58.9, with Claude Opus 4.8 (55.7) and GPT-5.6 Terra (55.0) close behind; Google's Gemini 3.1 Pro leads the LMArena text board within a statistical tie of Opus. Since that snapshot, our 3 September review placed two Chinese models inside the global top four — Alibaba's Qwen3.8-Max (58) and Moonshot's Kimi K3 (57.1) — so the US-only leaderboard implied below no longer holds.

But there is no single "best." The honest answer is that the best model depends on the task in front of you — Claude and GPT-5.6 for agentic coding, Gemini for multimodal and 2M-token context, DeepSeek and Qwen for near-frontier reasoning at a fraction of the price. Below we rate every major lab's flagship on a defined nine-point rubric and tag each with what it is actually best for.

Methodology

We score each model 1–5 (rendered as ●●●●○ filled/empty dots) across nine criteria:

  1. Reasoning & Math
  2. Coding
  3. Writing & Language
  4. Multimodal (vision / audio / video)
  5. Long context
  6. Speed & latency
  7. Price / value
  8. Reliability (factual accuracy, low hallucination)
  9. Agentic & tool use

Ratings synthesize named public leaderboards — LMArena / Chatbot Arena, Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index, SWE-bench Verified and SWE-bench Pro, MMLU-Pro, GPQA Diamond, AIME / HMMT, and MMMU-Pro — plus published pricing and context specs, as of July 2026 for the US frontier, and 3 September 2026 for the Chinese models. These scores are an editorial synthesis of public benchmarks, not absolute fact; leaderboards shift weekly and some benchmarks (e.g. SWE-bench Verified) face contamination questions. FAQ

What is the best AI model in 2026?

There is no single winner, and the answer now depends on which month you ask. As of July 2026, GPT-5.6 Sol held the top overall composite (Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index 58.9), with Claude Opus 4.8 and Gemini 3.1 Pro in a statistical tie just behind on LMArena. Our 3 September 2026 review then placed two Chinese models inside the global top four — Alibaba's Qwen3.8-Max (58) and Moonshot's Kimi K3 (57.1). The US-frontier composites on this page are carried forward from July and are pending re-verification, so treat the ordering at the very top as provisional. The best model still depends on the task — see the matrix above.

What is the best AI model for coding?

Claude Opus 4.8 leads SWE-bench Pro (69.2%) and is the reference for agentic coding, with GPT-5.6 Sol essentially tied on agentic coding evals. For value, Claude Sonnet 5 delivers near-Opus coding at roughly 40% lower cost.

What is the cheapest capable AI model?

DeepSeek V4-Pro — an MIT-licensed open-weight model at about $0.87 per million output tokens that still posts MMLU-Pro 87.5 and GPQA Diamond 90.1, near-frontier reasoning for a fraction of the flagship price.
