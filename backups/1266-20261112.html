Analysis

The state of play: one default, three forces redrawing the map

Measured by raw traffic, the AI world looks unipolar. Similarweb counted ChatGPT at 55.7% of global generative-AI chatbot web visits in October 2026, ahead of Gemini at 24.2% and Claude at 11.5% MEDIUM. OpenAI said on 31 August 2026 that ChatGPT has more than 1 billion weekly active users, and Alphabet said on 28 October 2026 that the Gemini app has more than 1 billion monthly users — one weekly and one monthly count, which cannot be compared directly. Anthropic publishes no user count for Claude; third-party estimates range from 56M to 245M CONTESTED. In the US, UK, France and Germany, ChatGPT led StatCounter's October 2026 count of chatbot referral clicks with 37–44%, with Gemini second at 31–36% HIGH. But the national maps tell a different story, because three forces override the default wherever they apply.

Force one: access and regulation. China blocks Western frontier models outright, so its market is a parallel universe: QuestMobile counted 419.08 million monthly active users for ByteDance's Doubao in September 2026, against about 158 million for Alibaba's Qianwen and about 123 million for DeepSeek MEDIUM. Russia's market, where US assistants face access and payment restrictions, is led by domestic and Chinese tools. StatCounter, which counts clicks from AI chatbots through to websites rather than users, put Perplexity at 47.9% of chatbot referrals in Russia in April 2026; it does not track Sber's GigaChat or Yandex's Alice AI HIGH. By monthly reach, Mediascope found 55% of Russians aged 12+ used an AI service in September 2026, led by Yandex's Alice AI (about 48%), DeepSeek (11%) and GigaChat (5%) MEDIUM.

Force two: ecosystem and price. Where the query mix skews toward everyday personal tasks — and OpenAI's own usage study (NBER Working Paper 34255) found non-work messages rose from 53% to 73% of consumer ChatGPT messages between June 2024 and June 2025 — distribution and cost beat benchmark scores. Doubao wins China partly because it lives inside Douyin. Gemini is gaining fastest in price-sensitive markets like India because it bills in rupees over UPI, ships free tiers through AI Studio, and comes pre-installed on Android — the only frontier assistant that meets a $3-ARPU market on its own terms.

Force three: language. Local champions hold the line only where frontier models serve a language poorly or cannot reach users — and Korea shows how fast that edge erodes. In South Korea, ChatGPT's app had 18.76 million monthly users in October 2026 (Mobile Index), about 91% of AI-app users; Naver's HyperCLOVA X reaches users mainly through search, after Naver closed its standalone Clova X chatbot in April 2026, and Naver said in May 2026 that its AI Briefing answers reach about 30 million people a month MEDIUM. Japan's Digital Agency began testing three domestic models on its government AI platform Gennai in July 2026: NTT's tsuzumi 2, Fujitsu's Takane 32B and Preferred Networks' PLaMo 2.0 Prime MEDIUM. In India, the sovereign program backs Sarvam. The pattern across all three forces: the model that wins a country is the one that fits its query mix — frontier English models win research-and-writing queries, ecosystem apps win daily-utility queries, and local models win local-language queries.

Key takeaways
  • ChatGPT is the default, not the universal. ChatGPT had 55.7% of global gen-AI web visits in October 2026 (Similarweb) and more than 1 billion weekly users (OpenAI, 31 Aug 2026) — but it is blocked in China and trails Yandex's Alice AI and DeepSeek in Russia.
  • The query mix picks the winner. As usage shifts personal (73% of consumer ChatGPT messages were non-work by June 2025, per OpenAI's NBER study), ecosystem reach and price matter more than benchmark rank.
  • Growth has moved to Asia. From H1 2025 to Q2 2026, the share of working-age people using AI rose by a relative 56.8% in South Korea, 47.9% in Japan and 47.3% in Thailand, about twice the US rise of 25.5% (DERIVED from Microsoft AI Diffusion data).

Why: the query-type evidence

The "why" behind each map line becomes visible in what people actually ask. OpenAI's own usage research divides prompts into three intents — asking (49%: seeking information and advice), doing (40%: producing output — writing, code, plans), and expressing (11%: companionship, reflection) — and finds non-work use rising from 53% to 73% of consumer messages between June 2024 and June 2025 (Chatterji et al., NBER Working Paper 34255) MEDIUM. That is why Gemini's international footprint (strongest in East Asia) and China's domestic apps are growing faster than the category leader.

The prompt layer: what each country actually asks

Prompt-level data sharpens the picture further. Globally, software development is still the #1 prompt category at 29% of prompts — but falling fast (44% in April 2024) as mainstream users arrive; history & society is second at 15%, and economics, finance & tax is the fastest-growing category, more than tripling its share in a year (Visual Capitalist / prompt-panel data, 2025–26 MEDIUM). Read together: India's paradox resolves — the world's most coding-dominant prompt mix (45.2%) sits in its most price-sensitive major market, which is why the fight there is between ChatGPT's developer mindshare and Gemini/DeepSeek's free-tier economics rather than between consumer brands. And the global drift — coding's share falling from 44% to 29% while finance and everyday categories surge — is the mainstreaming wave that pushes every market toward the ecosystem-and-price dynamics described above.

Three deep dives

China's parallel market. QuestMobile counted 546 million monthly users of AI-native apps in China in September 2026, up 82.1% year on year MEDIUM. Doubao leads on distribution, not benchmarks; Qianwen's 58-fold surge shows Alibaba converting commerce reach into AI usage; and DeepSeek is the paradox — its consumer app is shrinking even as its open-weight models spread worldwide as infrastructure. Full country picture: AI in China 2026.

India, the swing state. ChatGPT's largest market by weekly users (OpenAI India, 3 Nov 2026) is also the market where Gemini's economics bite hardest — native rupee billing over UPI, free Flash tiers, Android defaults. Whichever model wins India's price-sensitive, multilingual, mobile-first query mix will bank the largest user gain of the decade; the IndiaAI Mission's Sarvam adds a sovereign wildcard.

Korea, proof that language quality drives adoption. Korea had the largest gain of any major economy in Microsoft's AI diffusion data for three consecutive editions, rising from 25.9% of the working-age population in H1 2025 to 40.6% in June 2026 (12th worldwide) HIGH. Microsoft attributes the surge to national AI policy, a jump in frontier models' Korean-language ability (GPT-4o and GPT-5 on the Korean college entrance exam) and the April 2025 viral image-generation moment. The main beneficiary was ChatGPT, not a local model: ChatGPT's app had 18.76 million monthly users in October 2026 (Mobile Index), about 91% of AI-app users MEDIUM. Naver's HyperCLOVA X reaches users mainly through search, after Naver closed its standalone Clova X chatbot in April 2026, so app trackers do not measure it. The lesson for every sovereign-AI program (tracked in our Sovereign AI Index): a local model's language edge lasts only until frontier models close the gap.

FAQ

What is the most popular AI model in the world in 2026?

ChatGPT. Similarweb counted ChatGPT at 55.7% of global generative-AI web visits in October 2026, ahead of Gemini (24.2%) and Claude (11.5%), and OpenAI said on 31 August 2026 that ChatGPT has more than 1 billion weekly active users. Alphabet said on 28 October 2026 that the Gemini app has more than 1 billion monthly users, a monthly count that is not comparable with ChatGPT's weekly one. ChatGPT is blocked in China, and in Russia Yandex's Alice AI and DeepSeek reach more people.

What is the most popular AI model in China?

ByteDance's Doubao. QuestMobile counted 419.08 million monthly active users for Doubao in September 2026, ahead of Alibaba's Qianwen (about 158 million) and DeepSeek (about 123 million). Western models are blocked.

Which AI do Russians use most?

Yandex's Alice AI. By monthly reach, Mediascope found 55% of Russians aged 12+ used an AI service in September 2026, led by Yandex's Alice AI (about 48%), DeepSeek (11%, about 13 million people) and Sber's GigaChat (5%, about 6 million). StatCounter, which counts clicks from AI chatbots through to websites rather than users, put Perplexity at 47.9% of chatbot referrals in Russia in April 2026; it does not track Sber's GigaChat or Yandex's Alice AI. An earlier version of this page presented that referral share as Perplexity's share of AI use.

Which countries prefer local AI models over ChatGPT?

China (by policy) and Russia (by access: Yandex's Alice AI, DeepSeek and Sber's GigaChat lead where US assistants face restrictions), with Japan, India and the Gulf running sovereign models in public-sector niches. Korea is no longer on the list: ChatGPT's app had 18.76 million monthly users there in October 2026 (Mobile Index), about 91% of AI-app users.

What do people prompt AI most, and does it differ by country?

Globally, coding is still the top prompt category (29%, down from 44% as mainstream use grows), with history/society second and finance the fastest-growing. By country the differences are stark: 45.2% of India's AI use is software tasks (#1 worldwide), while high-adoption countries like the US and Singapore spread prompts across education, science, business and personal life.

Which AI model is growing fastest internationally?

Gemini has the most internationally distributed user base with particular strength in East Asia, powered by Android distribution and aggressive pricing; by Report AI's calculation from Microsoft's AI Diffusion data (DERIVED), the fastest relative rises in AI user share from H1 2025 to Q2 2026 were South Korea (+56.8%), Japan (+47.9%) and Thailand (+47.3%).
