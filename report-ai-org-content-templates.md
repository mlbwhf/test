# report-ai.org — Content Templates & Seed Drafts

Ready-to-publish scaffolding. **Numbers are intentionally left as
`[VALUE — source, date]` placeholders** — a stats site only has value if every
figure is real and cited, so these get filled from primary sources (I can
research and populate them on request). Templates below keep every post
consistent and sourced.

---

## Editorial rules (apply to every post)
1. **One claim, one source.** Every statistic links to a primary source + date.
2. **Date everything.** "As of [month year]" and a visible *Last updated*.
3. **Link inward.** News/blog posts link to the relevant Statistics and pillar pages.
4. **No orphan numbers.** A stat without a source link does not get published.

## B. Pillar page template — "State of Enterprise AI 2026"

**Intro (2–3 paragraphs):** what the page covers, who it's for, when last updated.

**Sections (each = a cluster of sourced stats):**
- Adoption: *"[VALUE]% of enterprises have deployed AI in at least one function [source, date]."*
- Generative AI use: *"[VALUE]% report using generative AI regularly [source, date]."*
- ROI & outcomes: *"[VALUE]% report measurable ROI from AI [source, date]."*
- Spend & budgets: *"Average enterprise AI budget grew [VALUE]% YoY [source, date]."*
- Barriers: *"Top blockers: [list] [source, date]."*

**Closing:** internal links to related stat posts + the Assessment CTA.

## C. Statistic post template

**Title:** `[The stat as a headline]` — e.g., "X% of Enterprises Use Generative AI in 2026"
**Body:**
- **The number:** `[VALUE]` with one sentence of context.
- **Source:** `[Organization, report name, date]` → outbound link.
- **Why it matters:** 2–3 sentences.
- **Related:** internal links to pillar + similar stats.
**Taxonomies:** Topic, Industry, Region, Year, Source, Format=Statistic.

---

## D. News post template

**Title:** clear, factual headline.
**Body:** what happened · why it matters · the relevant numbers (linked to the
Statistics Library) · what to watch next.
**Cadence:** weekly roundup + breaking items. Always link to evergreen stats.

---

## E. Blog/explainer template

**Title:** question or how-to framing (e.g., "How to Measure AI ROI").
**Body:** intro · the concept explained · supporting stats (linked) · takeaways.
Evergreen, SEO-targeted at informational queries; funnels readers to stats + assessment.
