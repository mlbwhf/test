# Brew-n-Chill Robo-Barista — Economic & ROI Analysis (2026)

_Model for a single Robo-Barista serving **coffee + ice cream**. Machine price: **$75,000** (before shipping & handling). Figures are directional, from 2026 industry benchmarks; actual results depend on location, hours and pricing._

## 1. Headline

| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| Machine (full unit) | **$75,000** before shipping & handling |
| Gross margin (coffee + soft-serve) | **70–85%** |
| Ingredients (COGS) | ~20–25% of revenue |
| Net profit / machine / month | **~$3,500 (moderate) → $12,000+ (high-traffic)** |
| Payback on $75K | **~5–18 months** by location |
| Reported owner ROI (annual) | **80–300%** in good locations |

## 2. Unit assumptions
- Avg coffee price **$4.50**; avg ice cream **$4.00**.
- Gross margin **~78%** (coffee 70–85%; soft-serve 70–80%, base mix $0.15–0.50/serve).
- Operating deductions: ingredients (in COGS), location fee ~10–15% of revenue, power $1–2/day, cleaning ~$30/mo, restock ~1 hr/day (owner or service plan).
- ~30 trading days/month.

## 3. ROI scenarios

| Scenario | Coffee + ice cream/day | Daily rev | Monthly rev | Est. net/mo | Payback ($75K) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Moderate** — quiet office | ~55 cups + 15 serves | ~$308 | ~$9,200 | ~$3,500–$4,500 | ~16–18 mo |
| **Strong** — gym, hotel, clinic | ~90 + 35 | ~$545 | ~$16,400 | ~$7,000–$9,000 | ~9–11 mo |
| **High-traffic** — mall, campus, airport | ~140 + 70 | ~$910 | ~$27,300 | ~$12,000–$15,000 | ~5–6 mo |

## 4. Why a Robo-Barista beats a staffed café

| | Staffed coffee shop | Robo-Barista |
|---|---|---|
| Upfront | $150K–$200K (build-out $80K–$150K) | **$75K** + S&H |
| Staffing | 2–3 baristas + manager (~34% of revenue, ~$280K/yr) | **$0** |
| Rent | Storefront ~$42K/yr | ~2 m², existing space |
| Gross margin | ~60–70% before labour | **70–85%** |
| Serves | Coffee | **Coffee + ice cream** |
| Payback | 2–3 years (if it survives) | **5–18 months** |

The single biggest swing is **labour**: a staffed café's ~$280K/yr payroll is eliminated entirely, and ice cream adds a second high-margin revenue stream from the same footprint.

## 5. Levers that change the outcome
- **Location** is the #1 driver — high-traffic venues outperform 2–3×.
- **Dual menu**: pushing ice cream in the afternoon/evening lifts revenue without extra footprint.
- **Pricing**: each +$0.50 on ~100 serves/day ≈ +$1,500/mo.
- **Uptime**: connected monitoring + a maintenance contract protect revenue.
