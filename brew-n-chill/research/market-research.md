# Brew-n-Chill — Market Research

_Compiled June 2026. Figures are from public market reports and industry sources; treat ranges as directional, not guarantees._

## 1. The opportunity

Brew-n-Chill sits at the intersection of two growing markets:

| Market | 2024/25 value | Forecast | CAGR |
|---|---|---|---|
| Robot coffee kiosk | ~$20.3B (2024) | ~$51.1B by 2032 | 12.3% |
| Smart vending (F&B-led) | ~$26B (2024) | growing through 2030 | >12% |
| Office coffee service | $6.8B (2025) | $11.4B by 2034 | 5.9% |

Tailwinds:

- **Return to office.** Major-market office occupancy recovered to ~62% in early 2026 (from ~35% in 2021), reviving demand for managed beverage amenities.
- **Labour pressure.** Automated kiosks eliminate barista payroll entirely and run 24/7 with no shift changes — attractive when staffing is hard and expensive.
- **Unattended retail shift.** The market is moving from fixed storefronts toward mobile, 24/7 micro-retail; a full coffee offer now fits in ~2 m².

## 2. The technology

Fully automated / robotic coffee kiosks:

- Make espresso-based and iced drinks in **under 60 seconds**, up to **~60 drinks/hour**.
- Footprint as small as **~2 m²** (robotic units ~3.8 m²).
- Touchscreen + mobile/app ordering, cashless payment, remote telemetry, self-cleaning.
- Restocking ≈ **1 hour/day**; ingredients ≈ **20–25% of revenue**.

**Notable hardware suppliers** (the pool Brew-n-Chill can curate from as a reseller): Cafe X, COFE+ / Hi-Dolphin, Yummy Future, RobotAnno (Anno), Robojo, Leader Automation, VendingLab (CaféXbot), intelliCAFE, Touch Coffee, Crown Coffee, Orion Star, Rozum Robotics.

> **Positioning implication:** because many vendors exist, Brew-n-Chill's edge is being *technology-agnostic* — curating and standing behind the best machine for each site, plus owning the coffee quality — rather than being a single-box dealer.

## 3. Unit economics (single machine, illustrative)

| Line | Value |
|---|---|
| Volume | 60 cups/day |
| Avg price | $4.00 |
| Daily revenue | $240 |
| Monthly revenue | ~$7,200 |
| Ingredient cost | ~$0.80/cup (20–25%) |
| Gross profit | ~$5,760/month |
| **Net profit** | **~$3,000–$4,000/month** after location fee, power, restocking |
| Payback | 12–18 months (6–12 in prime sites) |

Hardware price bands:

- Entry / compact: **$2,000–$7,500**
- Mid-range professional: **$10,000–$25,000**
- Premium robotic: **$28,000–$60,000+**

**Location is the dominant variable.** A machine doing ~55 cups/day in a quiet office can do 95+ on a busy campus — a 40–60% swing in monthly net profit. Poor placement is the #1 cause of underperformance.

## 4. B2B demand drivers (primary audience)

- **Retention:** replacing an employee costs **50–200% of salary**; quality F&B ranks top-3 among retention perks (HBR). One retained hire can outweigh the whole program cost.
- **Engagement:** **89%** of workers say high-quality coffee makes them more engaged; **81%** say coffee breaks build colleague relationships.
- **Amenity arms race:** post-2024 RTO has companies investing in better furniture, food and coffee to make offices worth commuting to.

## 5. Best verticals (priority order for Brew-n-Chill)

1. **Offices & coworking** — flagship; retention story + captive audience.
2. **Gyms & studios** — pre/post-workout drinks, added revenue line.
3. **Hotels & serviced apartments** — 24/7 lobby coffee, no labour.
4. **Hospitals & healthcare** — night staff + visitors, café often shut.
5. **Universities & campuses** — high footfall, long hours, best cup volumes.
6. **Retail / transit / convenience** — malls, stations, forecourts; micro-retail.
