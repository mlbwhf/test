# Brew-n-Chill — Brand & Positioning

## Positioning statement

> **Brew-n-Chill** brings barista-grade coffee to the places people work, train, heal, study and pass through — as a turnkey, self-running kiosk. We are a **coffee technology partner** (we supply, install and service the best automated kiosks) **and a coffee brand** (our beans and blends are tuned to pour like a café). Hardware that performs; coffee that delivers.

## Dual model (and how the site reflects it)

| Role | What it means | Primary audience | Site emphasis |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Technology reseller** (PRIMARY) | Source, install, service automated coffee kiosks; flexible buy/lease/revenue-share | Businesses & site owners (B2B) | Home, How It Works, Machines, Industries, Pricing & ROI |
| **Coffee brand** (SECONDARY) | Own beans/blends/recipes dialled in for the machine; consumer-facing quality | End drinkers + proof of quality for B2B | Our Coffee page; brand wrap on every kiosk |

Decision (from client): **B2B leads, B2C supports.** The homepage sells the kiosk-as-amenity/asset; "Our Coffee" makes the quality credible.

## Audiences

1. **Employers / office & facilities managers** — want a retention-grade perk with zero labour.
2. **Operators / investors** — want a machine that nets $3–4k/month with 12–18 month payback.
3. **Venue owners** (gyms, hotels, hospitals, campuses, retail) — want 24/7 service and a new revenue line.
4. **End drinkers** — want genuinely good coffee, fast, any time.

## The name as the menu

- **Brew** = the hot line (espresso, latte, cappuccino, flat white, americano, hot chocolate).
- **Chill** = the cold line (iced lattes, cold brew, refreshers).
- One kiosk, both moods, all day. Lean on this in copy and signage.

## Voice & tone

- **Confident, plain-spoken, a little warm.** No buzzword soup.
- **Honest about numbers.** We'd rather say "this location won't work" than oversell.
- **Quality over novelty.** The robot is fun; the coffee is the point. (Recurring line.)

## Taglines (pick one primary)

1. **"Barista-grade coffee. Zero staffing. 24/7."** (primary — clearest B2B promise)
2. "Coffee as a technology. Run as a brand."
3. "Brew or chill. Always on."
4. "A café in two square metres."

## Visual direction

- **Palette:** warm espresso browns + cream, with a cool teal/mint accent to carry the "chill" half.
  - Espresso `#3A2A1E` · Roast `#7A4A25` · Crema/cream `#F6EFE6` · Chill teal `#2FA89B` · Caramel `#C8A06A` · Ink `#1C1A17`
- **Type:** a friendly geometric sans for UI (e.g. Inter / Plus Jakarta Sans) + a characterful display face for headlines (e.g. Fraunces) — both free on Google Fonts.
- **Imagery:** real cups, steam, the machine in situ; the robotic arm as hero where the premium kiosk is featured. Avoid generic stock baristas — we don't have baristas, that's the point.
- **Logo idea:** wordmark "Brew-n-Chill" with the "-n-" styled as a small coffee-bean or droplet; hot/cold split in the two color families.

## Messaging pillars (reuse across pages & ads)

1. **Effortless** — install in an afternoon, runs itself, we service it.
2. **Profitable / worth it** — clear ROI for operators; retention ROI for employers.
3. **Always on** — 24/7, no queue, no closing time.
4. **Actually good** — specialty beans tuned for the machine; consistent every cup.

## Proof points to keep handy

- Drinks in <60s, up to 60/hour, ~2 m² footprint.
- ~$240/day revenue potential; ~$3–4k/month net; 12–18 month payback.
- 89% of workers more engaged with quality coffee; replacing an employee costs 50–200% of salary.
- Robot coffee kiosk market ~$20B → ~$51B by 2032.
