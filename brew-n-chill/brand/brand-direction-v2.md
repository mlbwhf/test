## 3. NEW: the machine serves ICE CREAM too

This unlocks the real meaning of the name and the logo (cone + cup):
- **BREW** = the hot/iced coffee line (espresso, latte, cappuccino, flat white, americano, cold brew).
- **CHILL** = **ice cream, soft-serve and frozen treats** (plus iced refreshers).
- One machine, two profit centres: **coffee + dessert**, served 24/7 with no staff.
- Widens the vertical story: malls, cinemas, family entertainment, gyms (protein soft-serve), hotels, campuses.
- Copy hook: _"Brew a coffee. Chill with ice cream. One robot, both cravings."_
