Frequently asked questions

How much space does a kiosk need?

Most units fit in about 1–2 square metres — roughly a vending-machine footprint. Compact models sit on a countertop; robotic kiosks need up to ~4 m².

What does it need to run?

A standard power outlet and either a plumbed water line or a refillable tank. We confirm everything in the site survey.

Who maintains and restocks it?

We do, or we train your team. Connected machines alert us to low stock and service needs automatically; day-to-day restocking is about an hour.

How do customers pay?

Tap, chip, mobile wallet, and optional QR / app ordering. You can also set drinks to free (employer-subsidised) or any price you like.

Is the coffee actually good?

Yes — that's the whole point. We use specialty-grade Brew-n-Chill beans tuned for the machine, so the quality is café-level and identical cup after cup.

Buy, lease or revenue-share?

All three. Buying is cheapest long-term, leasing keeps upfront cost low, and revenue-share suits high-traffic sites where we place the machine and split takings. See Pricing & ROI.

How fast can we go live?

Once the machine and location are confirmed, install and first cup usually happen in a single visit. Book a demo and we'll map out the timeline.
