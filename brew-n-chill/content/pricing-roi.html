Pricing & ROI

Three ways to get a kiosk, and honest numbers behind all of them.

Three ways to work with us

  • You own the asset

Lease

  • Little/no upfront cost
  • Aligned incentives

What the machines cost

Automated coffee hardware spans a wide range depending on throughput and features:

  • Entry / compact: roughly $2,000–$7,500
  • Mid-range professional: roughly $10,000–$25,000
  • Premium robotic kiosk: roughly $28,000–$60,000+

Indicative market ranges — your exact quote depends on the model, contract type and supply agreement. Ask us for a tailored number.

A worked example (single office machine)

  • 60 cups/day × $4 average price = $240/day revenue
  • Ingredients ~$0.80/cup (20–25% of revenue)
  • ≈ $5,760/month gross profit before location costs
  • ≈ $3,000–$4,000/month net per machine after location fee, power and restocking
  • Typical payback: 12–18 months (as fast as 6–12 in prime spots)

Location is everything. The same machine that does 55 cups/day in a quiet office can do 95+ on a busy campus — a 40–60% swing in monthly profit. We help you place it right.
