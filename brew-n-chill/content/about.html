About Brew-n-Chill

We make great coffee effortless for the places people work, train, heal, study and pass through.

Why we exist

Everyone wants good coffee on site. Almost nobody wants to hire baristas, manage a café, or babysit a temperamental machine. Brew-n-Chill closes that gap: we bring the technology, the beans and the service, and you get a self-running coffee bar that just works.

Technology partner and coffee brand

Most companies in this space sell you a box. We do two jobs at once. As a technology reseller we pick the best automated kiosks on the market, install them and keep them running. As a coffee brand we make sure what comes out of them is genuinely worth drinking. Hardware that performs, coffee that delivers — under one roof.

How we work

  • Honest economics. We model the numbers before you commit, and we'll tell you if a location won't work.
  • Flexible terms. Buy, lease or revenue-share — whatever fits your risk appetite.
  • Genuinely hands-off. We monitor, restock and service so you don't think about it.
  • Quality, not novelty. The robot is fun; the coffee is the point.
