Industries we serve

Captive audiences and long hours are where automated coffee shines. These are the spaces we know best.

Offices & coworking

The flagship use case. Quality coffee ranks among the top three workplace perks for retention, and with replacement costs at 50–200% of salary, a kiosk that keeps one good hire is already paid for. No more cheap drip pot — a real café in the breakroom, with no office manager playing barista.

Gyms & fitness studios

Pre-workout espresso, post-workout protein lattes and cold brew — an extra revenue line and a membership perk that runs itself while your team focuses on the floor.

Hotels & serviced apartments

24/7 lobby coffee the front desk never has to make. Guests get specialty drinks at any hour; you get an amenity with no labour and a new ancillary revenue stream.

Hospitals & healthcare

Staff on night shifts and visitors in waiting rooms need coffee when the café is shut. Unattended kiosks deliver consistent quality around the clock in a tiny footprint.

Universities & campuses

High footfall, long hours and a captive student audience. Moving a machine from a moderate-traffic office (~55 cups/day) to a busy campus (~95+ cups/day) can lift monthly net profit by 40–60%.

Retail, transit & convenience

Malls, stations, forecourts and lobbies. A full coffee offer in two square metres turns dead corners into 24/7 micro-retail.

Don't see your space? If people walk past it and want coffee, we can probably make the numbers work.
