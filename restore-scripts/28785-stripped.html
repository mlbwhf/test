Agile Maturity Assessment

Where is your organization on the journey from Absent to Flying? Rate four dimensions across a five-stage model — and see what to build next.

Four dimensions · Five stages · No login

The maturity model rates four dimensions of enterprise agility. Read each dimension below and locate where your organization sits today, from Absent to Flying.

( 01 )

Operating Model & Alignment

Vision, sponsorship, and how leadership shows up.

1 · Absent
  • No conversations
  • Lack of vision & leadership sponsorship
2 · Nascent
  • Exploratory discussions
  • Named sponsorship
3 · Designed
  • Rules of engagement
  • Challenges identified
  • Active leadership championship
4 · Accelerating
  • Peer (leadership) partnerships
  • Key wins
  • Shared accountability
5 · Flying
  • Strategic planning
  • Leaders filling in for each other
  • Leaders as coaches
( 02 )

Value Delivery Engagement

Awareness of agile and value delivery across teams.

1 · Absent
  • Lack of awareness
  • No movement
  • Ad hoc communication & collaboration
2 · Nascent
  • Plans and discussions
  • Agile-compliance mindset
3 · Designed
  • Strong awareness & understanding of agile and value delivery across functional teams
4 · Accelerating
  • User-driven activities
  • Peer coaching
  • Championing mindset
5 · Flying
  • A way of life
  • Master coaching
( 03 )

Workforce Enablement

Training, tools, and knowledge for the people doing the work.

1 · Absent
  • No tools
  • Training non-existent
  • Work in silos
2 · Nascent
  • Basic training in progress
  • Inconsistent use of tools
3 · Designed
  • Champions, coaches & training rolled out
  • Consistently utilized tools
4 · Accelerating
  • Seamless onboarding
  • Easy access to knowledge management & tool assistance
5 · Flying
  • Self-sufficient teams training each other
  • Communities of practice in place
( 04 )

Ways of Working

Roles, autonomy, collaboration, and how you improve.

1 · Absent
  • Siloed, unaligned or sedimented into waterfall methods
2 · Nascent
  • Limited to one area
  • Agile roles being discussed, inconsistent use, limited autonomy
  • Focus on cooperation
3 · Designed
  • Process to redefine ways of working in place
  • Agile roles documented, people transitioning in
  • Collaboration by design
4 · Accelerating
  • Core areas covered
  • Agile roles are championed
  • Teams collaborate & operate autonomously
5 · Flying
  • Constant reinvention & adjustment
  • Agile roles help each other
  • Continuous organizational learning
