<h2 class="wp-block-heading" style="color:#0F172A;font-size:32px;font-weight:800;letter-spacing:-0.02em">The SAFe® Implementation Roadmap, in Four Moves</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"fontSize":"17px","lineHeight":"1.7"},"color":{"text":"#334155"},"spacing":{"margin":{"bottom":"28px"}}}} -->
<p style="color:#334155;font-size:17px;line-height:1.7;margin-bottom:28px">Implementing SAFe is a sequence, not an event. Each step has explicit entry conditions and a measurable outcome before the next begins.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list {"ordered":true,"style":{"typography":{"fontSize":"16px","lineHeight":"1.8"},"color":{"text":"#334155"},"spacing":{"padding":{"left":"24px"}}}} -->
<ol class="wp-block-list" style="color:#334155;font-size:16px;line-height:1.8;padding-left:24px">
<li><strong>Reach the Tipping Point — establish the need for change.</strong> Formulate a compelling vision for the transformation, secure executive sponsorship, and identify the key business drivers (accelerating time-to-market, improving quality, optimizing flow metrics).</li>
<li><strong>Train Lean-Agile Change Agents — form the guiding coalition.</strong> Certify internal change agents as SAFe® Practice Consultants (SPCs) and establish a Lean-Agile Center of Excellence (LACE) to govern, optimize, and sustain transformation practices across organizational boundaries.</li>
<li><strong>Identify Value Streams — map the operational flow.</strong> Analyze operational value streams to see how customer value flows through the enterprise, then design and launch your initial Agile Release Train (ART) of 50–125 cross-functional professionals.</li>
<li><strong>Execute &amp; Accelerate — launch and continuously improve.</strong> Facilitate the first Planning Interval (PI), transition to dynamic Lean Portfolio Management (LPM) budgeting, and establish a continuous-learning culture to accelerate business agility.</li>
</ol>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading" style="color:#0F172A;font-size:1.125rem;font-weight:600">What is the format and passing score of the SAFe Practice Consultant (SPC) exam?</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"fontSize":"1rem","lineHeight":"1.75"},"color":{"text":"#374151"}}} -->
<p style="color:#374151;font-size:1rem;line-height:1.75">The SPC examination is a web-based, closed-book, 120-minute timed test of 60 multiple-choice and multiple-select questions. Candidates must achieve an 83% passing score — 50 of 60 correct — to unlock the official credential.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3,"style":{"typography":{"fontSize":"1.125rem","fontWeight":"600"},"color":{"text":"#0F172A"}}} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading" style="color:#0F172A;font-size:1.125rem;font-weight:600">Why is internal SPC training critical for corporate Agile transformations?</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph {"style":{"typography":{"fontSize":"1rem","lineHeight":"1.75"},"color":{"text":"#374151"}}} -->
<p style="color:#374151;font-size:1rem;line-height:1.75">External consulting interventions rarely stick long-term without an internal guiding coalition. Training internal change agents ensures that custom value streams, DevOps pipelines, and continuous-delivery methods are sustained by leaders embedded permanently within the corporate structure — not lost when a consultant rolls off.</p>
