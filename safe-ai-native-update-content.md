## The model (as I understand it — confirm)
**Five SAFe Disciplines** (the framework structure):
1. **Lean Portfolio Management** — strategy, Lean budgets, portfolio flow & governance.
2. **Product Development Flow** — building the right things, fast, with flow.
3. **Team & Technical Agility** — high-performing teams + technical/engineering excellence.
4. **Large Solution Integration & Delivery** — coordinating big, multi-team solutions.
5. **Leadership & Culture** — Lean-Agile leadership and the culture that sustains it.

**AI-Empowered Agility** (how AI is applied — 4 shifts + 1 culture = the "5 dimensions"):
1. **Outcomes & Intent** — steer by outcomes, let AI handle more of the "how."
2. **Iterative Learning & Rapid Experimentation** — shorten learning loops with AI.
3. **Development & Innovation at Scale** — AI amplifies build/innovation capacity.
4. **Cross-Functional, AI-Augmented Teams** — humans + AI working as one team.
5. **Human-Centric AI Culture** — AI augments people; humans stay accountable (the sustaining dimension).

Plus **AI-Native** — Scaled Agile's new **standalone** AI training/certification (no prior SAFe required).
