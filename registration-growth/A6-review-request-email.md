# A6 — Post-class review request (draft for Mark)
_Send 2–3 days after class ends, while the exam-prep glow is still on. One email, two asks, in this order: Google review (feeds GBP + future aggregateRating), then a publishable testimonial with explicit permission. Log every response in `A6-review-tracking-sheet.csv` — that sheet is what makes the Phase B aggregateRating schema legitimate and auditable._

**Send To:** each attendee (per class roster) · **From:** Mark's real address
**Subject:** `How was your {{course_name}} class? (2 minutes, big favour)`

---

Hi {{first_name}},

Congratulations on completing **{{course_name}}** — it was a pleasure having you in the cohort. Good luck on the exam; you're well prepared.

One small favour, two ways to help (either or both, ~2 minutes):

**1. A Google review.** Honest words from real students are how the next {{first_name}} finds us:
👉 [Leave a Google review]({{gbp_review_link}})

**2. A sentence for our course page.** Reply to this email with one or two sentences about the class. If you're happy for us to publish it with your **name, role, and company**, just say "OK to publish" — we never publish without that.

That's it. Thank you — and when you pass the exam, tell me; I like hearing it.

Mark
Agile Agilist · SAFe® Gold Partner

---

## Mechanics
- `{{gbp_review_link}}`: the direct GBP "write a review" short link (Business Profile → Ask for reviews).
- Every reply gets logged in the tracking sheet same day, including permission status.
- Phase B3 gate: aggregateRating schema returns ONLY once the sheet holds enough published-with-permission reviews to back the numbers on the visible page.
