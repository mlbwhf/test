## 1. Positioning: turn "vendor-neutral" into the funding hook

Public funders (governments, workforce boards, universities) are often **barred
from or wary of** endorsing a single tech vendor. That makes your neutrality a
direct advantage they cannot get from Microsoft/Google/AWS-branded credentials.

**Core messages to lead every pitch with:**

- **"Vendor-neutral & technology-agnostic"** — we teach AI principles, ethics, and
  applied skills that transfer across *any* tool, so learners aren't locked in.
- **"Industry-recognized credential"** — the exact phrase grant programs require
  (see US ETPL rules below). Make sure your cert is described this way everywhere.
- **"Stackable & short-cycle"** — most funding favors 4–16 week programs that lead
  to a credential and a job outcome. Frame your cert to fit that window.
- **"Trainer-of-trainers ready"** — governments running mass-upskilling (Saudi,
  UAE) need neutral curricula they can scale; offer a licensable/white-label track.

**Three revenue/funding models to pursue in parallel:**

| Model | What it means | Best-fit region |
|---|---|---|
| **A. Grant-funded delivery** | A public program pays (50–100%) of learner tuition; you deliver. | US (DOL/WIOA, NSF), Canada (Upskill Canada) |
| **B. Curriculum licensing / white-label** | A government or university licenses your neutral curriculum to run at scale. | Middle East (SDAIA/MBZUAI/Digital Dubai) |
| **C. Employer / co-funded cohorts** | Employers + a matching fund split the cost of upskilling their staff. | All three; strong in Canada (up to 70% covered) |

## 3. CANADA — target funders & partners

Canada is currently the **easiest grant-funded delivery** market for you: active
programs, clear application routes, and up to ~70% cost coverage.

### 3.1 Upskill Canada (powered by Palette Skills) — **top priority**
- **What:** Federally seeded national upskilling initiative; funds partners to run
  short-cycle (4–16 week) programs combining technical + business + interpersonal
  skills in priority sectors (incl. **digital technology / AI**). Partners can
  access **up to 70% of total eligible costs.**
- **Why you fit:** Vendor-neutral, credential-bearing, short-cycle AI training is
  squarely in scope; they have already funded AI upskilling (e.g., RBC-backed
  ethical/sector-specific AI training, U of T Data Sciences certs).
- **How to contact:** Apply as a delivery partner via Palette Skills →
  https://paletteskills.org/upskill-canada — use the "Partner with us / Become a
  partner" route.
- **First action:** Submit partner expression of interest positioning your cert as
  a ready-to-run, vendor-neutral AI credential for SMEs.

### 3.2 Scale AI (Canada's Global Innovation Cluster for AI)
- **What:** Funds AI adoption + workforce/training projects; deployed **$128.5M
  across 44 projects** in its largest 2025 round.
- **Why you fit:** They explicitly fund AI training/education to bridge the skills
  gap. A neutral curriculum that any member company can adopt is attractive.
- **How to contact:** https://www.scaleai.ca → "Funding" / "Submit a project."
  Partner with a member employer to strengthen the application.

### 3.3 Mitacs (ADOPT / AI+X)
- **What:** **$200M** in AI investment; trains researchers and helps ~1,500
  companies adopt AI. Programs include Mitacs ADOPT and AI+X.
- **Why you fit:** Pair your cert with a company's AI-adoption project as the
  training component.
- **How to contact:** https://www.mitacs.ca and https://innovation.mitacs.ca/en/artificial-intelligence
  → contact a regional Business Development rep (listed on site).

### 3.4 Future Skills Centre (FSC)
- **What:** Government of Canada Future Skills Program; funds future-of-work and AI
  skills projects/research.
- **How to contact:** https://fsc-ccf.ca → "Funding opportunities" (watch for open
  calls; submit when a relevant call is live).

### 3.5 Provincial layer (stackable)
- **Ontario Skills Development Fund**, and equivalents in BC, Alberta, Quebec.
  Apply provincially in parallel with federal — funds can stack on the same cohort.

**Canada sequencing:** Upskill Canada (partner EOI) → Scale AI (with an employer
partner) → Mitacs (with an adoption project) → provincial top-ups.

## 4. UNITED STATES — target funders & partners

US money is larger but more competitive and compliance-heavy. The unlock is the
phrase **"industry-recognized credential"** + getting on state provider lists.

### 4.1 Get on the **Eligible Training Provider List (ETPL)** — foundational
- **What:** Under WIOA, learners use public funds **only** at ETPL-listed
  providers. Listing = a steady, government-funded learner pipeline.
- **Requirements:** Program must (a) lead to an **industry-recognized credential**
  (your cert qualifies if described that way), and (b) map to an in-demand
  occupation on the **state Target Occupations List.**
- **How:** Apply through **each state's** workforce board portal (e.g., CalJOBS in
  CA, Texas Workforce Commission, WA Workforce Board). Start with the 2–3 states
  where you have the most learners.
- **Finder / proof of demand:** https://www.careeronestop.org/LocalHelp/EmploymentAndTraining/find-WIOA-training-programs.aspx
- **First action:** Register with your top state's workforce board and submit the
  cert for ETPL review.

### 4.2 NSF — TechAccess: AI-Ready America
- **What:** New NSF national initiative; **up to $224M** to establish up to **56
  State/Territory AI Coordination Hubs.** DOL is connecting these hubs to American
  Job Centers, apprenticeships, and its AI Literacy Framework / AI Workforce Hub.
- **Why you fit:** Hubs need neutral training content and delivery partners.
- **How to contact:** Read the solicitation and find the cognizant Program Officer
  (named in the doc) → https://www.nsf.gov/funding/initiatives/ai-ready and
  https://www.nsf.gov/focus-areas/ai/workforce
- **First action:** Email the program officer; ask how training providers plug into
  Coordination Hubs. Consider partnering with a university lead applicant.

### 4.3 NSF ExLENT (Experiential Learning in Emerging & Novel Technologies)
- **What:** Funds educator-led teams for hands-on AI/biotech/quantum learning —
  **~$38M to 46 teams** in the 2025 cycle.
- **How to contact:** Watch for the next solicitation on https://www.nsf.gov ;
  join as a partner on an educator-led team. (Micron Foundation co-invests.)

### 4.4 DOL — Industry-Driven Skills Training Fund
- **What:** **Up to $30M** to address critical workforce needs; **AI infrastructure
  is a named priority industry.**
- **How to contact:** Monitor https://www.dol.gov/agencies/eta and Grants.gov for
  the open NOFO; typically applied for with an industry/employer consortium.

**US sequencing:** ETPL listing (pipeline) → join an NSF TechAccess Hub / ExLENT
team (as partner, lower lift than lead) → pursue DOL NOFO with an employer
consortium when open.

## 5. MIDDLE EAST (GCC) — target partners & buyers

The Gulf is less about *grants to you* and more about **licensing / white-label /
delivery contracts** against enormous national upskilling targets. **2026 is Saudi
Arabia's official "Year of AI."** Your neutrality matters: national programs don't
want to be seen as a single US vendor's channel.

### 5.1 Saudi Arabia — SDAIA & the SAMAI program — **top priority**
- **What:** SDAIA leads a national push to train **1,000,000 Saudis** in AI; **SAMAI
  trained 1.1M+ in 2025** and SAMAI 2 (2026) runs with **11 ministries.** MCIT is
  certifying 100,000 citizens with partners.
- **Why you fit:** Mass-scale programs need neutral, white-labelable curriculum and
  trainer-of-trainers capacity. Position as a licensable accredited curriculum.
- **How to contact:**
  - SDAIA → https://sdaia.gov.sa/en → "Partnerships" / contact form.
  - **MCIT** and **HRDF/Hadaf** (Human Resources Development Fund) for funded
    delivery.
  - Route via **Misk Foundation** for youth programs.
- **First action:** Submit a partnership proposal to SDAIA offering an accredited,
  vendor-neutral curriculum + train-the-trainer model for SAMAI scale.

### 5.2 UAE — Digital Dubai (AI+) & MBZUAI
- **What:** UAE is training **80,000 federal staff** in Agentic AI (MBZUAI
  knowledge partnership) and **50,000 Dubai government staff** via Digital Dubai's
  **AI+** program (under Dubai Future Foundation).
- **Why you fit:** They assemble curricula "with leading universities and global
  tech firms" — a neutral provider fits the specialist/trainer-support tracks.
- **How to contact:**
  - MBZUAI training programs → https://mbzuai.ac.ae/innovate/training-programs/ →
    executive education / partnerships.
  - Digital Dubai / Dubai Future Foundation → partnership enquiry via
    dubaifuture.ae.
- **First action:** Propose your cert as a neutral module within AI+ specialist or
  trainer tracks.

### 5.3 Corporate co-delivery (warm channel into government)
- Microsoft (3M Saudis by 2030 commitment), Gartner/Wipro exec programs are active.
  You can sit *alongside* them as the **neutral foundation layer** — pitch as
  complementary, not competitive.

**GCC sequencing:** SDAIA partnership proposal (Saudi) → MBZUAI / Digital Dubai
(UAE) → position as neutral layer beside corporate programs. Local partner or
registered entity usually required — line one up early.
