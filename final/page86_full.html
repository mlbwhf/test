Curriculum — six modules

Modules deliver across all three tracks at the right depth. Foundations practitioners go wide; executives go deep on governance and mutation.

M01 · AI fluency — the new literacy

Establish the baseline. How LLMs actually work, what they can and cannot do, and how to evaluate output rigorously rather than vibes-checking.

M02 · Agents & workflow automation

Move beyond single-prompt use. Design multi-step agent workflows for the highest-leverage tasks in your role — reporting, research, drafting, review.

M03 · Leading team-level AI adoption

Run the rollout. Maturity assessment, ceremony redesign, training cadence, resistance patterns, measurement of compounding value.

M04 · Safety, ethics & risk governance

Risk taxonomy, data privacy boundaries, model selection by sensitivity, human-in-the-loop patterns, and how to talk to your CISO.

M05 · AI-native operating model

Restructure value streams to treat agents as first-class participants. Governance, talent, capability building, and the AI Steward role.

M06 · Strategy, portfolio & mutation measurement

Enterprise scorecard. How to measure whether the AI investment is actually mutating the org rather than producing demos. Quarterly executive review pattern.

FAQ

Do I need a technical background?

Only for portions of Foundations — and even there, no coding required. Change Agent and Executive tracks are entirely operational and strategic.

Can I just take one track?

Yes. Each track stands alone and grants its own credential. Most participants stack over 6–12 months as their role evolves.

How does this relate to ASPC?

ASPC is for active SPCs going deep on D3/D4/D5 in a SAFe rollout. AI-Native is broader — for any role or org adopting AI as the operating system.

Ready to go AI-native?

Cohorts open quarterly. Enterprise pricing on request. Tracks can be taken individually or stacked.
