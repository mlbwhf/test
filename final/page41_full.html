The standard course, plus four dimensions

Every SPC course covers the Implementation Roadmap. Ours adds the four dimensions that the standard curriculum doesn't reach.

D2 · Innovation Framework

How to make innovation a routine output of every PI, not a once-a-quarter hackathon.

D3 · AI-Native

Restructuring roles and ceremonies around AI, not bolting AI onto today's process.

D4 · AI Automation

Concrete automation of pre-PI prep, dependency mapping, and ROAM across the toolchain.

D5 · Mutation

How to measure whether the operating model actually changed — mutation indicators, not vanity metrics.

Four days, end to end

The official curriculum runs across the first three days; day four is our framework extension.

Day 1-2 · Lead the transformation

The Implementation Roadmap, reaching the tipping point, designing the implementation, launching ARTs and trains.

Day 3 · Train and coach

Becoming a SAFe Practice Consultant: how to train, coach, and certify inside your own organization.

Day 4 · The framework extension

Our exclusive add-on: D2 through D5 worked through with real templates you take home. This is what makes the certification AI-native.
