Five phases, assess to mutate

The roadmap is a sequence, not a menu. Each phase has an entry condition and a measurable exit.

Phase 1 · Assess

Current-state assessment across the five dimensions. Output is a scored baseline and a prioritized transformation backlog. Two weeks, fixed scope.

Phase 2 · Foundation

LACE stood up, first ART launched, first PI Planning run. D1 and D2 become standing practice. Exit when PI1 completes with a working System Demo.

Phase 3 · Scale

Additional ARTs, Solution Train where needed, LPM stand-up. D3 AI-Native restructure begins. Exit when portfolio flow is visible end to end.

Phase 4 · Automate

D4 AI Automation across the toolchain. Pre-PI prep, dependency mapping, ROAM automation. Exit when automation removes measurable manual toil.

Phase 5 · Mutate

D5 mutation confirmed: decision rights inside cadence, behavior change durable without scaffolding. Exit when the operating model holds on its own.

Three ways to run the roadmap

Same roadmap, three levels of support. Pick based on internal capacity and urgency.

DIY

You run the roadmap with our published playbooks and templates. Best for orgs with mature internal SPC capacity and time to self-serve.

Coached

We coach your team through each phase with weekly office hours and milestone reviews. The most popular model for mid-size rollouts.

Delivered

Our team runs the roadmap end to end alongside yours. Best when the mandate is urgent and internal capacity is thin.
