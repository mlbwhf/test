Who this is — and isn’t — for

ASPC is intentionally narrow. We cap the cohort at 12 because the value depends on cohort interaction, not lecture.

Required

  • Active SAFe Practice Consultant (SPC) credential
  • At least one ART launch you’ve directly led or co-led
  • Live access to an org currently rolling out SAFe
  • Willingness to share real cases with the cohort under NDA
  • Commitment to 90 days of bi-weekly calls plus inter-call work

Not required

  • Deep AI/ML engineering background
  • Existing AI Automation tooling in your environment
  • Authority over the rollout (coaches benefit too)
  • A specific SAFe configuration
  • Pre-completed Leading SAFe (you already have SPC)

The three dimensions classroom SPC doesn’t teach

ASPC assumes D1 and D2. The 90 days are spent in the depths of D3, D4, and D5 — with your real environment as the case study.

D3

Module 01

AI-Native — structural restructure

Three weeks. You leave with a re-drawn value stream map for your organization that includes the AI Capability Train as a first-class development value stream.

  • The AI Steward role: charter, accountabilities, RACI
  • Restructuring ceremonies so AI agents have obligations
  • Value stream redesign exercise on your environment
  • Failure mode patterns: when D3 stalls and how to detect early

D4

Module 02

AI Automation — operationalizing AI in ceremonies

Three weeks. You deploy at least one of the four highest-ROI automations into your environment during the module, with cohort support.

  • PI planning prep automation: tooling, prompts, integration
  • Dependency mapping automation: continuous graph maintenance
  • Risk surfacing automation: agent-pre-classified ROAM
  • Portfolio insights automation: weekly LPM digest
  • ROI measurement: how to count time saved credibly

D5

Module 03

Mutation — measuring whether the rollout takes

Three weeks. You implement the four mutation indicators in your environment and baseline them. Final cohort session is mutual peer review of indicator trends.

  • Mutation Theory applied to enterprise change
  • The four 90-day mutation indicators: instrumentation
  • How to read indicator trends and intervene early
  • Sustaining mutation past the consultant exit
  • Indicator review session with the full cohort

The 90-day coaching cadence

ASPC is not a class. It’s a coaching cohort. Bi-weekly 90-minute group calls plus inter-call work in your own environment. Modules overlap rather than running in pure sequence.

Days 0–30

D3 deep dive + restructure

Bi-weekly calls. Re-draw your value stream map. AI Steward charter. First ceremony re-design.

Days 30–60

D4 automation deploy

Deploy at least one highest-ROI automation. Measure baseline-to-actual time saved. Cohort review of deployment patterns.

Days 60–90

D5 indicator baseline

Instrument the four mutation indicators. Baseline them. Final session is peer review of trends + sustainability plan.

FAQ

I just earned my SAFe Practice Consultant. Can I do ASPC right away?

We strongly prefer SPCs with at least one ART launch under their belt. If you’ve just earned the credential and haven’t yet been in the field, the 90-day post-SPC coaching cohort is the right next step.

What if my organization isn’t doing AI yet?

That’s actually a good moment for ASPC. The D3 restructure is something you do before AI saturates the operating model, not after. Cohort members regularly describe the value stream redesign exercise as the most important thing they did all year.

Is this trainer-led or peer-led?

Both. Our SPCT leads the framework material. The cohort does heavy lifting on case-study review — you’ll get more out of 11 other senior SPCs reviewing your value stream map than from any individual coach.

What credential do I get?

Advanced SAFe Practice Consultant (ASPC) issued by our practice. It’s not a Scaled Agile certification — the SPC class is the Scaled Agile credential. ASPC is the recognition of demonstrated depth across D3, D4, D5.

How is this different from D3/D4/D5 on the framework page?

The framework page explains the dimensions. ASPC is where you apply them in your environment with cohort support and a 90-day cadence. Reading the framework is necessary; it isn’t sufficient.
