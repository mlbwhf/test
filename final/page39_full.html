When consulting is right for you

Not every org needs consulting. Three patterns where a coached or co-delivered engagement reliably outperforms a DIY rollout.

Signal 01

You’re scaling past 3 ARTs and ceremonies are breaking

At three-plus ARTs, dependencies multiply nonlinearly. PI planning runs long. System Demos slip. The fix is structural — Solution Train design, real LPM — not another training class. An outside SPCT pays for itself in a single PI.

Signal 02

Leadership wants a transformation, not a training catalog

The CEO said “become AI-native” and the response can’t be “we’ll send 40 people to Leading SAFe.” You need an operating-model change with measurable mutation indicators by month 12. The deliverable is rewired behavior, not certified headcount.

Signal 03

You’ve tried internal LACE and it stalled

A LACE chartered as PMO subsidiary, with part-time SPCs and no budget authority, predictably stalls between ART 2 and ART 4. Co-delivered engagement re-charters the LACE as an operating function and gets it through the plateau.

Our delivery rhythm, discover to mutate

Every engagement runs on the same four-phase cadence. The framework you see on the framework page is the content; this is the rhythm we use to land it.

Week 1-2

Discover

Stakeholder interviews, current-state assessment, transformation backlog drafted with sponsor.

Month 1-3

Foundation

LACE chartered as operating function. First ART launched with full PI Planning. D1 and D2 standing operating practice by end of PI1.

Month 3-6

Scale

Additional ARTs launched. AI-Native restructure (D3). First AI automations land (D4). Mutation indicators baselined.

Month 6+

Mutate

Operating model changes confirmed (D5). LPM stand-up, decision rights moved inside cadence. Capability transfer begins.

The rhythm is not waterfall. Phases overlap deliberately. D3 design starts in Foundation. D5 measurement starts on day 90.

Questions buyers usually ask

How long is a typical engagement?

Coached: 3-6 months. Co-Delivered: 6-12 months and the most common shape. Fully Delivered: 12-24 months. Duration is set by how many ARTs need to launch and how far the operating model needs to move — not by tier alone.

Do we have to commit to a tier upfront?

No. Every engagement starts with a 2-week Discover phase that produces a written charter and a 90-day measurable plan. The tier becomes obvious once we’ve seen the current state.

What if we already have SPCs internally?

Even better. Coached or Co-Delivered are usually the right tiers. We don’t replace internal SPCs — we calibrate them and add the dimensions (D3, D4, D5) that classroom SPC certification doesn’t cover in depth.

How is this different from a Big-4 consultant?

One: an SPCT-credentialed lead personally leads delivery on every engagement. Two: we’re a SAFe Gold Partner with active SPCT credentials, not generalists. Three: our deliverable is a working operating model with measurable mutation indicators — not a slide deck.

Is this a proprietary framework?

No. SAFe and the Implementation Roadmap are properties of Scaled Agile, Inc. What our practice has assembled is an integration: D1 (Roadmap) + D2 (Innovation) + D3 (AI-Native) + D4 (AI Automation) + D5 (Mutation Theory). The integration is ours; the foundation belongs to Scaled Agile.
