Where the RTE role leads

The RTE role sits at the crossroads of delivery, leadership, and transformation. It's a natural step up from Scrum Master or Agile Coach, and a launchpad toward enterprise-level roles:

  • Solution Train Engineer (STE) — coordinating multiple ARTs on large solutions
  • SAFe Program Consultant (SPC) — leading transformations and certifying others
  • Agile Transformation Lead / Director of Agile — shaping the operating model enterprise-wide
  • Value Stream / Portfolio leadership — connecting strategy to execution
