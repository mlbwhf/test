# Certification pages — Corrections + per-cert FAQ
_Fixes: (1) SPCT vs Gold Partner terminology, (2) instructor attribution per course, (3) FAQ that's specific to each certification (not all SPC boilerplate)._

---
## 1) Terminology — get this exactly right (compliance)
| Term | What it actually is | Correct use |
|---|---|---|
| **SPCT** | SAFe Practice Consultant **Trainer** — an **individual's certification** (top SAFe credential; can train SPCs) | "Taught by a **certified SPCT**" — *only* for courses an SPCT teaches |
| **SAFe® Gold Partner** | A **company / partnership tier** with Scaled Agile | "Agile Agilist is a **SAFe® Gold Partner**" |
| **SPCT Gold Partner** | A Gold Partner **that employs a certified SPCT** | Use for the **company** when you have an SPCT on staff |
| **Authorised SAFe Instructor** | An **SPC or ASPC** authorised to teach role courses | "Taught by an **Authorised SAFe Instructor (SPC/ASPC)**" |

### ❌ Never write
- **"Gold SPCT"** — conflates a person's cert (SPCT) with a company tier (Gold Partner). Wrong.
- **"SPCT-led"** on a course an SPCT does **not** teach (see table below).

### ✅ Right
- Company line: **"SAFe® Gold Partner"** (or **"SPCT Gold Partner"** since you have a certified SPCT).
- A person is a **certified SPCT**; the company is an **SPCT Gold Partner**.

## 4) Per-certification FAQ (specific, not SPC boilerplate)

### Leading SAFe® (SA)
- **Who is it for?** Leaders, managers, and change agents guiding a Lean-Agile transformation. No prerequisite.
- **What do I earn?** The **SAFe® Agilist (SA)** certification after passing the exam (included).
- **How long?** 2 days live-virtual + exam.
- **Do I need experience?** No prerequisites; familiarity with Agile helps.
- **Who teaches it?** An **Authorised SAFe Instructor (SPC/ASPC)**.

### SPC — Implementing SAFe®
- **Who is it for?** Internal change agents, coaches, and consultants who will **launch ARTs and train others**.
- **What do I earn?** **SAFe® Practice Consultant (SPC)** — which licenses you to teach many SAFe courses.
- **How long?** 4 days live-virtual + exam.
- **Prerequisites?** Significant Agile/SAFe experience strongly recommended.
- **Who teaches it?** A **certified SPCT** (required for this course).

### ASPC — Advanced SPC
- **Who is it for?** Experienced SPCs advancing to enterprise-scale, AI-empowered transformation leadership.
- **What do I earn?** **Advanced SAFe Practice Consultant (ASPC)**.
- **Prerequisites?** Active **SPC** certification.
- **How long?** 4 days live-virtual + exam.
- **Who teaches it?** A **certified SPCT**.

### RTE — Release Train Engineer
- **Who is it for?** RTEs, STEs, and program/ART facilitators.
- **What do I earn?** **SAFe® Release Train Engineer (RTE)** certification.
- **How long?** 3 days live-virtual + exam.
- **Prerequisites?** SAFe experience recommended; launching/running an ART helps.
- **Who teaches it?** A **certified SPCT**.

### POPM — Product Owner / Product Manager
- **Who is it for?** POs, PMs, and business owners delivering value in a SAFe enterprise.
- **What do I earn?** **SAFe® Product Owner/Product Manager (POPM)**.
- **How long?** 2 days live-virtual + exam.
- **Who teaches it?** An **Authorised SAFe Instructor (SPC/ASPC)**.

### ASM / SSM — Advanced Scrum Master / Scrum Master
- **Who is it for?** Scrum Masters and team leads in a SAFe context.
- **What do I earn?** **SAFe® Scrum Master (SSM)** or **Advanced Scrum Master (SASM)**.
- **How long?** 2 days live-virtual + exam.
- **Who teaches it?** An **Authorised SAFe Instructor (SPC/ASPC)**.

### SAFe® DevOps (SDP)
- **Who is it for?** Anyone in the value stream building a continuous delivery pipeline.
- **What do I earn?** **SAFe® DevOps Practitioner (SDP)**.
- **How long?** 2 days live-virtual + exam.
- **Who teaches it?** An **Authorised SAFe Instructor (SPC/ASPC)**.

### LPM — Lean Portfolio Management
- **Who is it for?** Portfolio leaders, PMO, finance, and strategy stakeholders.
- **What do I earn?** **SAFe® Lean Portfolio Management (LPM)**.
- **How long?** 2–3 days live-virtual + exam.
- **Who teaches it?** An **Authorised SAFe Instructor (SPC/ASPC)**.

### APM — Agile Product Management
- **Who is it for?** Product managers applying design thinking + Lean UX at scale.
- **What do I earn?** **SAFe® Agile Product Management (APM)**.
- **How long?** 3 days live-virtual + exam.
- **Who teaches it?** An **Authorised SAFe Instructor (SPC/ASPC)**.

### AI-Native
- **Who is it for?** Anyone closing the enterprise AI adoption gap — no prior SAFe required.
- **What do I earn?** The **AI-Native** certification.
- **Who teaches it?** An **Authorised SAFe Instructor (SPC/ASPC)**.
