# 1. The spine

Five states. Three are **ground** (where an organization stands); two are **crossings** (what it must do to move). The animated strip on the site is this spine, and from now on the five states are the single ordering principle for the framework, the assessment, the workshops, the certification, the cast, and both books.

| # | State | Kind | Operating-model wave | Culture (Westrum-style) | One line |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 01 | **REFLEX** | Ground (start) | 01 Iterative delivery at scale | Metric-bound · control-oriented | Watching the wrong scoreboard. |
| 02 | **PLAYGROUND** | Crossing one | 02 Innovation in cadence | → generative · from control to trust | Innovation practiced, not announced. |
| 03 | **SIGNAL SENSING** | Ground (across) | 03 AI-Native | Generative · signal-aware | A signal answers: what is about to? |
| 04 | **IMMUNE** | Crossing two | 04 AI automation | → signal-first · from metrics to signals | Your immune system is your people. |
| 05 | **MUTATION** | Ground (landed, never finished) | 05 Mutation | Signal-first · drift-oriented | A capability you keep needing. |

**The two chasms** fall where the crossings are: Chasm One is crossed *by* the Playground (control → trust); Chasm Two is crossed *by* IMMUNE (metrics → signals). This is the same five-wave operating model already on agile-agilist.com/services/operating-model and in the "Which wave are you in?" assessment — the five states are the **names** of the five waves, so there is one model, not two.

**Naming rules.** The state is always written *Signal Sensing* (two words) so it never collides with *SIGNAL* (Book Two's title) or *SignalNet* (the tool). *IMMUNE* in caps when it names the state or the counter-mutation; *immune system* in lower case when it is the metaphor. *Mutation* is both the fifth state and the title word of the theory book — that is intentional.

# 2. State by state

Each state carries: the culture it describes, where it lives in the books, the theory it rests on, the practices and tools, the workshops, the cast, the assessment items, the measurable "you are here" signals, the line to quote, and what it takes to leave.

## 01 · REFLEX

*Ground. The starting condition. Also the state an organization relapses into after a rebrand.*

- **Culture:** metric-bound, control-oriented. Information arrives late and aggregated. Messengers wait for permission. Decisions are made on lagging numbers that are true "just too late to do anything about it."
- **In the books:** *The Innovation Playground* Book One opening (Elevate Labs before the Task Force) and Book Two Ch. 1–2 (Layla's first boardroom; "the metrics lie"; Exhibit 2.1). *The Mutation Age* Part I Ch. 1 (The Executive Blind Spot; lagging vs leading) and Part II (nine case studies of what Reflex costs).
- **Theory:** lagging vs leading indicators; the Four Filters that kill a signal on its way up (Aggregation, Hierarchy, Incentive, Identity); Goodhart's law; organizational reflex ("memory is useful, reflex is fatal"); Westrum's bureaucratic culture.
- **Diagnostic instrument:** **The Seven Stagnation Signals** (the red panel) — R&D Translation Gap · Platform Dilution · Ecosystem Disengagement · Consulting-to-Product Imbalance · Talent Flight from Core Teams · Brand Perception Lag · Absence of Anomaly Listening. These are the fingerprint of Reflex.
- **Workshops:** none run *in* Reflex; it is diagnosed. **S1 Signal Mapping** is the exit door (it turns the dashboard into Exhibit 2.1). **P7 The Rebrand Test** detects relapse.
- **Cast:** Terry — The Incumbent CEO ("So now we're running governance by group chat?"). Connor, the analyst who asks whether the metrics lie.
- **Assessment:** wave-01 items (predictable cross-team cadence; dependencies visible; one planning rhythm) **plus** a Stagnation-Signals screen. Low wave-01 means the organization has no floor; high wave-01 with high Stagnation Signals is textbook Reflex.
- **Quote:** "We're not losing because our product is bad. We're losing because we're watching the wrong scoreboard."
- **To leave:** cross Chasm One — control → trust — by building the Playground.

## 02 · PLAYGROUND

*Crossing one. The method that carries a culture from control to trust. Book One.*

- **Culture shift:** from bureaucratic to generative (Westrum). Information is actively sought; messengers are trained; failure causes enquiry; novelty is implemented.
- **In the books:** *The Innovation Playground* Book One, Ch. 1–7: Oliver's Task Force, the six Playground practices, the Cultural Wall, the Candor Contract, the Innovation Matrix (Ch. 6), the first Athena.
- **Theory:** Amabile (creativity under pressure); psychological safety; attention and boredom (Zomorodi, Kahneman); Inverse Conway; Christensen's disruption as a market pattern; the 4×4 Innovation Matrix; "experience, then evidence, then build."
- **Practices (the six):** protect attention · tell the truth sooner · test in small loops · first useful moment · redesign the team shape · choose two cells and say no to fourteen.
- **Workshops:** **P1** Charter the Playground · **P2** Attention Audit · **P3** The Candor Contract · **P4** First Useful Moment · **P5** The Cultural Wall · **P6** The Innovation Matrix *(flagship, guide live)* · **P7** The Rebrand Test.
- **Cast:** Oliver Reid — The Sponsor Who Chooses · Maya Brooks — The Signal Reader · Max — The Operating-Model Engineer · Rhea Kapoor — The Firestarter.
- **Assessment:** wave-02 items (protected experiment capacity; pre-agreed success criteria; an outside idea shipped last quarter). MRX dimension: **Ambidextrous Capital** (explore budget that survives quarterly pressure) and half of **Structural Flexibility**.
- **Quote:** "Experience, then evidence, then build." / "We are not playing the whole matrix."
- **The trap at the far side:** the Rebrand. "The moment a company gives itself a new name is usually the moment it decides it has finished transforming." P7 exists for this.

## 03 · SIGNAL SENSING

*Ground. Across the first chasm, facing the second. Book Two opens here.*

- **Culture:** generative and now signal-aware. The culture changed; the scoreboard hasn't yet. Leaders begin to read live behaviour instead of final reports.
- **In the books:** *The Innovation Playground* — Interlude, The Rebrand, The Second Chasm framework, and Book Two Ch. 1–3 (The Metrics Lie; Ghost in the Dashboard; Athena's mutation; Grown, Not Built; the five-layers dialogue between Layla and Daniel). *The Mutation Age* Part I Ch. 2–3 (The Cambrian Explosion of AI; Signals vs Noise; the two signal tests — is it repeated? is it costly for the source?).
- **Theory:** Moore's sequence (Crossing the Chasm → Tornado → Zone to Win → the AI chasm; "a chasm is crossed when one function completely re-engineers itself"); metrics → signals; Exhibit 2.1 (lagging metric → the signal that moves first → Goodhart check → named listener); Grown, Not Built (a thing you grow cannot be governed like a thing you write); the five capability layers.
- **Instrument:** **The Six Vitality Signals** (the green panel) — Customer Learning Velocity · Internal Rewiring Rate · AI Talent Flywheel · Platform Surface Area · Mutation Ecosystem Density · Agentic AI Behaviour Emergence. These are what a sensing organization watches instead of revenue, share, NPS and feature velocity.
- **Workshops:** **S1** Signal Mapping *(flagship, guide live)* · **S2** Grown, Not Built · **S3** Observe, Don't Ask · **S4** Sensemaking Cells.
- **Cast:** Layla Sharif — The Transformation Officer · Daniel Torres — The Builder Who Sees Too Late · Athena — The System You Grew.
- **Assessment:** wave-03 items (a core process redesigned around AI; roles changed; AI treated as actors with identity and scoped permissions). MRX dimension: **Signal Sensitivity** and **AI Talent Flywheel**.
- **Quote:** "A lagging metric answers: what happened? A signal answers: what is about to?"
- **To leave:** cross Chasm Two — metrics → signals, made structural — by building IMMUNE.

## 04 · IMMUNE

*Crossing two. The counter-mutation: sensing turned into a distributed organ. Book Two's turn.*

- **Culture shift:** from signal-aware to signal-first. Noticing becomes load-bearing rather than personality-dependent — "the meeting that runs without her in the room."
- **In the books:** *The Innovation Playground* Book Two Ch. 7–11: the Side Door / Silent Infiltration ("This isn't competition. It's colonization." / "The invasion wasn't coming. It had already happened."), Governance Latency, Terry's rebellion, and Ch. 11 The Counter-Mutation — Layla writes IMMUNE, draws the three rings, and SignalNet goes live. *The Mutation Age* Part IV Ch. 4 (Governance for Exponential Eras).
- **Theory:** Matzinger's danger model (the immune system responds to damage, not foreignness); inattentional blindness (Simons & Chabris); NASA's Aviation Safety Reporting System as the precedent for blame-free signal logging; the XZ half-second (Andres Freund, 2024); the agent era's plumbing (AAIF / MCP) and its breakouts (Salesloft Drift 2025; the OpenAI sandbox escape, July 2026); governance latency as signal-to-decision delay; "a rulebook does not report its own cost."
- **The three rings:** Suppliers · Internal Systems · People — with People at the core.
- **Operating line:** **Athena detects. Humans decide. Governance records.**
- **Workshops:** **S5** Governance Latency · **S6** IMMUNE / SignalNet *(flagship, guide live)* · **S7** The Blind-Spot Register.
- **Cast:** Layla (as architect), Terry (as the one who changes), Julian and Gabriel (the Second System team).
- **Assessment:** wave-04 items (a named human owns every autonomous decision; automation chosen where it changed unit economics; every autonomous action reconstructible). MRX dimensions: **Ethical Guardrails** (containment as velocity) and the other half of **Structural Flexibility**.
- **Quote:** "AI is not the threat. Speed without sense is. Your immune system is your people. Teach them to notice."
- **What it produces:** a live SignalNet, a governance-latency baseline, a blind-spot register, and the rule that every version of the playbook names what the last version got wrong.

## 05 · MUTATION

*Ground — the landing that is not a finish line. Book Two's close and the whole of* The Mutation Age.

- **Culture:** signal-first, drift-oriented. Information is noticed before it becomes a number. The organization can change the model itself, not just adapt inside the one it has.
- **In the books:** *The Innovation Playground* Book Two Ch. 12–13 and Epilogue (The Living Playbook; Oliver's return — "She was always listening. We were the ones who stopped."; "The noticing doesn't route through you."). *The Mutation Age* Part III (Five Levers of Reinvention; Six Vitality Signals; the Indicator Dashboard), Part IV (the Leadership Playbook), Part V (Mutation Readiness field guide; the Mutation Readiness Scorecard).
- **Theory:** the Five Levers — Platform-First Mindset · Ecosystem Orchestration · AI Talent Flywheel · Ambidextrous Capital Allocation · Ethical Guardrails for AI Velocity; the Leading-Indicator Dashboard; the MRS scale (0 Blind · 1 Aware but slow · 2 Reactive · 3 Proactive and embedded · 4 Self-mutating); Moore's warning that a chasm is a capability you keep needing.
- **Instruments:** the live **Mutation Readiness Index** (six dimensions: Signal Sensitivity · Structural Flexibility · AI Talent Flywheel · Ambidextrous Capital · Ethical Guardrails · Narrative Coherence; bands Mutation-Blind / Aware / Ready) and the **Leading-Indicator Dashboard**.
- **Workshops:** none documented yet — this is the gap. Proposed: **M1 The Living Playbook** (write the "what we got wrong" rule into the operating model; artifact: version-zero of the organization's playbook with its correction clause) and **M2 Mutation Readiness, facilitated** (the MRX run with a leadership team, scored against the Vitality Signals; artifact: the readiness scorecard and the one lever to pull next).
- **Cast:** Layla (the meeting without her), Oliver (returned), Athena (still listening).
- **Assessment:** wave-05 items (a core part of the model retired or rebuilt in twelve months; leading signals tracked; someone rewarded for a signal that invalidated a plan) **plus** the full MRX as the deep dive. MRX dimension owned here: **Narrative Coherence** (the story the organization tells itself still matches what it does — the opposite of the Rebrand).
- **Quote:** "The playbook is alive only while it can still be corrected." / "Crossing a chasm is not an event you complete. It is a capability you keep needing."
- **Why the strip must not stop here:** the ember ball keeps pulsing. Mutation is a condition you maintain, and relapse to Reflex is one rebrand away.

# 4. The rearrangement

What changes, asset by asset, so the five states become the spine everywhere.

**Framework page (mutation.agile-agilist.com).** The animated five-state strip becomes the master navigation. Each tile opens a state section with the nine blocks above (culture, books, theory, instrument, workshops, cast, assessment, quote, exit). The "Two Chasms" figure stays as the explanatory diagram beneath it; the strip is the thing people click.

**Assessment ("Which wave are you in?").** Keep the 15 items and the scoring unchanged. Relabel the five layers as state + wave: *01 Reflex — Iterative delivery at scale … 05 Mutation.* The result page reads "You are in **SIGNAL SENSING**; **IMMUNE** is not holding" and routes to that state's flagship workshop. The live Mutation Readiness assessment on agile-agilist.com stays where it is and becomes the Mutation state's deep dive — linked, not moved.

**Workshops.** Regroup the catalogue from two crossings into five states: Reflex (diagnostic + S1 as exit + P7 as relapse test) · Playground P1–P7 · Signal Sensing S1–S4 · Immune S5–S7 · Mutation M1–M2 (new; to be written). Keep the P/S codes so the guides don't need renaming; add M.

**Cast.** Tag every persona with its state(s); the Cast page gets a five-state filter. Terry and Oliver appear twice (start and end), which is the point of their arcs.

**Certification.** Foundations (2 days) = Reflex → Playground → Signal Sensing: diagnose, build the Playground, map the signals. Facilitator (3 days) = all five states, all fourteen workshops, licence to run the guides. Leading (1 day, executive) = Immune → Mutation: governance latency, the living playbook, the MRX read with the leadership team.

**Books.** Book One of the novel is Playground; Book Two runs Signal Sensing → Immune → Mutation; *The Mutation Age* is the Mutation state's textbook, with Part I as the Reflex diagnosis. The sales page, the back-cover copy and the podcast season arc all follow the same five-state order.

**Social.** The six quote cards and the 27 snaps already map onto the states (table 3.1, last row); tag each post with its state so the four pre-launch weeks walk the audience from Reflex to Mutation.

**Trademark.** The state names are descriptive words and are not registrable on their own; the registrable marks remain THE TWO CHASMS and MUTATION READINESS INDEX (with the disclaimer already flagged), and the five-state strip as a design mark if you want it.
