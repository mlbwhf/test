Recommended IA

Top level (4): Indexes · Reports · Glossary · About. Subscribe/Contact demoted to a thin utility strip above the bar — visible, never first.

Indexes → 9 sections + Compare is the entire second level. The dropdown is styled as a miniature index page itself — numbered rows, mono eyebrow — rather than a generic list, so the credibility signal lands before a click. It links to section hubs only; individual index pages live on those hub cards, so publishing a new index never touches the nav.

Reports → 3 items: the two collections (Dark Side, Real-World AI) plus "Latest Reports," with an "All Reports" escape hatch. Same logic: standalone news reports never get their own menu entry.

Glossary, About: flat links, no second level — they don't need one and a fake dropdown would dilute the pattern.

Flags

Fully expressible as a nested WP <ul> — the panel is just an unusually-styled .sub-menu (CSS grid instead of a vertical list). No custom framework needed.

Hover-only dropdowns break on touch: nav items with children need click/tap-to-toggle (GeneratePress's built-in submenu-toggle arrow covers this) — not pure :hover CSS.

LLM/crawler-friendliness: keep every link a real <a href> present in server-rendered markup at all times — panels should hide via CSS/ARIA (aria-expanded), never by omitting the markup or lazy-loading it on click.

"Compare" is a tool, not a content section — kept visually distinct (right-aligned, no number) inside the Indexes panel rather than filed as a 10th section.
