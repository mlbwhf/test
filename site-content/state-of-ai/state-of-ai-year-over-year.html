What changed in three years

Three years of data show AI accelerating in scale while several growth rates slow. ChatGPT's weekly users went from 300 million in December 2024 to 800 million in October 2025 and more than 1 billion in August 2026, but grew only 11% in the last six months of that run. Organisational adoption, as McKinsey measures it, rose from 78% to 88% and then stalled at 89%. Individual use at work kept climbing: Gallup's share of US employees using AI a few times a week or more went from 12% to 23% to 30%.

The money compounded. Big-four capital expenditure went from about $245 billion in 2024 to about $413 billion in 2025 and $508 billion in the first nine months of 2026. Venture funding to AI companies went from more than $100 billion to $202.3 billion and then about $488 billion in nine months, by Report AI's calculation from Crunchbase. The combined run-rate revenue of OpenAI and Anthropic rose from about $6.5 billion in December 2024 to about $115 billion in the third quarter of 2026, about 18-fold.

Prices and volumes moved in opposite directions, and so did costs and rules. The cheapest GPT-4-level tokens got 58% cheaper between 2024 and 2026 while Google's monthly token volume rose about 330-fold. AI-attributed US job cuts went from 12,742 to 54,836 to 121,401 in ten months of 2026, and incidents logged by the AI Incident Database from 233 to 362. Regulation moved from enactment to delay: the EU AI Act entered into force in 2024, applied its first obligations in 2025, and postponed its high-risk rules in 2026. Changes into 2026 compare a year to date or latest reading with a full year; each is marked.

Usage

  • ChatGPT's weekly users grew from 300 million (December 2024) to 800 million (October 2025) to more than 1 billion (August 2026) — roughly 3.3x in under two years (DERIVED: 1,000M / 300M), with growth decelerating in 2026.
  • Organisational AI adoption rose from 78% (2024) to 88% (2025) to 89% (2026) in McKinsey's surveys: the big move was 2023 to 2025, and the 2026 change is within noise.
  • Individual use roughly doubled on every general-population measure: US employees using AI at work went from 27% (Q2 2024) to 46% (Q4 2025) to 52% (May 2026) in Gallup's survey, and US adults aged 18 to 64 using generative AI went from 44.6% (August 2024) to 54.6% (August 2025) to 62% (May 2026) in the St. Louis Fed's survey.
  • Use shifted from work to everyday life: the non-work share of ChatGPT consumer messages rose from 53% (June 2024) to 73% (June 2025) in OpenAI's NBER study, while AI's share of website referral traffic stayed below half a percent (0.02% in 2024, 0.24% in 2025, 0.32% in 2026, SE Ranking).

Spending

  • Big-tech capex roughly doubled in two years: about $245 billion in 2024 and $413 billion in 2025 for Microsoft, Alphabet, Amazon and Meta, and $508 billion in the first nine months of 2026 alone (Report AI sums of reported figures).
  • IDC's measured AI infrastructure spending went from $153.9 billion (2024) to $318.7 billion (2025), with $521 billion forecast for 2026; the first two are actuals, the third is a forecast.
  • Gartner's worldwide AI spending series rose from about $988 billion (2024 estimate) to $1.79 trillion (2025 estimate) and a $2.67 trillion forecast for 2026, but category changes between editions mean part of each step is definitional.
  • In enterprise LLM APIs the leader changed and then pulled away: Anthropic's share of enterprise API spend went 24% (2024), 40% (2025), 55% (2026), while OpenAI went 34%, 27%, 31% (Menlo Ventures, an Anthropic investor).

Finance

  • AI's share of global venture funding rose from about a third in 2024 to about half in 2025 and about 72% in January–September 2026 (Crunchbase basis; 2026 is Report AI's calculation from quarterly figures).
  • OpenAI's valuation rose from $157 billion (October 2024) to $500 billion (October 2025) to a reported $1.25 trillion (November 2026); Anthropic's priced valuation went from about $18 billion (early 2024) to $183 billion (September 2025) to $965 billion (May 2026).
  • Model-maker revenue grew far faster than capex: OpenAI's recognised revenue went from about $3.7 billion (2024) to $13.07 billion (2025), and Anthropic's from about $0.4 billion to $4.6 billion to $16.3 billion in H1 2026 alone, narrowing big-four capex per lab revenue dollar from about 60x to 23x to about 13x on 2026 guidance.
  • Nvidia's data-centre revenue went from $115.2 billion (fiscal year to January 2025) to $193.7 billion (to January 2026) to $164.2 billion in the first half of fiscal 2027 alone, while the financing of AI shifted from cash flow to bonds, equity and off-balance-sheet vehicles.

Work

  • AI-attributed US job cuts rose from 12,742 in 2024 to 54,836 in 2025 and 121,401 in January–October 2026; as a share of all announced cuts, from about 1.7% to 4.5% to 19.7% (Report AI's division of Challenger figures).
  • The share of US Indeed postings mentioning AI rose from 2.71% at the end of 2024 to 4.44% at the end of 2025 and 7.34% on 30 September 2026, about 2.7 times in 21 months.
  • US software-development postings sat about 32.5% below their February 2020 level at the end of both 2024 and 2025, then recovered to 18.4% below by 30 October 2026: AI did not stop developer hiring, but the recovery favours senior roles while early-career employment in exposed occupations kept falling behind.
  • PwC's AI skills wage premium in job advertisements rose from 25% (2023 ads) to 56% (2024 ads, restated 57%) to 62% (2025 ads), and daily AI use among US employees rose from about 4% (Q2 2024) to 12% (late 2025) to 15% (May 2026).

Models & Prices

  • Coding: the top SWE-bench Verified score went from 71.7% (o3 preview, Dec 2024) to 80.9% (Claude Opus 4.5, Nov 2025) to 97.0% (Claude Opus 5, Sep 2026, independent harness) — saturated in under two years.
  • Competition: the top US model's Arena lead over China went 9.26% (Jan 2024) → 1.70% (Feb 2025) → 2.7% (Mar 2026), and the closed-over-open lead 8.04% → 1.70% → 3.3%: convergence, then a modest reopening.
  • Frontier prices: Anthropic's Opus-tier output price fell from $75 (2024) to $25 (2025) to $20 per million tokens (2026), and OpenAI's newest flagship from $60 (o1) to $10 (GPT-5, GPT-6 Sol) — but a new top tier appeared above Opus at $50 (Claude Fable 5.1).
  • Reasoning: AI went from a 28/42 silver at the 2024 IMO to a certified 35/42 gold in 2025 to reported perfect 42/42 scores in 2026.

Tokens

  • Tokens per dollar at GPT-4 quality rose from about 2.1 million (2024) to 3.2 million (2025) to 5.0 million (2026), against 27,000 for GPT-4 in March 2023, so most of the gain came before 2024. At OpenAI's flagship the figure barely moved: 229,000, then 291,000, then 250,000.
  • Google's monthly token volume went from 9.7 trillion (spring 2024) to 480 trillion (May 2025) to 3.2 quadrillion (May 2026); Doubao's daily volume went from 4 trillion (Dec 2024) to 50 trillion (Dec 2025) to 180 trillion (Jun 2026).
  • Combined OpenAI and Anthropic run-rate revenue went from about $6.5 billion (Dec 2024) to $29 billion (end 2025) to $115 billion (Q3 2026): about 18 times in under two years, an order of magnitude less than token volume. Menlo's enterprise spend estimates ($11.5 billion for 2024; $37 billion, restated to $33 billion, for 2025; an annualised $176.8 billion in Sep 2026 on a new definition) are not comparable across that break.
  • Reported business return improved only slightly. Wharton's positive-ROI share went from about 65% to 74% to 80%, McKinsey's EBIT-contribution share held near 37-39%, and the share reporting strong or full returns stayed below one in three.

Infrastructure & Energy

  • Nvidia's data-centre revenue went from $115.2 billion (FY ending Jan 2025) to $193.7 billion (FY ending Jan 2026) to $164.2 billion in the first half of fiscal 2027 alone; custom silicon grew faster, with Broadcom's AI revenue going from $12.2 billion to about $20 billion to $35.9 billion in three quarters.
  • North American primary-market vacancy fell from 2.8% (H1 2024) to 1.6% (H1 2025) to 1.4% (H1 2026) while capacity under construction nearly doubled from 3,872 MW to 7,481 MW: building faster has not loosened the market.
  • The scale of power deals grew roughly eightfold in two years, from Microsoft's 835 MW reactor restart (2024) to Amazon's 1,920 MW Talen contract (2025) to Meta's 6.6 GW nuclear package (2026), and PJM capacity prices rose from $269.92 to the $325–333 cap.
  • H100 rental prices fell from about $8 an hour (2023) to about $2 (late 2024 and December 2025), then rose to about $2.88 (October 2026), a reversal that marks the shift from training-led to inference-led demand.

Geography

  • US private AI investment rose from $109.1 billion in 2024 to $285.9 billion in 2025 while China's rose from $9.3 billion to $12.4 billion, widening the ratio from about 12x to 23x (Stanford HAI).
  • The US share of newly added AI compute climbed from about 60% (2024) to 72% (2025) to 78% (H1 2026), according to Epoch AI, even as China's share of AI patents and open-model downloads rose.
  • The US-China top-model gap fell from 9.26% (Jan 2024) to 1.70% (Feb 2025) and then held near parity at 2.7% (Mar 2026) on Chatbot Arena/LMArena (Stanford HAI).
  • China's generative AI user base grew from 249 million (Dec 2024) to 602 million (Dec 2025) to more than 700 million (Jun 2026), according to CNNIC, making it the largest national user base by that survey measure.

Frequently asked

What grew fastest in AI between 2024 and 2026?

Token volume: Google's monthly token processing rose about 330-fold, from about 9.7 trillion in spring 2024 to more than 3.2 quadrillion in May 2026. Combined OpenAI and Anthropic run-rate revenue rose about 18-fold over a similar period.

Did AI adoption keep growing in 2026?

Organisational adoption plateaued: McKinsey found 78% of organisations using AI in 2024, 88% in 2025 and 89% in 2026. Individual use kept growing: Gallup found the share of US employees using AI a few times a week or more rose from 12% to 23% to 30%.

Are AI prices falling?

At fixed capability, yes: the cheapest model of GPT-4 quality cost about $0.48 per million tokens in 2024 and about $0.20 in 2026 on a 3:1 input:output blend. At the frontier, list prices barely moved: OpenAI's flagship bought about 229,000 tokens per dollar in 2024 and about 250,000 in 2026 (Report AI calculations).

Are AI job cuts rising?

Annual totals rose from 12,742 in 2024 to 54,836 in 2025 and 121,401 in January to October 2026, according to Challenger, Gray & Christmas. The monthly count fell to 1,265 in October 2026, the lowest of the year.

How are the year-over-year changes calculated?

Report AI computes each change only where both years have a comparable value on the same definition; these are marked DERIVED. 2026 values are year to date or the latest reading, so a change into 2026 compares a part year with a full year where noted.

Methodology & sources

Figures are compiled from company disclosures, regulators, research firms and reputable reporting, and cross-checked against Report AI's own index pages. Each value carries its as-of date, source and a confidence rating: HIGH where the primary source was read, MEDIUM where it rests on consistent reputable secondary reporting (most figures, because many primary pages cannot be opened from our research environment), DERIVED where it is our own arithmetic, and CONTESTED where credible sources disagree. A year means the calendar year the figure describes; 2026 values are the latest available and marked “to date”. Weekly and monthly user counts, and forecasts and outcomes, are never compared as if they were the same measure. Percentage changes are Report AI arithmetic (DERIVED) and are shown only where both years have a comparable numeric value; 2026 values are to date, so changes into 2026 compare a part-year or latest reading with a full year where noted. These reports compile and verify secondary data with original analysis; they are not original surveys. See our editorial standards, corrections log and fact-check register.
