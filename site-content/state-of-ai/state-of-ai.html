Analysis

The State of AI series is Report AI's set of named, dated reports on artificial intelligence, built to be quoted. Each of the eleven quarterly reports covers one theme: usage, spending, finance, work, models and prices, tokens, infrastructure and energy, geography, industries, risk, and regulation. Each opens with an executive summary of self-contained figure sentences, argues its case in chapters with numbered exhibits, compares 2024, 2025 and 2026, and ends with what to watch next.

Once a year the series is rolled up into State of AI in Numbers, the annual edition: one section per theme, written as a record of the year. The 2024 and 2025 editions were published as retrospectives in November 2026; the 2026 edition follows in mid-December. A year-over-year page puts every comparable measure side by side.

Every value carries its source, the date it describes and a confidence rating, and every report has a CSV download. Most figures are MEDIUM: they rest on consistent reputable reporting because many primary pages cannot be read directly from our research environment. Figures we compute are marked DERIVED and explained. Errors are logged in our corrections log the day they are fixed.

Frequently asked

What is the State of AI series?

A set of eleven quarterly Report AI reports, one per theme (usage, spending, finance, work, models and prices, tokens, infrastructure and energy, geography, industries, risk and regulation), plus the annual State of AI in Numbers and a year-over-year comparison.

How often are the reports updated?

The theme reports are updated quarterly, with the edition named on each page (the current edition is Q4 2026). State of AI in Numbers is published each December, and the year-over-year page is updated with each edition.

How should I cite a State of AI report?

Use the citation line in each report's Cite this report box, for example: Report AI, State of AI Usage 2026, Q4 2026 edition, published 11 November 2026, https://report-ai.org/indexes/state-of-ai/state-of-ai-usage/.

Can I use the data?

Yes. Every report has a free CSV download with the source, date and confidence rating for each value, and the figures and charts may be quoted with attribution to Report AI and a link.

How reliable are the figures?

Each value is rated HIGH (primary source read), MEDIUM (consistent reputable secondary reporting), DERIVED (Report AI arithmetic, explained) or CONTESTED (credible sources disagree, both given). Corrections are logged publicly on the day they are made.

Methodology & sources

Figures are compiled from company disclosures, regulators, research firms and reputable reporting, and cross-checked against Report AI's own index pages. Each value carries its as-of date, source and a confidence rating: HIGH where the primary source was read, MEDIUM where it rests on consistent reputable secondary reporting (most figures, because many primary pages cannot be opened from our research environment), DERIVED where it is our own arithmetic, and CONTESTED where credible sources disagree. A year means the calendar year the figure describes; 2026 values are the latest available and marked “to date”. Weekly and monthly user counts, and forecasts and outcomes, are never compared as if they were the same measure. These reports compile and verify secondary data with original analysis; they are not original surveys. See our editorial standards, corrections log and fact-check register.
