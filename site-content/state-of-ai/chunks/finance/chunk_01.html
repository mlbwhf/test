At a glance
  • Crunchbase recorded $679 billion of global venture funding in January–September 2026, more than in any previous full year, and Report AI calculates that about 72% of it, roughly $488 billion, went to AI companies.
  • OpenAI closed a $122 billion funding round at an $852 billion post-money valuation on 31 March 2026, the largest private round on record, and closed a first tranche of about $11 billion at a reported $1.25 trillion valuation on 6 November 2026.
  • Anthropic's public S-1, filed on 26 October 2026, reports revenue of $16.3 billion for the first half of 2026, up from about $1.2 billion a year earlier, and its first operating profit, $974 million, in the second quarter.
  • OpenAI told investors its annualised revenue was almost $50 billion at the end of September 2026 (Financial Times, CNBC, Reuters), and Bloomberg reported Anthropic's run-rate at more than $65 billion at the end of July 2026; the two companies count partner revenue differently.
  • Nvidia reported data-centre revenue of $89.0 billion for the quarter to late July 2026, up 117% year on year, after $193.7 billion for the fiscal year to January 2026.
  • Amazon, Alphabet, Microsoft and Meta reported about $207 billion of combined capital expenditure for July–September 2026, and their full-year 2026 guidance sums to about $720–740 billion, roughly 13 times the annualised first-half revenue of OpenAI and Anthropic (Report AI calculation).
  • SpaceX, which absorbed xAI in February 2026, raised $75 billion at a $1.77 trillion valuation in its June 2026 IPO and then agreed to buy Cursor's maker, Anysphere, for $60 billion in stock.

In this report
  1. 1. Venture funding — AI took about 72% of a record $679 billion of venture funding in the first nine months of 2026, but a few frontier labs took most of it
  2. 2. Valuations — The leading labs are valued at 15 to 25 times their annualised revenue, and every 2026 round priced higher than the one before it
  3. 3. Revenue and losses — Lab revenue has grown faster than at any software business on record, and Anthropic reached an operating profit in 2026, but losses still roughly match revenue
  4. 4. Compute suppliers and capex — Nvidia's data-centre sales and big-tech capex grew in step, and capex per dollar of lab revenue fell from about 60 to about 13
  5. 5. Debt and financing — The build-out is increasingly paid for with bonds, new equity and off-balance-sheet vehicles, and the cost of that money is rising
  6. 6. Exits — 2026 reopened the exit window, and the year's largest IPO and largest startup acquisition were both AI deals
