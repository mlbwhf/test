At a glance
  • US private AI investment reached $285.9 billion in 2025, more than 23 times China's $12.4 billion and up from $109.1 billion in 2024, according to Stanford HAI's 2026 AI Index.
  • The United States received 78% of the AI compute added worldwide in the first half of 2026 and 72.2% of all AI compute added since 2022, against 9.6% for China and 5.1% for the EU, according to Epoch AI.
  • The top US model led the top Chinese model by 2.7% on the LMArena leaderboard in March 2026, down from 9.26% in January 2024, according to Stanford HAI's AI Index reports.
  • Microsoft estimated that 18.8% of the world's working-age population used generative AI in June 2026, from 73.3% in the UAE to 33.0% in the United States, which ranked 21st.
  • China had more than 700 million generative AI users in June 2026, over half its population, up from 249 million in December 2024, according to CNNIC.
  • European AI start-ups raised $23 billion in the first half of 2026, more than the $17.5 billion they raised in all of 2025, according to Crunchbase data.
  • H200 sales to Chinese buyers were under 1% of Nvidia's data-centre revenue in the quarter to 26 July 2026, despite a January 2026 US rule allowing licensed exports, according to Bloomberg.
