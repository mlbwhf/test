2. Platforms

Gemini and Meta AI reached ChatGPT's order of magnitude through products people already used, on monthly measures that cannot be ranked against weekly ones

Two other assistants now report audiences in the hundreds of millions to a billion, but on a different measure. Alphabet said the Gemini app had 750 million monthly users at the end of 2025 and more than 1 billion on 28 October 2026, up from 350 million in March 2025 according to a slide shown at the US antitrust remedies trial. Meta said Meta AI had about 500 million monthly users in September 2024 and about 1 billion in May 2025. Both are monthly counts, which are always larger than weekly counts for the same product. Both products were also switched on inside services that already had billions of accounts: Android, Google Search and Workspace for Gemini; WhatsApp, Instagram and Facebook for Meta AI. Their figures therefore measure reach as much as deliberate choice, and should be read that way.

Placed like for like, the 2026 picture is narrower than the headlines suggest (Exhibit 3). On monthly users, the measure most products report, ChatGPT's app was reported at about 1 billion in mid-2026 and the Gemini app at more than 1 billion in October 2026. China's largest assistant, Doubao, had 419 million in September 2026 according to QuestMobile, Alibaba's Qwen 158 million and DeepSeek 122.65 million. These are the only 2026 monthly figures with a named source and a date. Meta has given no Meta AI total since May 2025, and the two figures on Report AI's own pages, 1.2 billion and about 600 million, disagree. Microsoft reported 150 million monthly users across its Copilot apps for the quarter to September 2025, then switched to relative growth rates in 2026.

Anthropic's Claude is the clearest case of a product whose user count cannot be stated. Anthropic does not publish one. Third-party estimates for the same months of 2026 range from 56 million monthly app users to about 245 million monthly users across web and app, a spread of 4.4 times, and a further estimate of 271 million also circulates. Anthropic's S-1 filing of 26 October 2026 reports business metrics, including more than 11,900 customers paying at least $100,000 a year, rather than consumer users. The honest statement is a range with its methods attached. The same caution applies, less dramatically, to every figure in this chapter: even the company disclosures are self-defined and unaudited, and companies choose when, and whether, to announce them.

Microsoft's numbers show the other route to scale: selling seats to employers. Microsoft 365 Copilot had 15 million paid seats in the quarter to December 2025 and more than 30 million by July 2026, according to Microsoft's results as reported by CIO Dive and UC Today. OpenAI reported more than 1 million business customers in November 2025 and more than 2 million by the end of July 2026. These are counts of seats and organisations, not people, and they should never be added to consumer audiences. What they show is that the consumer race and the workplace race are being run with different products and different metrics. Most published league tables mix the two, along with downloads and web visits, which is the main reason they disagree with each other by factors of two to four.

How we count users

A weekly active user has used a product at least once in seven days; a monthly active user at least once in 30 days. The monthly count for the same product is always higher, often much higher, so weekly and monthly figures are never placed in one column here. Company disclosures from OpenAI, Alphabet, Meta and Microsoft are self-defined and unaudited, and companies choose when to announce. Third-party panels such as QuestMobile, Sensor Tower and Similarweb infer use from a sample of devices or browsers and carry an error band. Downloads, registered accounts and web visits are not users at all. Where credible sources disagree by a wide margin, we publish the range and mark it CONTESTED rather than choose a number.
