Outlook

In 2027 the question is depth, not breadth: watch routine use at work, paid conversion and whether ChatGPT's growth resumes

The breadth phase of AI adoption is largely over in the places with good data. Nine in ten organisations report using AI, about half of US workers and three in five US adults of working age use it at least occasionally, and the largest assistants have reached a billion people a week or a month on their own measures. The measures that will move in 2027 are measures of depth: how often people use AI at work, whether a larger share pay for it, and whether organisations can show results. On current evidence each of those is moving slowly. Frequent use at work stood at 30% of US employees in May 2026, ChatGPT's paid conversion near 5%, and EBIT impact was reported by 37% of organisations.

The watch list below sets out the indicators Report AI will track, with the levels that would change this report's conclusions. Two disclosures would matter most. A first official consumer user count from Anthropic, or a renewed Meta AI figure, would allow a genuine cross-platform ranking for the first time. And a repeat of OpenAI's NBER usage study for 2026 would show whether the shift to personal use has continued, reversed, or been overtaken by agent-style work use that the June 2025 data predates. Without those disclosures, the 2027 edition of this report will still be ranking reach rather than use, and the honest answer to the question of which AI is most used will remain a set of ranges.

Definitions
Weekly active users (WAU)
People who used a product at least once in the previous seven days, as defined by the company reporting it. Always smaller than the monthly count for the same product.
Monthly active users (MAU)
People who used a product at least once in the previous 30 days (or calendar month). Not comparable with weekly figures.
Deduplicated
Counting each person once even if they use several products. QuestMobile's national AI-native app total is deduplicated; its per-app figures are not, so they cannot be summed.
Device-panel estimate
A usage figure inferred by a third party (such as QuestMobile, Sensor Tower or Similarweb) from a sample of devices or browsers and extrapolated; it carries an error band and is not a company disclosure.
AI-native app
QuestMobile's category for standalone AI apps such as Doubao, Qwen and DeepSeek, as distinct from AI features inside existing apps or assistants built into phones.
Regular use (McKinsey)
An organisation counts as using AI if respondents say it regularly uses AI in at least one business function.
Frequent use (Gallup)
Employees who say they use AI in their role a few times a week or more; 'any use' means at least a few times a year.
AI Overview
The AI-generated summary Google shows above some search results. Its user count measures exposure inside Google Search, not chosen use of an assistant.
AI referral traffic
Visits to websites that arrive from links in AI assistants or AI search, measured as a share of all website traffic by panels such as SE Ranking and Similarweb.
Paid conversion
Paying subscribers divided by active users at the nearest date; a Report AI DERIVED ratio, approximate where subscriber scope differs.
