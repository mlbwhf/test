1. Consumers

ChatGPT passed 1 billion weekly users in 2026, but its growth slowed from about 50 million a month in 2025 to under 20 million

ChatGPT remains the only AI assistant with a company-disclosed weekly user count, and the series is unusually complete. OpenAI reported 200 million weekly active users in August 2024, 300 million in December 2024, 400 million in February 2025, about 700 million in July 2025, 800 million in October 2025, more than 900 million on 27 February 2026 and more than 1 billion on 31 August 2026. That is a fivefold increase in exactly two years. The weekly measure matters: it counts people who came back within seven days, a stricter test of habit than the monthly figures most rivals publish, and it is the only audience figure in this report that the company itself has defined and repeated over time. Exhibit 1 plots every disclosed reading; no points are interpolated, and the 2026 milestone date should be read with the caveat in the exhibit notes.

The pace, however, has changed. Between February and October 2025 ChatGPT added about 400 million weekly users in eight months, or roughly 50 million a month. Between October 2025 and February 2026 it added about 100 million in four months, and between February and August 2026 another 100 million in six months, about 17 million a month, or 11% growth over the half-year (all DERIVED from OpenAI's milestones). Three explanations fit the data and cannot yet be separated. The easiest users to reach may already be counted; Google, Meta and Chinese apps are now absorbing first-time users through products people already have; and OpenAI announces round numbers when it crosses them, so the true curve between milestones is unknown. A further disclosure is needed before calling a plateau.

Paying users have grown at almost exactly the same rate as weekly users. ChatGPT had about 15.5 million paying subscribers at the end of 2024, according to figures OpenAI gave shareholders as reported by The Information; about 35 million Plus and Pro subscribers in July 2025; and more than 50 million paying subscribers in February 2026, according to OpenAI. Set against the weekly user count at the nearest date, that is 5.2%, 5.0% and 5.6% (DERIVED). Conversion has stayed at roughly one paying user in twenty while the base has more than tripled. Subscription scope differs between the three readings, and the July 2025 figure covers Plus and Pro only, so the ratio is approximate (Exhibit 2). No reading suggests conversion is rising or falling sharply, which means revenue from consumers has tracked audience growth rather than outrun it.

Two widely circulated figures should not be read as outcomes. A total of 122 million paying ChatGPT subscribers in 2026 matches an OpenAI internal forecast for consumer subscribers driven by the $8-a-month Go tier; it has not been disclosed as a result. OpenAI's figure of 1.2 billion weekly users, given at its developer conference on 29 September 2026, may cover OpenAI products beyond ChatGPT, and its date is disputed between 29 September and 3 November. Neither is used in this report. A third figure, about 1 billion monthly app users reported in mid-2026, is genuine but measures something different and appears only in the monthly comparison in Chapter 2. The disclosed picture is enough on its own: by August 2026 ChatGPT had passed the 1 billion weekly-user mark with a paying base of at least 50 million behind it.
