At a glance
  • OpenAI said ChatGPT had more than 1 billion weekly active users on 31 August 2026, five times the 200 million it reported in August 2024.
  • Alphabet said the Gemini app had more than 1 billion monthly users on 28 October 2026; that is a monthly count and cannot be ranked against ChatGPT's weekly figure.
  • QuestMobile counted 546 million monthly users of China's AI-native apps in September 2026, up 82.1% year on year, with ByteDance's Doubao at 419 million.
  • McKinsey's 2026 State of AI survey found that 89% of organisations regularly use AI in at least one business function, but only 37% report any EBIT impact from it.
  • Gallup found that 52% of US employees used AI at work at least a few times a year in May 2026, while 30% used it a few times a week or more and 15% used it daily.
  • OpenAI's NBER study of 1.5 million conversations found that 73% of ChatGPT consumer messages were unrelated to work in June 2025, up from 53% in June 2024.
  • AI platforms accounted for about 0.32% of all website traffic in 2026, up from 0.02% in 2024, according to SE Ranking.
