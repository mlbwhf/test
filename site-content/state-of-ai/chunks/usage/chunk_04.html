3. China

China's AI-native apps reached 546 million monthly users in 2026, in a market measured by panels, led by Doubao and impossible to sum

China runs a separate consumer AI market, closed to the leading US assistants and measured almost entirely by one third-party tracker. QuestMobile counted 546 million monthly users of AI-native apps in September 2026, up 82.1% year on year, after 440 million in the first quarter, 461 million in April and 499 million in May (Exhibit 4). The national figure is deduplicated: a person who uses three assistants is counted once. QuestMobile tracks two other categories separately, AI features inside existing apps and assistants built into phones, whose audiences are larger still and overlap with the AI-native count, so the three cannot be added together. Unlike the US figures, none of these numbers comes from the companies themselves. They are device-panel estimates with a known error band, which is why this report rates them MEDIUM.

ByteDance's Doubao has pulled clear of the field. It had about 56 million monthly users in November 2024, according to QuestMobile figures cited in secondary reports, 226 million in December 2025 and 419.08 million in September 2026, with 181 million daily users on 30 September 2026. That is roughly a 7.5-fold rise in under two years (DERIVED). Doubao's advantage is distribution as much as product: it sits inside ByteDance's Douyin ecosystem, much as Meta AI sits inside WhatsApp. Alibaba's Qwen climbed fastest in 2026, from about 25 million monthly users in December 2025 to 167 million in May 2026, before easing to 158 million in September (Exhibit 5). By September 2026 Doubao's monthly audience was equivalent to 77% of the deduplicated national total (DERIVED: 419 / 546), though the two are not strictly a share because of overlap.

DeepSeek shows that a burst of attention does not guarantee retention. Its consumer app, launched on 11 January 2025, took about 14 days to reach 1 million users and then reportedly added around 100 million in a week at the end of January 2025. By December 2025 QuestMobile put it at 135 million monthly users, about 130 million in May 2026 and 122.65 million in September 2026. It is the only major assistant in this report whose tracked audience fell during 2026. DeepSeek's models remain widely used through APIs and open weights outside China, which an app panel does not capture, so the app figure understates DeepSeek's overall reach. It does not, however, contradict the decline of DeepSeek's own app as a consumer destination inside China.

The per-app figures cannot be summed. In September 2026 Doubao, Qwen and DeepSeek alone total 699.73 million monthly users, against a deduplicated national total of 546 million (DERIVED), so at least 154 million people are counted more than once. Any chart that adds Chinese app audiences to make a market total double-counts by a quarter or more. Nor should China's figures be stacked against US ones. ChatGPT's weekly count is a company disclosure; QuestMobile's monthly counts are panel estimates; and nobody deduplicates across the two markets, so a person using ChatGPT at work and Doubao at home is two users in every comparison published. The defensible statements are within-market: Doubao leads China, the market is still growing fast, and DeepSeek's app has lost ground.

The 3.8 billion mistake

In 2026 at least one English-language headline reported that Doubao had 3.8 billion users. The Chinese source said 3.8 yi; a yi is 100 million, so the figure was about 380 million. The tenfold error is recorded in Report AI's fact-check register. Chinese large numbers are written in units of 10,000 (wan) and 100 million (yi), and any figure that converts to more than a few hundred million users for a single Chinese app should be checked against the unit before it is repeated. The latest QuestMobile figure for Doubao is 419 million monthly users, in September 2026.
