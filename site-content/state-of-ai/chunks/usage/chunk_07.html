Comparison

Between 2024 and 2026 almost every usage measure rose, but the leaders by habit, by reach and by organisational adoption are different products measured on different populations

Across the three years, every series in this report rose except one. ChatGPT's weekly users went from 300 million in December 2024 to 800 million in October 2025 and more than 1 billion in August 2026. Organisational adoption in McKinsey's surveys went from 78% to 88% to 89%. Gallup's measure of US employees using AI at work went from 27% to 46% to 52%, and the St. Louis Fed's measure of adults aged 18 to 64 using generative AI went from 44.6% to 54.6% to 62%. AI's share of website traffic went from 0.02% to 0.24% to 0.32%. The exception is DeepSeek's Chinese app audience, which fell from 135 million monthly users in December 2025 to 122.65 million in September 2026, according to QuestMobile.

The shape of growth changed from year to year. 2024 was the year of breadth inside organisations: McKinsey's adoption measure jumped from 55% in 2023 to 78%. 2025 was the year of consumer scale: ChatGPT's weekly users doubled in eight months and the personal share of its use rose to 73%. 2026 so far is a year of convergence and deceleration: ChatGPT's growth slowed to about 11% in six months, organisational adoption barely moved, and rivals, Gemini outside China and Doubao and Qwen inside it, reached comparable monthly scale. Worker use rose steadily throughout, by roughly 12 percentage points a year on Gallup's any-use measure (DERIVED: 27% in Q2 2024 to 46% in Q4 2025, then 52% in May 2026).

Across platforms, Exhibit 9 sets out every disclosed figure by metric, so that only like is compared with like. On weekly users only ChatGPT reports, so it has no comparator. On monthly users, ChatGPT's app and the Gemini app were both at about 1 billion in 2026, Doubao at 419 million and DeepSeek at 122.65 million, with Meta AI and Claude unresolved. The table also shows how thin the public record is: 10 of its 24 platform-year cells are empty or contested. That is itself a finding. Outside OpenAI's weekly series, Alphabet's and Meta's occasional monthly statements and QuestMobile's panel, the AI user numbers in wide circulation are not reliable enough to rank, and Exhibit 10 shows how unevenly the milestones were disclosed.
