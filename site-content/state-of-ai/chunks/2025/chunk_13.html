Tokens

In 2025 token volumes rose by one to two orders of magnitude, and longer prompts and reasoning made the average request more expensive despite cheaper tokens

  1. Google said in October 2025 that it was processing more than 1.3 quadrillion tokens a month across its products, up from 480 trillion in May 2025.
  2. OpenAI said on 6 October 2025 that its API was processing more than 6 billion tokens a minute, up from about 300 million in 2023.
  3. DeepSeek's September 2025 list price of $0.28 per million input tokens and $0.42 per million output tokens bought about 3.2 million tokens of GPT-4-level output per dollar on a 3:1 blend, against about 27,000 for GPT-4 in March 2023 (Report AI calculation).
  4. Menlo Ventures estimated enterprise generative-AI spending at $37 billion in 2025, 3.2 times the 2024 figure; its November 2026 report restates 2025 as $33 billion.
  5. MIT's Project NANDA reported in August 2025 that 95% of the enterprise generative-AI pilots it studied showed no measurable profit-and-loss impact, while Wharton's October 2025 survey found 74% of US enterprise leaders reporting positive ROI.

In 2025 the number of tokens per task, not the price per token, drove costs. Epoch AI measured the price of a fixed capability falling by a median of about 50 times a year, and DeepSeek's September price cut put GPT-4-level output at about 3.2 million tokens per dollar. OpenAI's GPT-5 bought about 291,000 tokens per dollar at list price, and Anthropic's Opus 4.5 cut the Opus tier's price to $5 and $25 in November. But reasoning models generated long hidden chains of output, and prompts grew: on OpenRouter the average prompt rose from about 1,500 to 6,000 tokens. At flagship list prices the average request cost about $0.0115 in November 2025, more than double a year earlier. Artificial Analysis spent about $2,767 running OpenAI's o1 through a seven-benchmark suite against $109 for GPT-4o, an early sign that reasoning would raise the cost of a task.

Disclosed volumes multiplied. Google processed 480 trillion tokens a month in May and 1.3 quadrillion in October. OpenAI said in October that its API handled more than 6 billion tokens a minute, about 8.6 trillion a day. Microsoft processed more than 100 trillion tokens in the first quarter, and over 500 trillion through Foundry in its fiscal year to June. Doubao reached about 50 trillion tokens a day in December, and China's national figure reached about 100 trillion a day by year end, 21.1 quadrillion for the year. OpenRouter passed 1 trillion tokens a day, and agents grew to about 8% of its traffic by October. Only Google and ByteDance published comparable all-product figures; together they reached about 2.8 quadrillion tokens a month late in the year, roughly twenty times the equivalent 2024 figure.

Revenue rose far more slowly than volume: combined OpenAI and Anthropic run-rate revenue reached about $29 billion by year end, about 4.5 times a year earlier. Menlo Ventures estimated enterprise generative-AI spending at $37 billion, later restated to $33 billion, and model-API spending at $8.4 billion a year by mid-2025. Customers spent about $1.00 per million tokens routed through OpenRouter, and ByteDance's Volcano Engine earned roughly $0.024 per million tokens on our estimate. The evidence on returns divided sharply. MIT's NANDA study found 95% of pilots had no measurable profit-and-loss impact within about six months, and IBM found 25% of AI initiatives met expected ROI, while Wharton found 74% of leaders reporting a positive return and Snowflake reported $1.41 back per dollar. These studies asked different questions of different samples, so their results do not cancel out, but together they show that the return on all this activity was not settled.
