Models & Prices

In 2025 reasoning models turned 2024's previews into products, open weights matched the frontier for a moment, and training costs reached an estimated half-billion dollars.

  1. Anthropic reported that Claude Opus 4.5, released on 24 November 2025, scored 80.9% on SWE-bench Verified, the first model reported above 80%.
  2. Google DeepMind's Gemini Deep Think scored 35 of 42 points at the July 2025 International Mathematical Olympiad, the first AI result officially certified at gold-medal standard.
  3. Epoch AI estimated that training xAI's Grok 4, released in July 2025, used about 5×10^26 FLOP and cost about $490 million, the largest known training run.
  4. DeepSeek priced its open-weight R1 reasoning model at $0.55 per million input tokens and $2.19 per million output tokens on 20 January 2025, against $15 and $60 for OpenAI's o1.
  5. Stanford HAI's 2026 AI Index reports that the median price per million tokens across AI models fell from $2.10 in 2024 to $0.85 in 2025.

The year began with DeepSeek-R1 on 20 January, an open-weight reasoning model priced at $0.55 and $2.19 per million input and output tokens, about 96% below o1. By February the top US model's Arena lead over China had fallen to 1.70%, and the closed-weight lead over open weights was also 1.70%. Humanity's Last Exam launched in January with the best models near 9%; OpenAI's Deep Research, using web tools, reached 26.6% in February. ARC-AGI-2 launched in March to replace a test that was close to solved. Releases quickened, with Gemini 2.5 Pro, o3, Llama 4, Claude 4, Grok 4 and, on 7 August, GPT-5 at $1.25 and $10.

Reasoning results moved from previews to certified results. In July Gemini Deep Think scored 35 of 42 at the International Mathematical Olympiad, the first AI result officially certified at gold-medal standard; OpenAI reported the same score for an experimental model, self-graded. In November Gemini 3 Pro and Claude Opus 4.5 arrived within a week of each other, and Opus 4.5 became the first model reported above 80% on SWE-bench Verified, at 80.9%, while cutting Anthropic's flagship price to $5 and $25. By December Gemini 3 Deep Think reported 93.8% on GPQA Diamond and 41.0% on Humanity's Last Exam without tools, and Poetiq's system reached a verified 54% on ARC-AGI-2.

Behind the releases, the cost of training climbed. Epoch AI estimated that Grok 4 used about 5×10^26 FLOP at a median cost of about $490 million, the largest known run, after Grok 3 became the first model above 10^26 FLOP in February. Stanford HAI's 2026 report found the median price per million tokens across models fell from $2.10 in 2024 to $0.85 in 2025. The US produced 50 notable models to China's 30, by the AI Index's chapter count, with a later Stanford page giving 59 and 35. Google reported that a median Gemini text prompt used 0.24 watt-hours.
