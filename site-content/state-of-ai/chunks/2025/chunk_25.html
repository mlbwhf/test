Frequently asked

How many weekly users did ChatGPT have in 2025?

OpenAI said ChatGPT reached 800 million weekly active users in October 2025, double the 400 million it reported in February 2025.

How much did big tech spend on capital expenditure in 2025?

Microsoft, Alphabet, Amazon and Meta spent about $413 billion on capital expenditure in calendar 2025, roughly 69% more than the $245 billion of 2024, by Report AI's sum of their reported results.

How much venture funding went to AI in 2025?

Crunchbase counted $202.3 billion of venture funding to AI companies in 2025, close to half of the $425 billion invested globally. PitchBook put AI's share at 53% and the OECD, using a broader definition, at 61%.

How many US jobs were cut because of AI in 2025?

Challenger, Gray & Christmas counted 54,836 US job cuts attributed to AI in 2025, 4.5% of the 1,206,374 cuts announced that year and more than four times the 2024 count of 12,742.

What AI rules applied in 2025?

The EU AI Act's bans on prohibited practices applied from 2 February 2025 and its general-purpose AI obligations from 2 August 2025. In the US, eight AI-focused executive orders were signed, by Report AI's count, including EO 14365 of 11 December 2025, which ordered a Justice Department task force to challenge state AI laws.

Methodology & sources

Figures are compiled from company disclosures, regulators, research firms and reputable reporting, and cross-checked against Report AI's own index pages. Each value carries its as-of date, source and a confidence rating: HIGH where the primary source was read, MEDIUM where it rests on consistent reputable secondary reporting (most figures, because many primary pages cannot be opened from our research environment), DERIVED where it is our own arithmetic, and CONTESTED where credible sources disagree. A year means the calendar year the figure describes; 2026 values are the latest available and marked “to date”. Weekly and monthly user counts, and forecasts and outcomes, are never compared as if they were the same measure. These reports compile and verify secondary data with original analysis; they are not original surveys. See our editorial standards, corrections log and fact-check register.
