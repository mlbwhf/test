2024 in eleven numbers
  1. OpenAI said ChatGPT had 300 million weekly active users in December 2024, up from 200 million in August 2024.
  2. Gartner estimated worldwide AI spending at about $988 billion in 2024, in the 2024 baseline of its September 2025 forecast, before it widened its AI categories in 2026.
  3. Crunchbase counted more than $100 billion of venture funding to AI companies in 2024, about a third of the roughly $314 billion invested in startups worldwide that year.
  4. Challenger, Gray & Christmas counted 12,742 US job cuts in 2024 that employers attributed to artificial intelligence, about 1.7% of the 761,358 cuts announced that year.
  5. Stanford HAI's AI Index 2025 found that the inference price of GPT-3.5-level performance fell from $20 per million tokens in November 2022 to $0.07 in October 2024, a more than 280-fold decline.
  6. Google processed about 9.7 trillion tokens a month across its products in spring 2024, the baseline Sundar Pichai cited at Google I/O in May 2025.
  7. Nvidia reported data-centre revenue of $115.2 billion for its fiscal year ended January 2025, which approximates calendar 2024, up 142% on the prior year.
  8. Stanford HAI's 2025 AI Index counted $109.1 billion of US private AI investment in 2024, nearly 12 times China's $9.3 billion and 24 times the UK's $4.5 billion.
  9. The FDA's AI-Enabled Medical Device List counted 1,016 authorized AI devices in its 20 December 2024 update, and the September 2026 snapshot of the list attributes 235 authorizations to calendar year 2024.
  10. Hong Kong police said in February 2024 that a finance employee at engineering firm Arup transferred about US$25 million (HK$200 million) after a video call in which every other participant was an AI deepfake, the largest publicly reported deepfake fraud until September 2026.
  11. The EU AI Act (Regulation 2024/1689) entered into force on 1 August 2024, with fines of up to €35 million or 7% of worldwide annual turnover for prohibited practices under Article 99.
