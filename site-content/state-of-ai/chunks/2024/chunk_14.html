Geography

In 2024 the US held a 12-to-1 lead over China in private AI investment while China cut the model gap to single digits

  1. Stanford HAI's 2025 AI Index counted $109.1 billion of US private AI investment in 2024, nearly 12 times China's $9.3 billion and 24 times the UK's $4.5 billion.
  2. US institutions released 40 notable AI models in 2024, against 15 from China and 3 from Europe, according to Stanford HAI's 2025 AI Index.
  3. In January 2024 the top US model led the top Chinese model by 9.26% on the Chatbot Arena leaderboard, according to Stanford HAI's 2025 AI Index.
  4. China had 249 million generative AI users at the end of December 2024, according to the China Internet Network Information Center (CNNIC).
  5. China was granted 74.2% of the world's AI patents in 2024, 97,206 of 131,121, against 12.1% for the United States, according to Stanford HAI's 2026 AI Index.

Stanford HAI's 2025 AI Index recorded $109.1 billion of US private AI investment in 2024, nearly 12 times China's $9.3 billion and 24 times the United Kingdom's $4.5 billion. The US figure had risen from $67.2 billion in 2023, while China's private total stayed in single digits. Chinese AI capital flowed mainly through state channels that Stanford's measure does not capture, including guidance funds that Stanford estimates had put about $184 billion into AI firms since 2000, and the ¥344 billion semiconductor fund Beijing registered in May 2024. Europe raised roughly $10-13 billion of AI venture funding, depending on the tracker, and Mistral was valued at €5.8 billion in June.

On models, US institutions released 40 notable AI models in 2024, China 15 and Europe 3. The distance between the countries' best systems was shrinking: at the start of the year the top US model led the top Chinese model by 9.26% on the Chatbot Arena leaderboard. China was granted 74.2% of the world's AI patents that year, against 12.1% for the United States, though US research remained more cited. Around 60% of the AI compute added worldwide in 2024 went to the United States, according to Epoch AI, continuing a pattern that had held since 2022.

Governments began funding AI directly. India approved its ₹10,372 crore IndiaAI Mission in March 2024, Abu Dhabi created the investment vehicle MGX, and Japan subsidised domestic cloud compute. Nvidia's revenue from sovereign customers in its fiscal 2025, close to calendar 2024, was not disclosed but was later implied to be under about $10 billion. Consumer adoption in China was at an early stage: CNNIC counted 249 million generative AI users at the end of 2024, and 302 generative AI services had completed filing with the Cyberspace Administration. By billing location, China supplied 13% of Nvidia's revenue that fiscal year.
