Industries

  • Paid business adoption on Ramp rose from 36.4% (Nov 2024) to 46.6% (Dec 2025) to 55.9% (Sep 2026); finance and insurance moved from 51.2% to 58.1% to 73.7% (Aug 2026), the steepest climb among large sectors.
  • FDA AI device authorizations went from 235 (2024) to a record 335 (2025, contested range 295-335) to roughly 167-178 in January-June 2026; the cumulative list grew from 1,016 to 1,614 entries between December 2024 and September 2026.
  • Practitioner adoption converged at a high level: physicians from 66% (Nov 2024) to 81% (early 2026), UK undergraduates from 66% to 92% to 95% across three academic years, legal professionals from 79% to 79% to 85% (Clio).
  • The measures themselves changed: Census BTOS switched questions in November 2025, Pew widened its question from ChatGPT to all chatbots, Ramp expanded its sample in June 2026, and Gartner restated 2025 spending from $1.48T to about $1.79T, so several apparent jumps are partly definitional.
