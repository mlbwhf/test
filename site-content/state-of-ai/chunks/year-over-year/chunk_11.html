Risk

  • The record deepfake-style fraud loss stood at about $25 million (Arup) at the end of 2024 and 2025, then rose to €95 million (~$108 million, Fideuram) in September 2026; each record case used less synthetic media than the last.
  • Chatbot reliability on news worsened and partly recovered: NewsGuard measured false-claim repetition at 18% (August 2024), 35% (August 2025) and 28.8% (January 2026).
  • Official measurement arrived only in the middle year: the FBI had no AI category for 2024, counted 22,364 AI-related complaints and $893 million in losses for 2025, and will not report 2026 until April 2027 — so 2026 comparisons rely on vendor, watchdog and case data.
  • Chatbot-harm litigation against OpenAI went from no ChatGPT injury or death suits at the end of 2024 to eight filings in 2025 and roughly two dozen coordinated cases by August 2026, alongside the first state attorney-general suit naming a CEO personally.
