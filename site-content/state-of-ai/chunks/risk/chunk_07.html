Comparison

Between 2024 and 2026 AI risk shifted from cheap imitation at the margins to capable misuse of mainstream products, while official measurement covered only the middle year.

Set side by side, the three years show a change in where harm originates more than in its volume. In 2024 the defining cases were a deepfake video call, a cloned robocall and fringe nudify sites. In 2025 the measures rose together — incidents up 55%, chatbot false-claim rates nearly doubled, AI child-abuse videos up from 13 to 3,443 — and AI began to operate an intrusion rather than assist it. In 2026 the largest events came from mainstream products and frontier labs: Grok's image-abuse episode, OpenAI's agents breaching Hugging Face, and two dozen cases over ChatGPT. The record fraud needed less synthetic media, not more.

The comparison is limited by uneven measurement. Only 2025 has an official US count of AI-linked fraud, because the FBI had no AI category for 2024 and will not report 2026 until April 2027. The incident series has no 2026 value yet. Several 2026 figures are half-year or to-date readings and are labelled as such in Exhibit 9. Where a series changed method — NewsGuard's 2026 detection, KnowBe4's restated phishing shares, the deepfake trackers — we show the values but do not compute growth. Comparing a forecast with these outcomes, as is often done with Deloitte's $40 billion, is not meaningful until that forecast's year arrives.

Across platforms, the pattern is that harm concentrates where scale meets weak verification. Deezer's AI tracks are more than half of uploads but 1–3% of streams; AI content farms multiply but compete for little attention; the costliest frauds and the largest abuse episode exploited channels or products with hundreds of millions of users. The firms that build frontier models also remain the main source of evidence about their own misuse: both documented agent intrusions were disclosed by developers, and the Future of Life Institute's best overall safety grade for any major developer rose only from C in December 2024 to C+ in 2025 and 2026.
