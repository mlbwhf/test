2. Releases

Frontier releases accelerated to one significant model every two to three weeks in 2026, and for the first time a release was suspended by government order.

The pace of frontier releases quickened across the three years. In 2024 the notable releases arrived about every six weeks: Claude 3 in March, GPT-4o in May, Claude 3.5 Sonnet in June, Llama 3.1 405B in July, o1 in preview in September and in full in December, and DeepSeek-V3 on 26 December. In 2025 the list lengthened and was led by reasoning models: DeepSeek-R1 on 20 January, Gemini 2.5 Pro in March, o3 and Llama 4 in April, Claude 4 in May, Grok 4 in July, GPT-5 on 7 August, and Gemini 3 Pro and Claude Opus 4.5 within a week of each other in November. In the ten months of 2026 to 8 November we count at least sixteen significant releases from eight labs, from Gemini 3.1 Pro in February to Mistral Large 4's open weights on 29 October.

Release dates have also become less reliable as a marker of availability. OpenAI's o3 was demonstrated in December 2024 and released in April 2025, so its record scores described a preview, not a product. Anthropic released Claude Fable 5 and Claude Mythos 5 on 9 June 2026 and disabled both for every customer three days later, after the US Commerce Department placed them under export controls; both returned on 1 July, Mythos 5 only for US organisations. Google announced Gemini 4 Argon on 30 September 2026 for a limited cyber-defence programme and made it generally available on 13 October. In August, OpenAI paused its largest planned reinforcement-learning run after evaluations could not rule out an unreleased model reaching the critical cyber-risk level in its own Preparedness Framework.
