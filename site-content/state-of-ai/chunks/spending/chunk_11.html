Frequently asked

How much will the world spend on AI in 2026?

Gartner forecast on 16 September 2026 that worldwide AI spending will reach about $2.67 trillion in 2026, up 49.5% from its estimate of about $1.79 trillion for 2025. About $1.48 trillion of the 2026 figure, roughly 56%, is AI infrastructure. It is a forecast that Gartner has raised twice this year, from $2.52 trillion in January and $2.59 trillion in May. IDC's narrower measure, AI infrastructure hardware only, is forecast at $521 billion for 2026 (6 November 2026).

How much are Microsoft, Alphabet, Amazon and Meta spending on capex in 2026?

They spent about $508 billion on capital expenditure from January to September 2026, by Report AI's sum of each company's quarterly results: Amazon $172.2 billion, Alphabet $132.0 billion, Microsoft $118.1 billion and Meta about $85.8 billion. Their full-year 2026 guidance, given in late October 2026, adds up to roughly $720–740 billion: Amazon about $220 billion, Alphabet $195–205 billion, Microsoft about $170 billion (calendar or fiscal year is disputed) and Meta $135–145 billion. The companies report on slightly different bases, so totals are approximate.

How much do enterprises spend on generative AI?

Menlo Ventures estimated that US enterprises spent $37 billion on generative AI in 2025, 3.2 times the $11.5 billion it now estimates for 2024. Its November 2026 report uses a different measure: enterprise AI deployment spending running at an annualised $176.8 billion in September 2026, up from a restated $33 billion a year earlier. That is a run-rate on a new definition, not money spent in 2026, so it should not be compared directly with the $37 billion.

How much is being spent on AI agents in 2026?

It depends on the definition. Gartner forecasts $29.2 billion of spending on standalone AI agents and assistants in 2026 (16 September 2026 forecast), up from $16.5 billion in 2025. Its better-known $206.5 billion figure for 2026 (5 May 2026) counts all spending on software that has agentic features built in, including existing enterprise applications. Research firms that size a standalone agents market put it at about $7–8 billion in 2025 (Report AI comparison, November 2026).

How much do governments spend on AI?

Far less than companies, and much of it is one-off. The US requested $3.3 billion of non-defence federal AI R&D for fiscal 2025, and Brookings counted $7.2 billion of federal AI contract obligations so far in 2026 (May 2026), almost all for defence. The EU's verifiable public commitment is the >€2.7 billion EuroHPC AI Factories programme, not the €200 billion InvestAI headline. The UK launched a £500 million Sovereign AI Fund in April 2026, topped up by £60 million in October, and Abu Dhabi's MGX closed a $49 billion fund in July 2026.

Methodology & sources

Figures are compiled from company disclosures, regulators, research firms and reputable reporting, and cross-checked against Report AI's own index pages. Each value carries its as-of date, source and a confidence rating: HIGH where the primary source was read, MEDIUM where it rests on consistent reputable secondary reporting (most figures, because many primary pages cannot be opened from our research environment), DERIVED where it is our own arithmetic, and CONTESTED where credible sources disagree. A year means the calendar year the figure describes; 2026 values are the latest available and marked “to date”. Weekly and monthly user counts, and forecasts and outcomes, are never compared as if they were the same measure. Exhibits plot only dated, sourced readings; lines are not interpolated between them, and values with different definitions are never placed on one axis.
