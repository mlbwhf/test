Numbers to stop quoting

Widely repeatedWhat the evidence supports
“Gartner: worldwide AI spending will be $2.59 trillion in 2026 (or $2.5 trillion).”Superseded. Gartner's 16 September 2026 forecast is about $2.67 trillion (rounded to $2.7 trillion), up 49.5%. The $2.52 trillion (15 Jan 2026) and $2.59 trillion (19 May 2026) figures were earlier editions. · detail
“$2.59 trillion in 2026 is up 47% from about $1.5 trillion in 2025.”The arithmetic does not work: $1.5 trillion plus 47% is about $2.2 trillion. Gartner had restated its 2025 base to about $1.76 trillion by January 2026; its September 2026 table gives $1.79 trillion. The $1.5 trillion was its September 2025 forecast for 2025. · detail
“IDC forecasts $487 billion (or $497 billion) of AI infrastructure spending in 2026.”Superseded. IDC raised its 2026 forecast to $521 billion on 6 November 2026, after $487 billion in April and $497 billion in July. · detail
“Hyperscaler capex will top $600 billion in 2026.”Out of date. Microsoft, Alphabet, Amazon and Meta alone had spent about $508 billion by September 2026, and their full-year guidance sums to roughly $720–740 billion (Report AI sum, October 2026). · detail
“The AI agents market is worth $206.5 billion in 2026 (Gartner).”Gartner's $206.5 billion counts spending on all software with agentic features built in. Its own line for standalone AI agents and assistants is $29.2 billion for 2026. · detail
“IDC: agentic AI spending will reach $1.3 trillion by 2029.”IDC's $1.3 trillion for 2029 (26 Aug 2025) is total AI spending, with agentic AI described as its main driver; it is not an agentic-AI market size. · detail
“Enterprises spent $177 billion on AI in 2026 (Menlo Ventures).”Menlo's $176.8 billion is an annualised run-rate of enterprise AI deployment spending as of September 2026, on a new definition. It is not money spent in 2026 and is not comparable with Menlo's $37 billion for 2025.

Outlook

In 2027 the question is whether spending keeps outrunning cash flow, and the first test is fourth-quarter capex

Every forecast available in November 2026 expects AI spending to rise again in 2027: Gartner projects about $3.64 trillion of worldwide AI spending, IDC expects AI infrastructure to pass $1 trillion by 2029, and Alphabet and Amazon have said their capex will increase. The risk to that view is financial rather than technical. Three of the four largest spenders had negative free cash flow in the third quarter of 2026, and the gap is being filled with bonds, new shares and off-balance-sheet structures whose cost is rising, with some data-centre debt marketed at 11% or more.

Two things would change our view. A cut to 2027 capex plans by any of the four, or a first downward revision to Gartner's or IDC's forecasts, would signal that the build-out has peaked. Conversely, enterprise spending on models and agents growing fast enough to cover the depreciation on this capex would show the spending is being earned back; on current figures, measured enterprise revenue streams are an order of magnitude smaller than capex.

Definitions
Capital expenditure (capex)
Spending on long-lived assets such as data centres, servers and land, capitalised and depreciated over years. Reported quarterly by listed companies; each company uses its own basis.
AI infrastructure (IDC)
Spending on AI-optimised servers and storage, measured by IDC's quarterly tracker from vendor revenue. Hardware only.
Worldwide AI spending (Gartner)
Gartner's total of spending on AI infrastructure, software, services, models, security and AI-enabled devices. Values for completed years are estimates that Gartner revises.
Enterprise generative-AI spending (Menlo)
Menlo Ventures' survey-based estimate of US enterprise spending on generative-AI applications and infrastructure; definition has changed between editions.
Annualised run-rate
Spending in a recent period scaled to a full year. Not the same as money spent in a calendar year.
Standalone AI agents vs agentic software
Standalone: products sold as AI agents or assistants. Agentic software: all software with agent features built in, including existing applications. Gartner's $29.2B and $206.5B for 2026 measure these two things.
Appropriation
Money a legislature or ministry has allocated for a period. Distinct from equity funds, sovereign-wealth vehicles and mobilisation headlines.
TWh
Terawatt-hour, one billion kilowatt-hours. Global electricity demand is roughly 30,000 TWh a year.
