Comparison

Between 2024 and 2026 every measured AI spending series at least doubled, but the measures moved further apart

On the measures that are counted rather than modelled, AI spending roughly doubled each year. Big-four capex went from about $245 billion in 2024 to about $413 billion in 2025, and reached about $508 billion in the first nine months of 2026. IDC's AI infrastructure series went from $153.9 billion to $318.7 billion, with $521 billion forecast for 2026. Enterprise API spending, enterprise generative-AI budgets and data-centre electricity all rose, though electricity grew far more slowly, by about 17–20% in 2025, because efficiency gains absorb part of the hardware growth.

Across companies, Amazon spent the most in each year, but Alphabet grew fastest: its January–September 2026 capex of $132.0 billion was 2.5 times its full-year 2024 figure. Microsoft's 2026 spending to September matched its whole 2025 total. Among model providers, Anthropic's share of enterprise API spending more than doubled from 2024 to 2026, while Google's rose and then fell. Meta's 2026 guidance is about twice its 2025 total, and Amazon's nine-month spending in 2026 exceeded every company's full-year figure for 2025.

The comparison also shows how unstable the measures are. Gartner's estimate for 2025 moved by more than $300 billion between editions, Menlo changed its definition twice, and IDC revised its 2026 infrastructure forecast three times. A reader comparing years should use one source and one edition at a time, and treat any cross-source growth rate as unreliable.
