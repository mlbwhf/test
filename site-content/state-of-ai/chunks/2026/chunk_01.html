2026 in seven numbers
  1. OpenAI said ChatGPT had more than 1 billion weekly active users on 31 August 2026, up from more than 900 million in February 2026.
  2. Gartner forecast on 16 September 2026 that worldwide AI spending will reach about $2.67 trillion in 2026, up 49.5% year on year and its third upward revision of 2026 this year.
  3. Crunchbase recorded $679 billion of global venture funding in the first nine months of 2026, a record for that period, with AI companies taking about 80% in the first quarter, more than 70% in the second and 64% in the third.
  4. Challenger, Gray & Christmas counted 121,401 US job cuts attributed to AI from January to October 2026, more than twice the 2025 total, but only 1,265 of them came in October, the lowest month of the year.
  5. Vals.ai's SWE-bench Verified leaderboard, updated on 1 September 2026, ranked Claude Opus 5 first at 97.0%, and Vals said the benchmark had saturated.
  6. Google said at I/O in May 2026 that it was processing more than 3.2 quadrillion tokens a month across its products, about seven times its level a year earlier.
  7. Nvidia reported data-centre revenue of $89.0 billion for the quarter ended 26 July 2026, up 117% year on year, bringing the first half of its fiscal 2027 to $164.2 billion.
  8. The top US model led the top Chinese model by 2.7% on the LMArena leaderboard in March 2026, according to Stanford HAI's 2026 AI Index, compared with 1.70% in February 2025.
  9. Ramp's AI Index showed 55.9% of US businesses on its platform paying for AI tools in September 2026; in August, technology firms were at 80.9%, finance at 73.7%, manufacturing at 61.1% and health care at 43.4%.
  10. Reuters reported on 25 September 2026 that fraudsters using a WhatsApp message and an AI-cloned voice took €95 million (about $108 million) from Fideuram, Intesa Sanpaolo's private-banking arm, the largest publicly reported deepfake-style fraud to date.
  11. The EU's AI Omnibus, Regulation (EU) 2026/1744, in force since 27 July 2026, moved the AI Act's stand-alone high-risk obligations from 2 August 2026 to 2 December 2027 and adds two Article 5 prohibitions, including 'nudifier' apps, from 2 December 2026.
