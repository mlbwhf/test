Industries

In 2026 to date a majority of firms tracked by Ramp pay for AI and practitioner use is near saturation, but outcome evidence remains limited

  1. Ramp's AI Index showed 55.9% of US businesses on its platform paying for AI tools in September 2026; in August, technology firms were at 80.9%, finance at 73.7%, manufacturing at 61.1% and health care at 43.4%.
  2. The US Census Bureau's Business Trends and Outlook Survey found 19.8% of US businesses using AI as of 3 May 2026, including 39.7% in the information sector and 33.9% in finance and insurance.
  3. The American Medical Association found that 81% of US physicians used AI in their practice in a survey of 1,692 physicians fielded from 15 January to 2 February 2026, up from 66% in November 2024.
  4. The FDA's AI-Enabled Medical Device List reached 1,614 entries in its 4 September 2026 update, covering decisions through 29 June 2026, up from 1,016 in December 2024.
  5. Clio's 2026 Legal Trends Report found that 85% of legal professionals reported some level of AI adoption at their firms, up from 79% in 2025.

In 2026 to date, paid AI adoption has passed half of the US businesses on Ramp's platform, reaching 55.9% in September. Technology firms were at about 80% and finance at 73.7% in August, while accommodation and food services were at 33.1%. The Census Bureau's representative survey found 19.8% of US businesses using AI as of 3 May 2026, with information at 39.7% and finance and insurance at 33.9%. The ranking of sectors is the same in both surveys and in Eurostat's 2025 data. McKinsey's 2026 survey found 89% of organizations using AI in at least one function and 37% reporting any EBIT impact.

Practitioner use has reached high levels in the professions. The AMA found 81% of US physicians using AI in a survey fielded in January and February 2026. HEPI found 95% of UK undergraduates using AI in the 2025-26 academic year, with 38% saying their university supplies tools. Clio found 85% of legal professionals at firms with some AI adoption. Pew found 54% of US teens had used chatbots for schoolwork, on a wider question than before, and Gallup and Walton found 59% of K-12 educators using AI in 2025-26. Stack Overflow's 2026 survey implies about 83% of developers use AI tools.

The regulated product pipeline continued at the 2025 pace. The FDA list reached 1,614 entries in its September 2026 update, with about 167 to 178 authorizations in the first half of the year, depending on the count. The clearest new outcome evidence came from a JAMA multisite study showing ambient scribes saved clinicians about 16 minutes of documentation a day. Gartner raised its 2026 forecast of worldwide AI spending to about $2.67 trillion, but no public source has updated industry-level spending beyond 2024, and Eurostat's 2026 enterprise data are due in December.
