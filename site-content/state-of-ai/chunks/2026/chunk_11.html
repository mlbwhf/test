Models & Prices

In 2026 to date the established benchmarks saturated, Chinese and open-weight models reached the top four, and frontier pricing split into a cheap base and a premium top.

  1. Vals.ai's SWE-bench Verified leaderboard, updated on 1 September 2026, ranked Claude Opus 5 first at 97.0%, and Vals said the benchmark had saturated.
  2. Stanford HAI's 2026 AI Index found that the top US model led the best Chinese model by 2.7% on the Arena leaderboard in March 2026, and the top closed-weight model led the best open-weight model by 3.3%.
  3. Silicon Data's usage-weighted LLM Token Expenditure Index fell to $0.97 per million tokens on 1 September 2026, while Anthropic's top model, Claude Fable 5.1, listed at $10 per million input tokens and $50 per million output tokens.
  4. The ARC Prize Foundation verified a score of 84.6% on ARC-AGI-2 for Google's Gemini 3 Deep Think in February 2026, up from a verified best of 54% in December 2025.
  5. Anthropic's Claude Haiku 5.5, launched on 7 October 2026, lists at $0.10 per million input tokens and $0.50 per million output tokens for prompts up to 100,000 tokens, a tenth of Claude Haiku 4.5's $1 and $5.

Through 8 November 2026 the leading benchmarks have stopped separating models. Gemini 3.1 Pro reported 94.3% on GPQA Diamond in February, Gemini 3 Deep Think reached a verified 84.6% on ARC-AGI-2 the same month, and Vals.ai's SWE-bench Verified board showed Claude Opus 5 at 97.0% on 1 September. Several systems were reported with perfect 42-point scores at the International Mathematical Olympiad in July, without official certification. Humanity's Last Exam remains the main broad test with headroom, with the top score at about 55–60% in September and October depending on the leaderboard.

The competitive field is the most crowded yet. Stanford HAI measured a 2.7% US lead over China and a 3.3% closed lead over open weights on Arena in March. By early September Alibaba's Qwen3.8-Max (58) and Moonshot's Kimi K3 (57.1) stood in the global top four on the Artificial Analysis index, behind GPT-5.6 Sol at 58.9. Releases arrived every few weeks, including DeepSeek V4-Pro, Claude Opus 4.8, GPT-5.6 Sol, Qwen3.8-27B, GPT-6 Sol, Claude Opus 5.5 and Gemini 4 Argon. Claude Fable 5 was withdrawn under US export controls within a week of its 9 June launch, and OpenAI paused a frontier training run in August.

Prices split in two. Claude Opus 5.5 lists at $4 and $20 and GPT-6 Sol at $2 and $10 per million tokens, while a premium tier sits above them at $10 and $50 for Claude Fable 5.1, and Google's Gemini 4 Argon rises to $4 and $20 on 30 November. At the base, Claude Haiku 5.5 launched on 7 October at $0.10 and $0.50, and DeepSeek V4-Pro offers near-frontier coding scores at about $0.87 per million output tokens. No 2026 frontier model has a public estimate of training compute or cost, and a 2026 Microsoft Research study put a median chatbot query at 0.31 watt-hours.
