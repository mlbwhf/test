Infrastructure & Energy

So far in 2026 chip revenue has hit records every quarter while energised capacity, grid supply and GPU rental prices show the build-out pressing against physical limits

  1. Nvidia reported data-centre revenue of $89.0 billion for the quarter ended 26 July 2026, up 117% year on year, bringing the first half of its fiscal 2027 to $164.2 billion.
  2. AMD reported Data Center segment revenue of $8.5 billion for the third quarter of 2026, up 97% year on year, and Broadcom reported AI semiconductor revenue of $16.7 billion for its quarter to 2 August 2026, up 221%.
  3. Gartner forecast on 10 June 2026 that global data-centre electricity consumption will reach 565 terawatt-hours in 2026, up 26%, with AI-optimised servers using about 175 terawatt-hours, or 31% of the total.
  4. Meta announced on 9 January 2026 agreements for up to 6.6 gigawatts of nuclear power by 2035 from Vistra's existing plants and from TerraPower and Oklo reactors not yet built.
  5. CBRE reported that North American primary-market data-centre vacancy fell to 1.4% in the first half of 2026, with a record 7,481 megawatts under construction and 80.4% of it already preleased.

Every major AI chip vendor is on course for its largest year. Nvidia's data-centre revenue reached $164.2 billion in the six months to 26 July, with the latest quarter at $89.0 billion, up 117%. Broadcom's AI revenue was $35.9 billion in the first three quarters of its fiscal year, the latest up 221%, and AMD's Data Center segment $21.0 billion in the first nine months, the latest quarter up 97% as MI450 shipments began. TSMC targets 120,000–130,000 wafers a month of CoWoS capacity by year end. GPU-hours became more expensive: Silicon Data's H100 index rose from $2.00 in December 2025 to $2.88 on 24 October 2026, and CME announced compute futures, since delayed to early 2027.

Contracted capacity ran well ahead of operating capacity. OpenAI said in April that it had secured 10 gigawatts of US capacity and set a 30-gigawatt target for 2030, while Epoch AI estimated its Abilene campus at about 421 megawatts of IT power in July against 1.2 gigawatts planned; a 600-megawatt Abilene expansion was dropped in March. The Department of Energy announced a 10-gigawatt campus in Ohio, with Nvidia's guarantee of its debt cut from about $250 billion to up to $105 billion in August. Meta moved its 960-megawatt El Paso campus into a $14.3 billion joint venture with BlackRock-managed funds. CBRE reported record-low vacancy of 1.4% and a record 7,481 megawatts under construction in North America's primary markets in the first half.

Electricity became the binding input. Gartner forecasts data-centre consumption of 565 terawatt-hours in 2026, up 26%, with AI-optimised servers at about 175 terawatt-hours; EPRI's February update projects US data centres at 9–17% of national electricity by 2030. Meta's January agreements for up to 6.6 gigawatts of nuclear power by 2035 were the largest of their kind, but most of that supply arrives after 2030. PJM's July auction cleared at its $325 cap for the third consecutive time and fell about 6,831 megawatts short of its reliability requirement. A Microsoft Research study put a typical chatbot response at a median 0.31 watt-hours and a long reasoning response at 3.91, so per-prompt efficiency is improving while reasoning workloads grow.
