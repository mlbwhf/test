Geography

In 2026 to date, the US-China model gap has held near parity at 2.7% while the US share of new compute rose to 78%

  1. The top US model led the top Chinese model by 2.7% on the LMArena leaderboard in March 2026, according to Stanford HAI's 2026 AI Index, compared with 1.70% in February 2025.
  2. Microsoft estimated that 18.8% of the world's working-age population used generative AI in June 2026, led by the UAE at 73.3%, with the United States at 33.0% and China at 17.5% on a measure that excludes several Chinese domestic tools.
  3. The United States received 78% of the AI compute added worldwide in the first half of 2026 and holds 72.2% of all AI compute added since 2022, against 9.6% for China and 5.1% for the EU, according to Epoch AI.
  4. China had more than 700 million generative AI users in June 2026, a penetration rate above 50%, according to a CNNIC report published on 29 September 2026.
  5. European AI start-ups raised a record $23 billion in the first half of 2026, 55% of all venture funding in the region, according to Crunchbase data compiled for HumanX.

Stanford HAI's 2026 AI Index put the top US model 2.7% ahead of the top Chinese model on LMArena in March 2026, and in September two Chinese models were in the global top four of the Artificial Analysis index. Chinese models accounted for 41% of Hugging Face downloads in the year to February 2026. Compute concentrated further: Epoch AI estimates the US received 78% of the AI compute added worldwide in the first half of the year. A January rule allowed licensed H200 exports to China with a 25% charge, but sales were under 1% of Nvidia's data-centre revenue in the quarter to July, and in June two Anthropic models were withdrawn under US export controls.

Capital remained concentrated in the US and grew quickly in Europe. Crunchbase counted about $319 billion, roughly 88% of AI start-up funding so far in 2026, going to US-headquartered companies, including OpenAI's $122 billion round. European AI start-ups raised $23 billion in the first half, more than in all of 2025, and Mistral was valued at more than €21 billion in September. In China, DeepSeek raised a reported $15-16 billion in October at a valuation reported between $74 billion and $90 billion. Stanford's country totals for 2026 will be published in 2027.

Microsoft estimated that 18.8% of the world's working-age population used generative AI in June 2026, with the UAE at 73.3%, South Korea at 40.6% and the United States at 33.0%. CNNIC counted more than 700 million Chinese generative AI users in June, over half the population, and QuestMobile put Doubao at 419 million monthly users in the third quarter. South Korea's 2026 AI budget of about ₩10.1 trillion tripled its 2025 level, India had onboarded 38,231 subsidised GPUs by March, and Abu Dhabi's MGX closed a $49 billion fund in July. These figures are to date and will be revised as full-year data arrive.
