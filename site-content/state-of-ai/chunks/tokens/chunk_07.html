Comparison

From 2024 to 2026 the cost of fixed capability fell, token volume rose by two orders of magnitude and lab revenue grew in between, with growth rates differing tenfold across platforms

Taken year by year, the three lenses move at very different speeds. Tokens per dollar at the GPT-4 level rose from about 2.1 million in 2024 to 3.2 million in 2025 and 5 million in 2026, a gain of about 2.4 times; most of the improvement since 2023 happened before 2024. At OpenAI's flagship, tokens per dollar were 229,000, 291,000 and 250,000, effectively flat. Volume moved fastest: Google's monthly tokens grew about 49 times from May 2024 to May 2025 and about 7 times in the following year, and Doubao's daily tokens grew about 12 times in 2025 and more than 3 times in the first half of 2026. Combined OpenAI and Anthropic run-rate revenue grew about 4.5 times in 2025 and about 4 times again by the third quarter of 2026; Menlo's enterprise spend estimates changed definition in 2026 and give no comparable multiple.

Across platforms, growth rates differ by an order of magnitude, partly because of different starting points. OpenRouter grew fastest, from tens of billions of tokens a week to nearly 200 trillion, because it began small and caught the move to agents. Google's growth slowed in relative terms as its base grew, from about 49 times in its first year to about 7 times in its second, while its API traffic still quadrupled year on year. Chinese volumes grew at least as fast as American ones on every series we found, and ByteDance's single disclosed figure now exceeds Google's. On revenue, the two leading US labs' combined run rate grew about 18 times in under two years, against about 330 times for Google's token volume, so revenue per token fell by about 95% even as total revenue soared.

  • Google's monthly token volume went from 9.7 trillion (spring 2024) to 480 trillion (May 2025) to 3.2 quadrillion (May 2026); Doubao's daily volume went from 4 trillion (Dec 2024) to 50 trillion (Dec 2025) to 180 trillion (Jun 2026).
  • Combined OpenAI and Anthropic run-rate revenue went from about $6.5 billion (Dec 2024) to $29 billion (end 2025) to $115 billion (Q3 2026): about 18 times in under two years, an order of magnitude less than token volume. Menlo's enterprise spend estimates ($11.5 billion for 2024; $37 billion, restated to $33 billion, for 2025; an annualised $176.8 billion in Sep 2026 on a new definition) are not comparable across that break.
  • Reported business return improved only slightly. Wharton's positive-ROI share went from about 65% to 74% to 80%, McKinsey's EBIT-contribution share held near 37-39%, and the share reporting strong or full returns stayed below one in three.
