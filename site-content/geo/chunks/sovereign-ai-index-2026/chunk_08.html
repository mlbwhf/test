6. Compute and money

Announced sovereign compute runs well ahead of what is operating

Most sovereign money buys infrastructure. The table lists the main national programmes with their headline and, where available, delivered figures. Four kinds of money appear and should never be added together: budget appropriations, state equity funds, sovereign-wealth vehicles, and 'mobilisation' headlines that count expected private investment.

Delivery lags announcement. Epoch AI's data-centre tracking shows Stargate UAE with no capacity operating in November 2026, against a first 200 MW projected for December. HUMAIN's 600,000-GPU figure is a three-year ceiling; its signed October 2026 deal covers about 27,000 GPUs and 84 MW through 2027. Nvidia, the clearest commercial proxy, reported more than $30 billion of sovereign AI revenue in its fiscal 2026 and has given only growth rates since.

Fact check

Numbers to stop quoting

Widely repeatedWhat the evidence supports
“Perplexity holds about 48% of Russia's AI-chatbot market (because it stayed accessible while rivals restricted access).”[11 Nov ET verification] The number has a source but the framing is wrong. Statcounter recorded Perplexity at 47.92% of AI-chatbot-referred page views in Russia in April 2026 (all platforms), but Statcounter measures clicks from chatbots through to websites, not market share or users; Perplexity fell to 21.33% in September 2026 and was 39.34% in October 2026, when Gemini had 32.44% and ChatGPT 23.38%. Statcounter does not track GigaChat or Alice AI at all. Web-traffic data for 2025 put ChatGPT near 40% and DeepSeek at 27.8%. The access-restriction explanation is not supported by any source found.
“South Korea runs on Naver's HyperCLOVA X / HyperCLOVA X is Korea's most-used AI model.”Naver closed its standalone Clova X chatbot in April 2026, and Korea's ministry dropped Naver Cloud from the national foundation-model project on 15 January 2026. ChatGPT's Korean app had 18.76 million monthly users in October 2026 (Mobile Index), about 91% of AI-app users. HyperCLOVA X's reach is through Naver search: AI Briefing ~30 million monthly users (May 2026). [11 Nov ET verification] Statcounter (Oct 2026) shows Gemini 65.25% and ChatGPT 23.62% of chatbot-referred page views in Korea and does not track Naver. On Hugging Face the official naver-hyperclovax org logged 33,507 downloads in 30 days vs 724,713 for LG's LGAI-EXAONE.
“Fugaku-LLM serves Japan's public sector.”No evidence of government adoption was found. Japan's Digital Agency began trialling three domestic LLMs on its 'Gennai' platform in July 2026: NTT's tsuzumi 2, Fujitsu's Takane 32B and PFN's PLaMo 2.0 Prime. [11 Nov ET verification] Hugging Face: the Fugaku-LLM org's 3 repos logged 24 downloads in the last 30 days (14,584 all-time).
“India is OpenAI's second-largest ChatGPT market.”True in February 2026 (100 million weekly users). On 3 November 2026 OpenAI's India managing director said India had become ChatGPT's largest market by weekly active users, with the user base doubling since January.
“The IndiaAI Mission has 34,000 GPUs.”34,000+ was the May-June 2025 count. 38,231 GPUs were onboarded by 25 March 2026 (Lok Sabha), and the IT minister announced 20,000 more in February 2026; the end-2026 target is 100,000.
“Falcon models have been downloaded 45 million times.”TII has since reported 55 million+ downloads across the Falcon ecosystem (2025); its Q1 2024 figure was 43 million. [11 Nov ET verification] Hugging Face API: TII's 137 public repos total 68.7M all-time downloads and 378,202 in the last 30 days (12 Nov 2026).
“Mistral AI is valued at $14 billion.”That was the September 2025 Series C (EUR 11.7B). Mistral's EUR 3 billion Series D on 8 September 2026 valued it at more than EUR 21 billion post-money (~US$24B).
“Le Chat is Mistral's consumer assistant.”Mistral renamed Le Chat to Vibe on 28 May 2026; quote the new name with 'formerly Le Chat'.
“Aleph Alpha is Germany's independent sovereign AI champion.”Canada's Cohere agreed to acquire Aleph Alpha (announced 24 April 2026); the combined group keeps the Cohere name and Aleph Alpha shareholders hold about 10%.
“Stargate UAE is live / went live in 2026.”Epoch AI's satellite-based tracking shows 0 MW operational at Stargate UAE as of November 2026, with the first two 100 MW buildings projected for December 2026.
“Saudi Arabia's HUMAIN is deploying 600,000 Nvidia GPUs.”600,000 is an 'up to' ceiling over three years across Saudi Arabia and the US. The deal HUMAIN signed in October 2026 covers up to 84 MW and about 27,000 GPUs through 2027, starting with ~6,000 B300s in Q4 2026.
“Most national 'sovereign' models are home-grown from scratch.”Counterpoint's H1 2026 index found 56% of sovereign LLMs are adapted from foreign bases (Llama 38%, Qwen 20%, Mistral 19% of those). CNAS puts from-scratch models at about 40% of tracked model projects (Aug 2026).
“Chinese models take 61% of OpenRouter tokens.”61% is the Chinese share among the top-10 models only, Feb-Mar 2026. Platform-wide snapshots in April 2026 showed ~51%, and Chinese models' share of US enterprise traffic was 30-46% weekly. [11 Nov ET verification] See the newer verified entry (week of 2 Nov 2026).
“Doubao has 382 million users; Qwen's app 167 million.”Those are May-June 2026 readings. QuestMobile's September 2026 figures: Doubao 419.08 million MAU, Qwen app 158.17 million (down), DeepSeek 122.65 million.
“The EU is building four AI gigafactories with EUR 20 billion.”No site has been chosen. The formal EuroHPC call for up to seven gigafactories closed on 12 November 2026, with awards expected in early 2027; public funding reported at EUR 10 billion plus ~EUR 20 billion hoped-for private money.
“Kazakhstan's KazLLM has 600,000 users.”Sources disagree on whether 600,000 refers to users or to requests; ISSAI reports 140,000+ downloads. Quote neither as 'users' without the caveat.
“StatCounter shows ChatGPT's (or any chatbot's) market share in a country.”[11 Nov ET verification] Statcounter's AI-chatbot series counts page views on Statcounter-tracked websites that were referred by a chatbot (clicks out of answers), based on ~3 billion monthly page views. It is not users, prompts or sessions, it tracks only six global assistants (no Doubao, GigaChat, Alice, Naver, Le Chat), and October 2026 shows a series-wide break (ChatGPT worldwide 80.47% Sep -> 51.03% Oct).
“Two Chinese models sit in the global top four (Artificial Analysis).”[11 Nov ET verification] On Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index v5.0.1 (12 Nov 2026) the nine highest-scoring distinct models are all from US labs; the best Chinese model is Moonshot's Kimi K3.1 Pro at 46.5 (#10, the top open-weights model). The September readings (Qwen3.8-Max 58, Kimi K3 57.1) were on an earlier index version and are not comparable.
“Chinese models are 61% of OpenRouter traffic.”[11 Nov ET verification] Latest complete week (from 2 Nov 2026): Chinese authors had 47.2% of text requests (DeepSeek 31.8%, Z.ai 7.4%, Qwen 5.7%, Tencent 2.3%) vs 38.7% for OpenAI, Google and Anthropic; by tokens, Chinese models are 83.4% of the top-10 models' volume. State the metric (requests vs tokens) and the week.
