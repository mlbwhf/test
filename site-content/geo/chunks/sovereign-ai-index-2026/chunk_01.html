At a glance
  • We catalogue 58 sovereign or national AI model families across 31 countries and regions. About 40 are described as trained from scratch and about 13 are adaptations of foreign open-weight models such as Meta's Llama, Google's Gemma, Alibaba's Qwen or Nvidia's Nemotron.
  • Measured by developer downloads, sovereign models are a niche: in the 30 days to 12 November 2026, 31 national model makers together recorded about 13.6 million Hugging Face downloads, about 5.8% of the 234.5 million for Alibaba's Qwen alone (Report AI calculation from Hugging Face data). France's Mistral accounts for most of that national total.
  • Measured by capability, the frontier is American and Chinese: 15 of the top 20 models on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index (v5.0.1, November 2026) come from US developers and 5 from Chinese ones. The best-scoring model from any other country is Mistral Large 4, at 26.1 against 64.6 for the leader.
  • Measured by home-market use, local champions win only where foreign assistants are blocked or weak: in China, ByteDance's Doubao had 419 million monthly users in September 2026; in South Korea, ChatGPT had about 91% of AI-app users despite years of investment in local models.
  • The money is mostly going into compute rather than models: national programmes in the UAE, Saudi Arabia, India, Japan, South Korea, Canada and the EU buy chips and data centres, then run a mix of imported and local models on them.
