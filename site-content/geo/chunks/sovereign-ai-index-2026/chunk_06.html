On capability leaderboards and developer platforms, the US and China take almost every top spot

On the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index (v5.0.1, 12 November 2026), the top nine models are all from US developers, led by Claude Fable 5.2 at 64.6; the best Chinese model is Kimi K3.1 Pro at 46.5, the highest-ranked open-weight model. No model from India, Japan, Russia or Germany appears on the index.

On OpenRouter, a developer marketplace that routes requests to many models, Chinese developers took about 47% of requests in the week beginning 2 November 2026 and US developers about 39% (Report AI calculation by developer country); DeepSeek alone had about 32%.
