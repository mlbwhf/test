Definitions

Terms used in this index

Sovereign AI (umbrella)
A state's capacity to develop, run and govern AI on infrastructure, data and models it controls, rather than renting them from foreign providers. Definitions are contested: 'hard' sovereignty means self-sufficiency across the stack; 'soft' sovereignty means control over the layers that matter for a given use case.
Sovereign infrastructure (compute)
Domestic data centres and GPU clusters under national legal control, funded by budgets, state banks or sovereign-wealth vehicles.
Sovereign models - from scratch
Foundation models pre-trained from random initialisation by a national lab or champion.
Sovereign models - adapted (continued pre-training / fine-tune of foreign open weights)
A national model built on a foreign open-weight base (Llama, Gemma, Qwen, Mistral, Nemotron) with local data.
Sovereign deployment of foreign models
Foreign proprietary or open models run in-country under local data residency, often via 'sovereign cloud' offers.
Sovereign data
Legal and practical control over national training corpora and the data pipeline (licensed national-library, news and administrative text).

Frequently asked

What is sovereign AI?

Sovereign AI is a country's capacity to develop, run and govern AI on infrastructure, data and models it controls, rather than renting them from foreign providers. In practice it covers four layers: domestic compute, national models (from scratch or adapted), control of training data, and in-country deployment of foreign models under local rules.

Which countries have built their own AI models?

Report AI's November 2026 index catalogues 58 national model families across 31 countries and regions, including France (Mistral), the UAE (Falcon, Jais, K2), Saudi Arabia (ALLaM), India (Sarvam, BharatGen), Japan (PLaMo, LLM-jp, tsuzumi), South Korea (EXAONE, A.X, Solar), Switzerland (Apertus), Singapore (SEA-LION), Russia (GigaChat, Alice AI) and China (Qwen, DeepSeek, Doubao, Kimi, GLM).

Are sovereign AI models popular?

Mostly not, outside China. In the 30 days to 12 November 2026, 31 national model makers together had about 13.6 million Hugging Face downloads, about 5.8% of Alibaba's Qwen alone, and no model from outside the US and China ranks in the top 20 of the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index.

What is the most popular sovereign AI model?

By home-market users, China's Doubao (ByteDance) with about 419 million monthly users in September 2026. Among models outside the US and China, France's Mistral leads on developer downloads and capability scores.

How much are governments spending on sovereign AI?

Announced figures run to hundreds of billions, but they mix budgets, state funds, sovereign-wealth vehicles and private 'mobilisation' totals that cannot be added together. Delivered compute is much smaller: Stargate UAE had no capacity operating in November 2026, and India's common compute portal had about 38,000 GPUs onboarded by March 2026.

Methodology & sources

Figures are compiled from company disclosures, regulators, research firms and reputable reporting, and cross-checked against Report AI's own index pages. Each value carries its as-of date, source and a confidence rating: HIGH where the primary source was read, MEDIUM where it rests on consistent reputable secondary reporting (most figures, because many primary pages cannot be opened from our research environment), DERIVED where it is our own arithmetic, and CONTESTED where credible sources disagree. A year means the calendar year the figure describes; 2026 values are the latest available and marked “to date”. Weekly and monthly user counts, and forecasts and outcomes, are never compared as if they were the same measure. The catalogue was compiled from developer announcements, model cards and national programme documents. Popularity measures come from the Hugging Face API (organisation download totals, read 12 November 2026), Artificial Analysis (Intelligence Index v5.0.1), OpenRouter's public rankings, StatCounter, and national app and audience trackers (QuestMobile, Mediascope, Mobile Index). Each measures something different; none is a count of users across all channels. Shares of downloads and requests by country, and counts of models by build type, are Report AI calculations (DERIVED).
