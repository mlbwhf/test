1. The ranking

The United Arab Emirates and Singapore lead the world in AI use; the United States ranks 21st

Microsoft's AI Economy Institute estimates the share of each economy's working-age population that used a generative AI product in a period, from anonymised telemetry adjusted for device share, internet penetration and population. In the second quarter of 2026, 31 economies were above 30%, up from 26 a quarter earlier.

The top of the table is dominated by small, high-income economies with near-universal connectivity and active government programmes: United Arab Emirates (73.3%, #1), Singapore (64.3%, #2), Ireland (49.9%, #3), France (49.6%, #4) and Norway (49.4%, #5). Among the largest economies, the United Kingdom is at 43.7%, Germany 32.3%, Japan 24.7%, Brazil 20.2%, India 18.5% and Indonesia 14.8%.
