Machine-Learning Models Compared: We Ran 13 Algorithms on 19 Datasets

A viral clip ranked nine models on one unnamed dataset. We ran 13, from Naive Bayes to the TabPFN transformer, on 19 public benchmark datasets with five random splits each, to show what wins, by how much, and how much the answer depends on how you test.

At a glance
  • Report AI ran 13 machine-learning classifiers on 19 public tabular datasets from the Grinsztajn, Oyallon and Varoquaux (2022) benchmark, with 1,000 training rows and five random splits per dataset, in November 2026.
  • TabPFN v2 had the highest mean accuracy, 76.7%, and was best on 17 of 19 datasets; CatBoost was second at 75.7%. Naive Bayes ranked last on average.
  • Single runs mislead: across 95 combinations of one dataset and one random split, 7 different models came first, and the overall winner was first in only 80.0% of them.
  • Gradient-boosted trees (CatBoost, LightGBM, XGBoost, HistGB) and random forests occupy most of the top half; the nine-model viral chart's order is not reproducible because it names no dataset.
  • In TabArena's default leaderboard view of 11 November 2026, the top 21 of 108 entries are tabular foundation models; the best non-foundation entry is the TabM-A neural network (tuned and ensembled, Elo 1517, 22nd), and the best tree model is LightGBM tuned and ensembled (Elo 1411, 32nd).
