2. The ranking

TabPFN v2 finished first; boosted trees and random forests filled most of the top half

Each model was trained with its library's default settings on 1,000 rows and tested on 2,000 different rows, five times per dataset with fresh random splits. All 19 tasks are balanced yes/no problems, so 50% accuracy is chance. We rank models by their average rank across datasets, the standard way to compare learners across tasks of different difficulty, and also report mean accuracy, ROC AUC and wins.

TabPFN v2 averaged 76.7% accuracy and a mean rank of 1.32. CatBoost followed at 75.7%. The fastest model, Naive Bayes, took a median 2.1 milliseconds to train and predict; the slowest, TabPFN v2, took 49.3 seconds on our 4-core CPU.
