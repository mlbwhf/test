At a glance
  • Report AI classified 93 notable large language models released between 2023 and November 2026 on eight dimensions: architecture, training stage and reasoning, openness, modality, size, specialisation, training-compute class and origin.
  • Reasoning went from rare to standard: in Report AI's table, 43 of 64 models released in 2025–26 (67.2%) can reason before answering, against 1 of 27 released in 2023–24.
  • Mixture-of-experts has become the default design for open-weight models: 27 of 43 open-weight models in the table released in 2025–26 (62.8%) use it, against 5 of 18 in 2023–24.
  • Closed models disclose almost nothing about their design: 25 of the 32 closed models in the table do not say even which architecture family they use.
  • On Artificial Analysis's Intelligence Index v5.0.1 (read 15 Nov 2026), the best proprietary model, Claude Fable 5.2 at max effort, scored 64.6 and the best open-weights model, Kimi K3.1 Pro, scored 46.5 — a gap of about 18 points.
