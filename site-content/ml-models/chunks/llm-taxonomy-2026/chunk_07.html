8. What the categories change for users

The category behind a chatbot decides its speed, price, privacy and failure modes

The same chat window can sit in front of very different models, and apps increasingly route each request to a different one. The table below translates each category into what a user or buyer notices. It is Report AI's synthesis of the definitions above, with links to the indexes that hold the figures.
