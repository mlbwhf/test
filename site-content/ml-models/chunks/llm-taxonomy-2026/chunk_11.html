Methodology & sources

Figures are compiled from company disclosures, regulators, research firms and reputable reporting, and cross-checked against Report AI's own index pages. Each value carries its as-of date, source and a confidence rating: HIGH where the primary source was read, MEDIUM where it rests on consistent reputable secondary reporting (most figures, because many primary pages cannot be opened from our research environment), DERIVED where it is our own arithmetic, and CONTESTED where credible sources disagree. A year means the calendar year the figure describes; 2026 values are the latest available and marked “to date”. Weekly and monthly user counts, and forecasts and outcomes, are never compared as if they were the same measure. The table covers 93 notable LLMs chosen to include the leading model families of each major developer and at least one example of every category. Each row was checked against the model's Hugging Face card and configuration, its paper on arXiv, or the developer's official page (HIGH); where those could not be opened, against Wikipedia or aggregators such as OpenRouter and Artificial Analysis (MEDIUM). Training compute is Epoch AI's estimate. Category counts, shares and the frontier classification rule are Report AI calculations (DERIVED) and describe the table, not the whole market. Hugging Face model counts were read from the Hub on 15 November 2026. These reports compile and verify secondary data with original analysis; they are not original surveys.
