# Google Search Console audit — report-ai.org (9 Aug 2026)

Source: GSC Coverage + Performance exports, last 3 months.

## The headline

**54 clicks from 29,247 impressions = 0.18% CTR, average position ~41.**

The site is being *seen* (29k impressions) and almost never *clicked*. That is not a
content-quality problem — it is a structural one. Three fixable causes account for
most of it.

| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| Clicks (3 mo) | 54 |
| Impressions (3 mo) | 29,247 |
| CTR | 0.18% |
| Avg position | ~41 (desktop 41.4, mobile 63.5) |
| Known URLs | 697 |
| Indexed | 366 (**52%**) |
| Not indexed | 331 |

## Problem 1 — The same content lives at 3–4 different URLs (CRITICAL)

The site serves identical pages under `/indexes/`, `/library/`, and `/stats/`.
This looks like an un-redirected site restructure. **11 confirmed duplicate slugs
split 4,718 impressions and 7 clicks between competing URLs.**

Worse: **Google often prefers the *old* namespace.** The `/library/` copy
frequently outranks the `/indexes/` copy the site actually promotes:

| Slug | Old URL | New URL |
|---|---|---|
| llm-market-statistics-2026 | /library/… **1,377 imp, pos 7.3** | /indexes/… 439 imp, pos 10.9 |
| ai-safety-governance-statistics-2026 | /stats/… **569 imp, pos 8.3** | /library/… 363 imp, pos 10.6 |
| ai-models-benchmarks-statistics-2026 | /library/… 233 imp, pos 7.6 | /stats/… 225 imp, pos 6.4 |
| geography-of-ai | /geography-of-ai 85 imp | /indexes/… 348 imp, pos 9.7 |
| ai-healthcare-statistics-2026 | /reports/… 18 imp | /indexes/… 255 imp |
| agentic-ai-statistics-2026 | /reports/… 55 imp | /indexes/… 169 imp |
| compare | /stats/compare 74 imp, pos 6.2 | /indexes/compare 70 imp, pos 15.8 |
| sovereign-ai-index-2026 | /reports/… 9 imp | /indexes/… 111 imp |
| ai-slop-content-pollution-internet | /indexes/dark-side… 4 imp | /reports/dark-side… 226 imp |
| ai-algorithmic-bias-discrimination | /indexes/dark-side… 4 imp | /reports/dark-side… 32 imp |
| ai-economics | /library/ai-economics 1 imp | /indexes/ai-economics 51 imp |

### Fix — 301 redirect the dead namespaces onto `/indexes/`

Use the **Redirection plugin** (already installed). Regex rules, not per-URL:

```
Source: ^/library/(.*)$        →  Target: /indexes/$1        (301)
Source: ^/stats/(.*)$          →  Target: /indexes/$1        (301)
Source: ^/geography-of-ai/?$   →  Target: /indexes/geography-of-ai/   (301)
```

Caveats before firing:
- `/stats/state-of-ai-by-year/` has no obvious `/indexes/` twin → map manually to
  `/indexes/state-of-ai/state-of-ai-2022-2026/`.
- `/library/enterprise-ai/ai-generated-content-statistics-2026/` → the live slug is
  `/indexes/enterprise-ai/ai-generated-content-statistics/` (no `-2026`) → manual rule.
- `/library/real-world-ai/*` and `/library/dark-side-of-ai/*` belong under
  `/reports/`, not `/indexes/` → two extra rules:
  `^/library/real-world-ai/(.*)$ → /reports/real-world-ai/$1`
  `^/library/dark-side-of-ai/(.*)$ → /reports/dark-side-of-ai/$1`

**Direction of consolidation:** onto `/indexes/` even though `/library/` currently
ranks better. Every internal link, hub page, and nav item points at `/indexes/`, and
a 301 passes the equity across. Consolidating onto the URL the site doesn't link to
would mean rewriting the whole internal-link graph.

## Problem 2 — Thin glossary pages are eating the head terms (CRITICAL)

**The glossary pulls 11,154 impressions — 38% of the entire site — and converts
7 clicks.** Average position ~82 (page 9).

| Glossary page | Impressions | Clicks | Position |
|---|---|---|---|
| /glossary/ai-agent/ | **5,419** | 2 | 82.0 |
| /glossary/foundation-model/ | 1,237 | 0 | 82.1 |
| /glossary/multimodal-ai/ | 955 | 0 | 82.2 |
| /glossary/multiagent-system/ | 841 | 1 | 81.8 |
| /glossary/hyperscaler/ | 742 | 1 | 76.5 |

These pages are Google's chosen answer for the site's biggest keywords —
"ai agent" (2,803 imp), "ai agents" (1,929), "multimodal ai" (599),
"foundation models" (285) — and they are too thin to rank above page 8.

Meanwhile the page that *should* own "ai agent" —
`/indexes/enterprise-ai/agentic-ai-statistics-2026/` — gets **169 impressions at
position 47.7**. The definition page is outranking and suppressing the real index.

### Fix
1. **Expand the top 5 glossary pages** to meet the standing content-depth rule —
   they currently violate it. These five alone carry 9,194 impressions (31% of site).
   Each needs: real data tables, sourced figures, and a prominent link to its index.
2. **Cross-link hard**: every glossary term → its data index, above the fold.
3. **Make the index pages target the head term** in title/H1 (e.g. "AI Agent
   Statistics 2026" should rank for "ai agents", not a 200-word definition).

## Problem 3 — Only 52% of the site is indexed, and it's getting worse

697 known URLs → 366 indexed, **331 not indexed**. The gap widened sharply:
**on 24 Jul 2026, not-indexed jumped 224 → 331 (+107) in a single day.**

| Reason | Pages |
|---|---|
| Discovered – currently not indexed | **147** |
| Excluded by 'noindex' tag | 120 |
| Not found (404) | **51** |
| Page with redirect | 9 |
| Crawled – currently not indexed | 3 |
| Blocked by robots.txt | 1 |

"Discovered – not indexed" at 147 pages means **Google is choosing not to crawl** —
a crawl-budget and site-quality signal, almost certainly driven by Problems 1 and 4.

### Fix
- Clear the 51 404s (export the URL list from GSC → Pages → Not found, then redirect
  or restore). The Coverage export ships counts only, so the URLs must be pulled from
  the GSC UI.
- Fixing Problems 1 and 4 directly reduces the discovered-not-indexed pile.

## Problem 4 — The RSS reposts confirm the duplicate-content diagnosis

241 root-level auto-republished posts: **3,844 impressions, 17 clicks.** They rank
for other people's paper titles — "dynadebate arxiv", "on the position bias of
on-policy distillation", "helixvision 280". This is the anti-pattern already named
in the house rules, now measurable: it burns crawl budget that the real indexes need.

### Fix — as already queued (task #7)
Noindex/prune the arxiv and newsletter reposts; rewrite the genuinely valuable ones
as original on-site briefs.

---

## Problem 5 — CTR is near-zero even where ranking is fine

398 queries rank in the top 20 → 1,439 impressions → **4 clicks**.
Example: *"ai roi enterprise survey 2025 2026"* sits at **position 2.7 with 94
impressions and 0 clicks.**

Two honest readings, and both probably apply:
- **Titles/meta aren't earning the click.** Fixable with rewritten SEO titles.
- **AI Overviews are absorbing the answer** — exactly the zero-click dynamic our own
  [AI Search Statistics](https://report-ai.org/indexes/enterprise-ai/ai-search-statistics-2026/)
  index documents (Pew: 8% CTR with an AI summary vs 15% without).

⚠️ **Caveat worth stating plainly:** a chunk of the "striking distance" queries look
synthetic — `maxenorworld.xyz ai native company evaluation`, `ai48026`,
`helixvision 280`, `growthwizsyam.com…`. Treat that top-20 CTR number as soft; do not
build strategy on it.

## Priority order (highest impact first)

1. **301 the `/library/` and `/stats/` namespaces onto `/indexes/`** — recovers
   4,718 split impressions, single biggest structural win. Redirection plugin, ~30 min.
2. **Expand the top-5 glossary pages + cross-link to indexes** — addresses 31% of all
   site impressions currently converting at 0.06%.
3. **Clear the 51 404s** (needs URL list from GSC UI).
4. **Noindex/prune the RSS reposts** — frees crawl budget for the 147 discovered-not-indexed.
5. **Rewrite SEO titles** on pages ranking top-20, targeting the actual query wording.
6. **Internal-link sweep** (already queued) — feeds crawl equity to unindexed pages.

Items 1, 2, 4, 5, 6 are all executable through the WordPress connection.
Item 3 needs the owner to export the 404 URL list from the GSC UI.
