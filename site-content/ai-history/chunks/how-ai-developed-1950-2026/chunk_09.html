6. Cost and diffusion

Capability got about 190 times cheaper in three and a half years and reached a billion weekly users

Prices fall fastest for a fixed level of capability. The Stanford HAI AI Index 2025 found that the cheapest model scoring at GPT-3.5's level on MMLU fell from $20 per million tokens in November 2022 to $0.07 in October 2024, a 280-fold drop. On our own GPT-4-level series the cheapest blended price fell from $37.50 per million tokens at GPT-4's launch in March 2023 to about $0.20 in October 2026 (Exhibit 6). Epoch AI's estimates of the annual decline range from about 10× to 50× a year depending on the task.

The frontier is priced differently. In 2026 the newest top-tier models listed at $10 per million input tokens and $50 per million output tokens for Claude Fable 5 and 5.1, before Anthropic cut Fable 5.2 and Mythos 5.2 to $8 and $40 in November. Cheap capability got cheaper while the best capability stayed expensive, a pattern covered in depth in State of AI Tokens 2026.

Use followed price. ChatGPT reached 100 million weekly active users in November 2023, a year after launch, and more than 1 billion on 31 August 2026 (Exhibit 7). The often-quoted '100 million users in two months' was a UBS estimate of monthly users from web-traffic data, not an OpenAI figure. In organisations, McKinsey's share of survey respondents reporting AI use in at least one business function rose from 20% in 2017 to 55% in 2023 and 89% in 2026, although most report limited effects on earnings.
