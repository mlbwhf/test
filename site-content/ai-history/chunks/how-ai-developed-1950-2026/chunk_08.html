5. Capability

Benchmarks that once lasted a decade now saturate within two years of release

MMLU, a test of knowledge across 57 subjects, was published in 2020, when GPT-3 scored 43.9%. GPT-4 reached 86.4% in March 2023 and OpenAI's o1 92.3% in 2024, at which point the test stopped separating leading models. GPQA Diamond, graduate-level science questions designed to be 'Google-proof', went from 59.4% for Claude 3.5 Sonnet in June 2024 to 94.3% for Gemini 3.1 Pro in February 2026.

Coding shows the same curve. On the original SWE-bench the best 2023 result was 4.4%. On SWE-bench Verified, a human-checked subset introduced in 2024, OpenAI's o3 preview reported 71.7% in December 2024, Claude Opus 4.5 80.9% in November 2025 and Claude Opus 5 97.0% on the Vals.ai harness in September 2026. Humanity's Last Exam, launched in January 2025 as a deliberately hard test, rose from about 9% at launch to 54.8% for GPT-6 Astra in October 2026 (Exhibit 5).

Two cautions apply. Vendor-reported and independent results differ: OpenAI reported 25.2% for o3 on FrontierMath in December 2024, while Epoch AI's own later run found about 10%. And the two SWE-bench versions are different tests, so '4.4% to 71.7%' compares unlike measures; our fact-check register corrected that pairing on four pages in November 2026.
