2. Capability

Capability: benchmarks rise from near zero to saturation in one to five years

Benchmark scores are the most quoted and least stable measure of AI progress. Each test measures something narrow, results depend on settings, and most scores are reported by the developer. The table lists the dated results in our dataset; the companion report charts four of them and estimates how long each test lasted.
