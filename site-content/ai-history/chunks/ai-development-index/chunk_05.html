1. Compute

Training compute: from 10⁶ FLOP for the Perceptron to about 10²⁷ for today's frontier

Training compute is the number of arithmetic operations used to train a model. It is the measure most closely linked to capability gains in the deep-learning era and the one with the longest consistent record. All values are Epoch AI estimates; labs rarely disclose them, and Epoch rates each estimate's confidence.

Growth rates: Epoch estimates a doubling roughly every 20 months before 2010 and about every 6 months afterwards (about 4–5× a year), and about 5× a year for frontier language models since 2020 (90% interval 4–6×).
