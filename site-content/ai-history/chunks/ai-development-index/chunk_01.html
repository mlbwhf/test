At a glance
  • The largest estimated AI training run grew from about 40 operations for Claude Shannon's maze-solving mouse in 1950 to about 9.4×10²⁶ for OpenAI's GPT-6 Astra in September 2026, according to Epoch AI.
  • Epoch AI estimates frontier language-model training compute has grown about 5× a year since 2020, and the cost of those runs about 3.5× a year.
  • Capability tests are saturating within one to two years of launch: SWE-bench Verified went from 49% to 97.0% between late 2024 and September 2026.
  • The cheapest GPT-4-level model cost $37.50 per million tokens in March 2023 and about $0.20 in October 2026.
  • Global corporate AI investment, in constant 2021 dollars, rose from about $17 billion in 2013 to $490 billion in 2025 (Our World in Data, from the Stanford HAI AI Index).
  • Reported AI incidents in the AI Incident Database rose from 233 in 2024 to 362 in 2025, as cited by the AI Index 2026.
