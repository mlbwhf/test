5. Adoption and research output

Adoption: from a specialist tool to a billion weekly users

Adoption is measured two ways here: by organisations (McKinsey's annual survey) and by people (ChatGPT weekly active users disclosed by OpenAI). Neither measures depth of use or financial impact. Research output is shown through notable-model counts by affiliation and country, and AI publications and patents.
