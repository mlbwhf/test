3. Cost

Cost: buying a fixed capability gets cheaper every year, while building the frontier gets dearer

Two cost measures run in opposite directions. The price of using a model at a fixed level of capability has fallen by one to two orders of magnitude every few years; the cost of training the newest frontier model has risen by about 2.4–3.5× a year, according to Epoch AI.
