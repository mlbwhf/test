The milestone register: 70 dated events from 1943 to 2026

Every milestone below carries its date, what it showed, a source and a confidence rating. Events from 2022 onward are covered in more detail in the State of AI timeline and annual editions.

All milestones

DateMilestoneWhat it showedSourceConfidence
1943McCulloch–Pitts neuronFirst mathematical model of a network of simplified neurons computing logical functions.McCulloch & Pitts, Bulletin of Mathematical Biophysics 5MEDIUM
1950-10Turing's 'Computing Machinery and Intelligence'Replaces 'Can machines think?' with the imitation game (Turing test). Turing predicted that in about 50 years machines with ~10^9 storage would leave an average interrogator no more than a 70% chance of a correct identification after five minutes.Turing, Mind 59(236)MEDIUM
1955-08-31Dartmouth proposal coins 'artificial intelligence'McCarthy, Minsky, Rochester and Shannon propose a summer study; the document introduces the term. Proposed as a '2 month, 10 man study'.AI Magazine reprint of the proposalMEDIUM
1956-06-18Dartmouth Summer Research ProjectConventional founding event of AI as a field. Ran roughly 18 Jun–17 Aug 1956 (about eight weeks) per Solomonoff's notes; other accounts say six weeks.Wikipedia, Dartmouth workshop (citing Solomonoff)MEDIUM
1958-07Rosenblatt's perceptron unveiledFirst learning machine to attract mass publicity; the US Navy's ONR demonstrated it in July 1958 (a simulation on an IBM 704). Mark I Perceptron used a 400-sensor 'retina' and ~1,000 weights; Epoch estimates its training compute at ~6.95×10^5 FLOP.Rosenblatt, Psychological Review 65(6) 1958; Wikipedia Mark I PerceptronMEDIUM
1966Shakey the robot (SRI)First mobile robot to reason about its own actions; its STRIPS planner became the basis for later automated planners.Holloway, Making Things ThinkMEDIUM
1966-01ELIZAWeizenbaum's pattern-matching 'therapist' program; first widely known chatbot and first demonstration of people over-attributing understanding to software.Weizenbaum, Communications of the ACM 9(1), 1966MEDIUM
1969Mansfield Amendment and 'Perceptrons'US law pushes DARPA toward mission-oriented research; Minsky & Papert's book highlights single-layer perceptron limits.Wikipedia, AI winterMEDIUM
1973Lighthill reportUK Science Research Council review concludes AI had failed its grand promises; UK funding is sharply curtailed. Debated on BBC TV in June 1973.Holloway / Wikipedia AI winterMEDIUM
1974DARPA cuts AI fundingMarks the start of the first AI winter in the US. DARPA cancelled ~$3M a year of Speech Understanding Research grants at Carnegie Mellon.Wikipedia, AI winterMEDIUM
1980R1/XCON at Digital Equipment CorporationFirst commercially significant expert system; starts the expert-systems boom. Most sources put savings at ~$25M a year; by 1986 it had processed 80,000 orders at 95–98% accuracy.McDermott (AAAI 1980); secondary historiesMEDIUM
1982Japan's Fifth Generation project (ICOT)Ten-year MITI programme for logic-programming computers; spurred Western counter-programmes. Initial budget ~¥54 billion over ten years; total spending a little under ¥57 billion (~$320M) by the end.Wikipedia, Fifth Generation Computer SystemsMEDIUM
1986-10-09Backpropagation popularisedRumelhart, Hinton & Williams show multi-layer networks learn useful internal representations. Nature 323:533–536.NatureMEDIUM
1987Lisp-machine market collapses; NETtalkEnd of dedicated AI hardware boom marks the second AI winter; same year NETtalk shows a neural net learning to pronounce text. NETtalk training compute ~2.8×10^10 FLOP (Epoch).Holloway; Epoch AI via searchMEDIUM
1997-05-11Deep Blue beats KasparovFirst defeat of a reigning world chess champion by a computer in a match under tournament conditions. Won the rematch 3½–2½ (two wins, one loss, three draws); evaluated ~200 million positions per second. Kasparov had won the 1996 match 4–2.IBM / History.com, via searchMEDIUM
1997-11LSTMHochreiter & Schmidhuber's long short-term memory, the recurrent architecture behind most sequence models until the Transformer.Neural Computation 9(8)MEDIUM
1998-11LeNet-5LeCun et al.'s convolutional network for handwriting recognition, deployed for cheque reading. Training compute ~2.8×10^12 FLOP (Epoch, 'Confident').Epoch AI via search summaryMEDIUM
2005-10DARPA Grand Challenge wonStanford's Stanley completes a 132-mile desert course autonomously, a year after no vehicle finished. 6 h 53 min 58 s; in 2004 the best vehicle managed ~7.4 miles.DARPA / Stanford Racing TeamMEDIUM
2006-07Deep belief netsHinton, Osindero & Teh show deep networks can be trained layer by layer, reviving neural-network research.Neural Computation 18(7):1527–1554MEDIUM
2010First ImageNet challenge (ILSVRC)Establishes the benchmark that later showcased deep learning. Winning top-5 error 28.2% (NEC-UIUC).ILSVRC results via searchMEDIUM
2011-02-16IBM Watson wins Jeopardy!Open-domain question answering beats the show's two best champions on TV. Two-game totals: Watson $77,147; Ken Jennings $24,000; Brad Rutter $21,600.IBMMEDIUM
2011-10Siri ships on iPhone 4SFirst voice assistant in mainstream consumer use.Search summaryMEDIUM
2012-09-30AlexNet wins ImageNetGPU-trained deep convolutional net wins by a wide margin, triggering the deep-learning era. Top-5 error 15.3% vs 26.2% for the second-best entry; ~60M parameters; trained on two GTX 580 GPUs; ~4.7×10^17 FLOP (Epoch).Krizhevsky, Sutskever & Hinton, NIPS 2012; Epoch AIMEDIUM
2014-01-26Google acquires DeepMindBig Tech begins buying frontier AI labs. Price undisclosed; reported at $400M (Re/code) to more than $500M (The Information).TechCrunchMEDIUM
2014-06-10Generative adversarial networksGoodfellow et al. introduce GANs, the main image-generation method until diffusion models.arXiv 1406.2661MEDIUM
2014-09Sequence-to-sequence learningSutskever, Vinyals & Le show end-to-end neural machine translation with LSTMs. Training compute ~5.6×10^19 FLOP (Epoch).Epoch AI via searchMEDIUM
2015-12ResNet beats human-level ImageNet errorResidual networks win ILSVRC 2015. Top-5 error 3.57%, below the 5.1% single-annotator human estimate.He et al., arXiv 1512.03385MEDIUM
2016-03-15AlphaGo defeats Lee SedolFirst defeat of a 9-dan Go professional without handicap, a decade earlier than many expected. 4–1 in Seoul, 9–15 Mar 2016; AlphaGo had beaten Fan Hui 5–0 in Oct 2015 (Nature, 27 Jan 2016).Nature / pressMEDIUM
2017-06-12The Transformer'Attention Is All You Need' introduces the architecture behind every frontier language model since. 28.4 BLEU on WMT14 English–German; ~7.4×10^18 FLOP training compute (Epoch).arXiv 1706.03762MEDIUM
2017-10AlphaGo ZeroLearns Go from self-play alone, without human games; among the first models trained on >10^23 FLOP. ~6.5×10^20 FLOP of direct training compute (Epoch, 'Confident'); OpenAI's 2018 figure of ~3.4×10^23 included self-play data generation.Epoch AI Notable AI Models CSVMEDIUM
2018-05OpenAI 'AI and Compute'First widely cited measurement of exploding training compute. More than 300,000× growth from AlexNet to AlphaGo Zero; 3.4-month doubling time (2012–2018).OpenAIMEDIUM
2018-10BERTPre-trained bidirectional Transformer resets most language-understanding benchmarks. BERT-Large ~340M parameters; ~2.8×10^20 FLOP (Epoch).Google; Epoch AIMEDIUM
2019-02-14GPT-2 and staged releaseOpenAI withholds the full model at first over misuse concerns, starting the release-norms debate. 1.5B parameters; ~1.9×10^21 FLOP (Epoch).OpenAI; Epoch AIMEDIUM
