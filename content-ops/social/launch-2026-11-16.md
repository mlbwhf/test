### 1 — State of AI series
The State of AI series from Report AI is now live: eleven reports on how AI is used, funded and governed.

The 11 themes are usage, spending, finance, work, models and prices, tokens, infrastructure and energy, geography, industries, risk, and regulation.

Each report covers 2024, 2025 and 2026 to date. Every figure carries its source, its date and a confidence rating.

Two examples from the year-over-year comparison:
• ChatGPT had 300 million weekly users in 2024 and 800 million in 2025.
• Big-tech capital expenditure was $245 billion in 2024 and $413 billion in 2025.

Series hub: https://report-ai.org/indexes/state-of-ai/
Year over year: https://report-ai.org/indexes/state-of-ai/state-of-ai-year-over-year/

### 2 — Machine-learning models compared (our own benchmark)
A viral chart ranked nine machine-learning models on one unnamed dataset. We wanted to know how much of that ranking survives proper testing.

Report AI ran 13 models on 19 public tabular benchmark datasets, with five random splits each. The models ranged from Naive Bayes to the TabPFN v2 transformer.

• TabPFN v2 averaged 76.7% accuracy and won 17 of the 19 datasets. CatBoost came second at 75.7%.
• Across 95 single train/test runs, 7 different models came first at least once.
• Skipping feature scaling cut SVM accuracy by 7.9 points and a neural net's by 6.4. Tree models were unaffected.

The full results, method and downloadable data are here: https://report-ai.org/reports/machine-learning-models-compared/

### 3 — Sovereign AI Index 2026
Report AI's Sovereign AI Index 2026 covers 58 national and sovereign AI models across 31 countries and regions.

Most coverage counts the models and the money behind them. This index also asks whether anyone uses them, measured by downloads, users, leaderboard ranks and home-market share.
