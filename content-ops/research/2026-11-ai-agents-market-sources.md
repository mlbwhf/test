## Analysis

**The spread.** The lowest 2030 figure is Gartner's enterprise cross-functional agents at over $23B. Among full-market forecasts the lowest is Omdia at $41.8B. The highest is Gartner's agentic AI software spending at $985B. That gap is about 23x–43x. Including IDC's $1.3T for 2029 widens it further, but IDC's figure is total AI spending and shouldn't be in the comparison.

**Definitions explain almost all of it.** There are four kinds of number here:
1. **Standalone agent revenue (research firms).** This is revenue from products sold as agents or agent platforms. Base values cluster tightly at $7–8B for 2025: MarketsandMarkets, Precedence, Fortune, GMI, Grand View AI agents, Polaris, Straits, BCC, Mordor. 2030 endpoints are $46–53B: MarketsandMarkets $52.6B and $46.0B, BCC $48.3B, Grand View's older edition $50.3B, TBRC $53.2B. Extending the 2032–34 forecasts back to 2030 at their own CAGRs (DERIVED; assumes constant growth) gives similar numbers:
   - Fortune AI agents ≈ $54B; Precedence AI agents ≈ $52B; Grand View AI agents ≈ $55B
   - MarketsandMarkets agentic 2032 edition ≈ $45B; Precedence agentic ≈ $47B
   - Straits ≈ $52B; Polaris ≈ $43B; Mordor ≈ $40B; GMI ≈ $39B; Fortune agentic ≈ $36B
   - Outliers: Grand View agentic ≈ $85B (its 2025 base is about twice everyone else's at $16.2B), MarketsandMarkets 2033 edition ≈ $75B, Emergen ≈ $16.5B
   - So the comparable cluster is roughly $36–85B, a ~2.4x spread. The core is $40–55B.
2. **Enterprise-only standalone.** Omdia $41.8B, MarketsandMarkets enterprise $46.0B, Gartner cross-functional over $23B. These sit at or below the first group, as expected.
3. **Embedded spending envelopes.** Gartner $985B for 2030, and $206.5B for 2026. These count all spend on software that has agentic features, so most of it is existing application spend being relabelled. The size of this envelope relative to the standalone figures is what separates the low and high ends of the range.
4. **Top-down value-capture TAMs.** BofA $155B and Goldman's implied over $468B. These estimate what agents could capture, not measured revenue. The 2025 base of every research-firm forecast is under 10% of Gartner's $86.4B for 2025, which is further evidence these are different measures.

**Which forecasts measure broadly the same thing.**
- Group 1 is comparable within itself, with the outliers and base-year problems noted below.
- "AI agents" and "agentic AI" reports from the same firm usually differ (Fortune $251B vs $139B for 2034; Precedence $236B vs $199B), so the two labels aren't interchangeable even within a firm.
- Gartner's $29.2B 2026 standalone line is the nearest Gartner figure to group 1.
- Deloitte's $35B is an average of others' estimates. Don't count it as a separate data point.

## Conflicts and gaps

1. **Precedence agentic AI base year.** The Sep 2025 GlobeNewswire release labels $10.86B as 2025. The report page gives 2025 as $7.55B and 2026 as $10.86B, and the two are consistent at the stated 43.84% CAGR. The release looks mislabelled.
2. **MarketsandMarkets agentic AI has two editions.** $7.06B (2025) → $93.20B (2032), and $19.33B (2026) → $205.88B (2033). They give 2025 as $7.06B and $11.56B (secondary) respectively. One syndication of the old edition is dated 17 Aug 2026.
3. **MarketsandMarkets AI agents** was revised from $47.1B to $52.62B for 2030. Several blogs still cite the old figure.
4. **Grand View AI agents has two editions.** The 2030 figure ($50.31B) only appears in secondary reposts. The current edition ends in 2033.
5. **GMI has two bases.** $5.9B for 2024 at 38.5%, or $7.7B for 2025 at 39.5%, both ending at $105.6B in 2034. One search summary credited GMI's numbers to Grand View, so watch for that misattribution.
6. **BCC base value** is about $8B in its press release and $5.7B on a partner page.
7. **Emergen's US figure** ($15.2B in 2024) is larger than its global figure ($5.2B in 2025). One of them is wrong.
8. **Gartner's agent-spend figures don't match each other.** $201.9B (early 2026, secondary only), $206.5B (May 2026 press release) and $29.2B (Sep 2026 table, different category). Secondary blogs also cite $78.1B and $219.9B for 2030; I couldn't verify either.
9. **IDC's $1.3T** is widely misquoted as "agentic AI spending". The press release headline itself says agentic AI will exceed 26% of worldwide IT spending and $1.3T in 2029, but IDC's text describes the $1.3T as total AI spending growing 31.9% a year, with agentic AI as the main driver.
10. **Omdia's growth rate.** A secondary "175% CAGR" claim doesn't match its own $1.5B→$41.8B path (about 95% a year).
11. **Goldman's 60%** may be a share of the profit pool rather than revenue. The over-$468B figure is my derivation.
12. **Salesforce redefined Agentforce ARR** in Q2 FY27, so it can't be compared with earlier quarters. No Q2 paid-deal count was found.
13. **Microsoft** didn't disclose a new Copilot paid-seat total in FY27 Q1. The 50M-agents figure is secondary only.
14. **Only secondary sources:** BofA, Mordor's headline numbers, the TBRC 2030 figure, Straits, Roots, DataM, GlobalData, ServiceNow, and most OpenAI figures.
15. **Dates not found** for MarketsandMarkets enterprise agentic AI, Emergen, Straits, Polaris, Roots and GlobalData.
16. **Statista's values** are paywalled and not verified.
