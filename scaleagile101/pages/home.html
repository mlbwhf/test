Scaled Agile in 30 seconds

A single Agile team is easy to coordinate. Now imagine fifty teams building one product. Scaled Agile is the set of roles, routines, and planning events that keep all of them aligned to the same goals, moving on the same heartbeat, and shipping working software together. SAFe® (the Scaled Agile Framework) is the most widely adopted way to do it.

The problem

Many teams, one product, lots of dependencies. Without alignment you get duplicated work, surprises at integration, and slow delivery.

The SAFe answer

Organize teams into an Agile Release Train, plan together every 8–12 weeks, and inspect & adapt continuously.
