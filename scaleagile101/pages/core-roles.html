The core roles

Scaled Agile is a team sport. Here's who's on the field and what each person is responsible for.

On the team

Scrum Master / Team Coach

A servant leader for one Agile team. Facilitates events, removes impediments, and coaches the team toward continuous improvement.

Product Owner (PO)

Owns the team backlog. Defines and prioritizes stories so the team builds the most valuable thing next.

The Agile Team

A small, cross-functional group of developers, testers, and specialists who can define, build, test, and deliver value together.

On the Agile Release Train

Release Train Engineer (RTE)

The "chief Scrum Master" of the train. Facilitates ART events, manages risks and dependencies across teams, and keeps value flowing. Think of them as the conductor of the orchestra.

Product Management

Owns the ART backlog and the vision. Decides what the train builds and in what order, working closely with the POs.

System Architect / Engineering

Defines the technical vision and architectural runway so teams can build at speed without painting themselves into a corner.

Business Owners

A small group of key stakeholders accountable for the business outcomes of the train. They assign business value and help steer.

New to the RTE role specifically? It's one of the most pivotal — and in-demand — roles in scaled Agile. We cover it in depth on the roles guide and in the glossary.
