Chapter 3

Cadence & events

Scaled Agile runs on a heartbeat. Once you know the rhythm, the whole framework clicks into place.

Everything in SAFe happens on a cadence — a fixed, predictable rhythm. The big one is the Program Increment (PI): a timebox of about 8–12 weeks made up of several short iterations. Each PI starts with planning and ends with a review, so the whole train always knows where it stands.

The key events

★

PI Planning

The signature SAFe event. The entire ART gathers (in person or virtually) for 1–2 days to plan the next PI together — building a shared plan, surfacing dependencies, and committing to objectives. If you attend one event, make it this one.

↻

Iterations (Sprints)

Inside each PI, teams work in short iterations (usually two weeks) — planning, building, and reviewing in a tight feedback loop.

⇄

ART Sync

Regular checkpoints (a Coach Sync and a PO Sync) where teams surface progress, risks, and dependencies between planning events. Facilitated by the RTE.

▦

System Demo

Every couple of weeks the train shows a working, integrated increment of the whole system — not slides, real software. It's the honest measure of progress.

✦

Inspect & Adapt (I&A)

At the end of each PI, the ART reviews results, runs a structured problem-solving workshop, and commits to improvements for next time.
