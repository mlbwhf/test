What's the difference between SSM and SASM?

SSM (SAFe® Scrum Master) is the foundational 2-day course for any Scrum Master in SAFe. SASM (Advanced Scrum Master) is the advanced 2-day course for experienced Scrum Masters and team coaches who facilitate at the program level, coach teams through dysfunction, and drive continuous improvement across the Agile Release Train.

What's included in the tuition?

2 days of live-virtual training with an Authorised SAFe Instructor (SPC/ASPC), the official SAFe® Advanced Scrum Master (SASM) exam (first attempt included if taken within the eligibility window), the attendee workbook, one year of SAFe Studio access, and a digital certificate and badge on passing. No hidden fees.

What are the prerequisites?

There are no hard prerequisites, but the course is designed for those with Scrum Master experience. Holding SAFe Scrum Master (SSM) or having served as a Scrum Master is strongly recommended so you get the most from the advanced material.

Is the SASM certification worth it?

Yes — SASM signals you can coach high-performing teams beyond the basics: facilitating cross-team events, removing systemic impediments, and improving flow across the ART. It's a strong differentiator for senior Scrum Masters and team coaches in SAFe enterprises.

How is the course delivered?

Live-virtual over 2 consecutive days via Zoom, in small interactive cohorts led by an Authorised SAFe Instructor (SPC/ASPC). Includes hands-on facilitation practice, coaching scenarios, and real-world examples. We don't sell pre-recorded videos.

What's the exam format?

The SASM exam is online, closed-book, and taken after the course within your eligibility window. Your first attempt is included in the tuition. We provide a study guide and practice questions to maximise your first-time pass rate.

What if I don't pass on the first attempt?

Retakes are available through Scaled Agile after a short waiting period, with a retake fee paid to Scaled Agile. We include a study guide, practice questions, and a prep session to help you pass the first time.

How long is the certification valid?

SASM certification is valid for one year. Renewal requires maintaining your SAFe Studio / Community Platform membership and paying the annual renewal fee.

Can I get a refund if my plans change?

Yes — we offer flexible options including transfer to a future cohort within 12 months if your plans change. Contact us to discuss specific terms.

Do you offer group or corporate discounts?

Yes. Groups of 3+ from the same organisation receive volume discounts, and we run dedicated private cohorts for teams. Contact an advisor for a quote.

Is training available in other languages?

Yes. We deliver cohorts in multiple languages including English, French, Spanish, Arabic, and German. Contact us to find a cohort in your language.

Who teaches the course?

The Advanced Scrum Master course is taught by an Authorised SAFe Instructor (SPC/ASPC). (A certified SPCT is required only for SPC, ASPC, and RTE courses; SASM is delivered by an Authorised SAFe Instructor.)
