# Video — Short Explainer (30–60s script)

Output format: a shooting script with two columns worth of information per beat — SAY (spoken words) and SHOW (on-screen text / visual note).

**[0:00–0:03] HOOK.**
SAY: One-sentence question or bold claim about [CONCEPT].
SHOW: Big on-screen text version of the hook (max 6 words).

**[0:03–0:10] SETUP.**
SAY: Why [CONCEPT] trips people up / why it matters to [AUDIENCE].
SHOW: 2–3 word keyword captions.

**[0:10–0:40] PAYLOAD.**
SAY: The explanation in 2–3 crisp steps or a before/after contrast. Concrete example required.
SHOW: Numbered captions or a simple diagram note per step.

**[0:40–0:50] PROOF or PITFALL.**
SAY: One real-world consequence, stat, or common mistake.
SHOW: The number or mistake as on-screen text.

**[0:50–0:60] CTA.**
SAY: One action: [FOLLOW / COURSE / LINK IN COMMENTS].
SHOW: CTA text + handle.

Style notes: spoken language (contractions, short sentences), no jargon without an instant gloss, script should read aloud in under 60 seconds (~150 words spoken).
