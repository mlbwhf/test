# Video — Talking Head (2–4 min script)

Output format: a spoken script in natural paragraphs, with [B-ROLL: ...] and [ON-SCREEN: ...] cues inline where they help.

**Cold open (first 10 seconds).** Start mid-thought with the most interesting claim or question about [TOPIC]. No "Hi, welcome back".

**Frame (20–30 seconds).** Who this is for and what they'll be able to do by the end. One sentence of credibility ([EXPERIENCE / CONTEXT]) woven in, not announced.

**Body (3–5 segments).** Each segment: one idea, one example, one transition. Mark each with [ON-SCREEN: segment title].

**Recap (20 seconds).** The 2–3 things to remember, phrased as actions.

**CTA (10 seconds).** One action: [SUBSCRIBE / COURSE / DOWNLOAD]. Mention where the link is.

Style notes: write for the ear — short sentences, rhetorical questions sparingly, no bullet-speak. Target ~140 words per minute.
