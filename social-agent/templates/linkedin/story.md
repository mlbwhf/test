# LinkedIn — Story Post

Structure:

**Opening scene (1–2 lines).** Drop the reader into a specific moment: [WHERE / WHEN / WHAT HAPPENED]. Present tense works well.

**Tension (1 short paragraph).** What went wrong or what was at stake.

**Turn (1–2 short paragraphs).** The decision, realization, or intervention that changed the outcome.

**Lesson (2–3 bullets or one tight paragraph).** What this taught about [TOPIC], stated so the reader can apply it without having lived the story.

**Close (1 line).** A grounded takeaway or invitation to share their version. No moralizing.

**Hashtags.** 2–4 on the final line.

Style notes: real, specific, anonymized. Numbers and sensory details over adjectives. First person if the account voice is personal; "we" for a company account.
