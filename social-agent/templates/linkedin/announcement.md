# LinkedIn — Announcement

Structure:

**Hook (1–2 lines).** A concrete, curiosity-raising opener tied to [WHAT IS BEING ANNOUNCED]. No "We're excited to announce".

**Why it matters (2–3 short paragraphs).** The problem the audience has, and how [WHAT IS BEING ANNOUNCED] addresses it. One concrete detail or number beats three adjectives.

**Key facts (bulleted).**
- [DATE / LOCATION / FORMAT]
- [WHO IT IS FOR]
- [PRICE OR OFFER, if any]

**Call to action (1 line).** One action only: [LINK OR NEXT STEP].

**Hashtags.** 3–5, on the final line, from the account's preferred set.

Style notes: short paragraphs (1–3 lines), no emoji walls (max 2 total), write at the reader not about ourselves.
