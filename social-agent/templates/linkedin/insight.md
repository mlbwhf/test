# LinkedIn — Insight / Thought Leadership

Structure:

**Hook (1 line).** A sharp claim or counterintuitive observation about [TOPIC]. It should be defensible, not clickbait.

**Context (1 short paragraph).** Where this observation comes from — a client situation, a cohort question, a pattern seen repeatedly. Anonymize specifics.

**The insight (2–4 short paragraphs or a numbered list).** The actual substance: what most people get wrong, what to do instead, why it works. Each point one idea.

**Takeaway (1–2 lines).** What the reader should do differently starting this week.

**Soft close (optional, 1 line).** A question to the reader OR a low-key pointer to [RELATED COURSE/RESOURCE] — never both.

**Hashtags.** 3–4 on the final line.

Style notes: no listicle clichés ("Let that sink in", "Agree?"), no engagement bait, favor the account's voice.
