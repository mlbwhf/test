# Trainyard French (France) style guide

- Audience: SAFe trainers and participants in a professional training. Use "vous" (vouvoiement). Imperatives use the vous form ("Ajoutez", "Choisissez").
- Terminology: use glossary-fr-FR.tsv's "Trainyard French" column. If a source text uses a SAFe term not in the glossary, prefer the official SAFe fr-ca wording and add a note.
- Keep in English: brand and product names (Trainyard, SAFe®, Scaled Agile, SAFe Studio, SAFe CoFund, Miro, Jira, Flowise, Groq, Stripe), company and case names (Terrific Transport Corporation, TTC, MedLink), acronyms (ART, PI, WSJF, RTE, PO, LPM, MVP, OKR, KPI, NPS, LACE, VMO, ROAM, CD, CI, DoD).
- Placeholders: keep {0}, {1}… exactly, as many times as in the source; reorder freely for natural French.
- Case: keep ALL CAPS when the source is ALL CAPS (accents kept: "ÉQUIPE"). Sentence case otherwise; French does not capitalise every word of a title.
- Variant: French as written in France (fr-FR). SAFe terms follow the official SAFe French glossary, with France usage where it differs (Business Owner, agilité métier, valeur métier, business case).
- Typography (Imprimerie nationale): a narrow non-breaking space (U+202F) before ; ! ?, a non-breaking space (U+00A0) before the colon and %, and inside « » guillemets. Use « » for quotes, ’ for apostrophes, … for ellipsis. Keep symbols and emoji as in the source.
- Vocabulary: e-mail, parking, week-end, télécharger (download) and téléverser or importer (upload).
- Length: buttons and chips stay short (aim for at most 1.3× the English). Prefer a shorter synonym over a long literal translation.
- Numbers, currency and dates come from code; translate only words around them.
- Sample data (example epics, key results, stories) is translated, as a French class would write it.
- Single words that may be a code value rather than text (e.g. "Draft", "Done"): translate as UI text, but add a note "check: may be a value".
