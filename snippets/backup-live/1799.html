( 01 ) — SAFe & our courses

SAFe, and the courses we teach.

What is SAFe?
SAFe (the Scaled Agile Framework) is the world’s most widely adopted framework for applying Lean, Agile, and DevOps practices at enterprise scale. It gives large organizations a proven operating model to align strategy, fund value streams, and deliver value faster across many teams — not just one.
Which SAFe courses do you offer?
We deliver the full SAFe curriculum — Leading SAFe (SA), SAFe Scrum Master (SSM), SAFe for Teams (SP), and SAFe Product Owner/Product Manager (POPM), through advanced tracks like Implementing SAFe (SPC), Release Train Engineer (RTE), Lean Portfolio Management (LPM), SAFe for Architects (ARCH), and SAFe DevOps (SDP), up to our Advanced SPC (ASPC) — plus AI-Native courses that extend SAFe for the AI era. How do I choose the right course?
It depends on your role and goal. New to Agile → SAFe Scrum Master; product or business → POPM; engineering or architecture → SAFe for Architects; leading teams → Leading SAFe; coaching or driving change → SPC; portfolio or PMO → LPM; coordinating multiple teams → RTE. Not sure? Take our free Career Selector or contact us and we’ll recommend a path.
Who teaches your courses?
Every course is led by a certified SAFe Practice Consultant Trainer (SPCT) — the highest SAFe teaching credential, held by roughly 125+ people worldwide. Agile Agilist is a Scaled Agile Gold Partner.
Do you offer AI-Native and advanced courses?
Yes. Alongside the standard SAFe catalog we offer AI-Native Foundations and advanced consultant tracks such as Advanced SPC (ASPC), plus focused micro-credentials.

( 03 ) — Certification & Scaled Agile

Exams, certification, and membership.

What certification do I earn?
On passing the exam you earn the official Scaled Agile certification for your course (for example SAFe Agilist, SAFe Scrum Master, or SAFe Practice Consultant), recognized globally.
How does the SAFe exam work?
The exam is taken online through your SAFe Studio account after the class. Your first attempt is typically included and available for a set window after the course; each exam has its own passing score and time limit, provided with your course details.
How long is a SAFe certification valid?
SAFe certifications are valid for one year and are renewable. Renewal keeps your certification current and maintains your SAFe Studio membership and digital badge.
What is SAFe Studio membership?
Enrollment includes one year of SAFe Studio, Scaled Agile’s member platform — your digital courseware, practice exam, the certification exam, digital badge, and a library of resources and templates.
Do your courses qualify for PDUs/SEUs?
Yes. Our courses typically qualify for PMI PDUs, Scrum Alliance SEUs, and other continuing-education credits. Details are listed with each course.
What’s the difference between an SPC and an RTE?
SPC (SAFe Practice Consultant) leads SAFe transformations, trains others, and launches Agile Release Trains — a change-agent role. RTE (Release Train Engineer) is a servant leader who facilitates ART events, removes impediments, and optimizes flow day-to-day.
Are there prerequisites?
Most foundational courses (Leading SAFe, SAFe Scrum Master, POPM) have no prerequisites. Advanced courses (SPC, ASPC, RTE) have recommended experience or certifications listed on each course page. New to Agile? Start with SAFe Scrum Master or Leading SAFe.
