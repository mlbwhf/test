( 02 ) — The mutation loop

Four moves, on repeat.

Mutation is not an event — it is a rhythm. These four moves run continuously, turning constant change from a threat into an operating advantage.

MOVE 01

Sense

Read the market, technology, and internal signals early — leading indicators that a change is coming.

MOVE 02

Reconfigure

Reshape teams, value streams, and structure to fit the new reality — continuously, not in a shock.

MOVE 03

Re-fund

Move money to the newest, highest-value bet on a Lean cadence — funding follows evidence.

MOVE 04

Re-skill

Grow people into the next operating model — so the organization mutates without leaving its talent behind.

Run the loop faster than your environment changes and disruption stops being a threat — it becomes the terrain you are built for.
