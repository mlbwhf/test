What is Business Agility

Scaled Agile’s 7 Core Competencies of Business Agility

Business Agility is the ability of an entire organization — not just IT — to compete and thrive in the digital age by responding quickly to market changes. Scaled Agile defines it through seven reinforcing competencies. We train, coach, and embed all seven.

Lean-Agile Leadership

Leaders model and teach the principles. Without it, every other competency stalls.

Team & Technical Agility

High-performing cross-functional teams with built-in quality and continuous delivery skill.

Agile Product Delivery

Customer centricity + design thinking + DevOps to deliver a continuous flow of value.

Enterprise Solution Delivery

Lean-Agile practices for the largest software, hardware, and cyber-physical systems.

Lean Portfolio Management

Strategy + funding + governance reimagined for value streams, not projects.

Organizational Agility

Lean-thinking people and the agile structures to act on opportunity at speed.

Continuous Learning Culture

Learning organization habits: experimentation, relentless improvement, innovation.

AI Enablement (our addition)

The 8th competency for 2026+: AI fluency, MLOps, and prompt patterns embedded in every layer.
