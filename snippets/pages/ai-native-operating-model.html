Bolt-on AI is a business dead end.

( 02 ) — The AI-Native ladder

From assisted to AI-Native.

Most enterprises sit on the bottom rung — a few copilots, ungoverned. The value lives higher up, where AI is embedded in how the whole organization operates.

RUNG 01

Assisted

Individuals use copilots ad hoc. Gains are personal and invisible to the business.

RUNG 02

Augmented

Teams standardize AI into shared workflows — consistent, but still bounded by old process.

RUNG 03
TARGET

AI-Native

AI is designed into every role, ceremony, and decision — governed, measured, compounding.

RUNG 04

Agentic

AI agents run whole workflows end-to-end under human oversight — the frontier we build toward.

The jump that matters is from rung 2 to rung 3 — where AI stops being a personal tool and becomes native to the operating model. That is the work.
