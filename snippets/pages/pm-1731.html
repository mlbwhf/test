( 01 ) — What POM is

A Product Operating Model funds products, not projects.

…an operating model where the unit of investment is a persistent product team aligned to a value stream, funded by a Lean Budget with guardrails instead of a project business case, and accountable for outcomes measured in OKRs — not the on-time, on-scope delivery of a fixed scope.

Product Managers own the “what and why,” engineering owns the “how,” and Lean Portfolio Management connects strategy to execution in 90-day loops — not annual planning cycles.

Working definition · SAFe-aligned

( 03 ) — Why POM + SAFe

SAFe gives a Product Operating Model the operating mechanics.

Cagan-style product thinking tells you why to shift. SAFe gives you the working ceremonies, funding mechanism, and governance to actually run one at enterprise scale.

THE CONNECTIVE TISSUE

Lean Portfolio Management

Replaces the annual funding cycle with quarterly portfolio sync, Lean Budgets per value stream, and Guardrails for Finance partnership. Without LPM, POM is theatre.

THE STRUCTURAL UNIT

Value Streams & ARTs

Identify the value stream, build the ART around it, fund it persistently. Now you have a real product team — not a project staffed by borrowed people.

THE PEOPLE

POPM + SPP roles

Product Owner / Product Manager + SAFe Product Practitioner roles — certified, trained, coached. The people who actually run the model in flight.
