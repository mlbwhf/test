Innovation ceremonies

Innovation embedded in SAFe events — not bolted on

Four lightweight ceremonies that plug directly into the PI cadence you already run. No new framework, no new committee, no new budget line. Just better outcomes from the events you have.

Mutation Workshops

Replaces 1 IP iteration day

Half-day workshop where teams identify external mutations (market, tech, regulatory) and design a small experiment to test their organization’s response. Embedded in the IP iteration so it doesn’t compete with delivery.

Innovation Playground conceptQuarterly

Failure Forums

Replaces 1 PI retrospective

Structured forum where teams share a failed experiment, the hypothesis it tested, and what the org learned. Leaders attend — and budget the next experiment based on what they heard. Kills the “failure is career-limiting” reflex.

Mutation Readiness conceptEvery PI

Mental Model Challenges

Embedded in Inspect & Adapt

Leadership-only session held inside I&A. Each leader names one mental model they hold about the business and invites the room to challenge it. The output is one assumption that gets tested next PI — with budget.

Mutation Readiness conceptEvery PI

AI Experimentation Sprints

2-week sprint inside the PI

One ART sprint each PI is reserved for AI experimentation. Teams pick a workflow, prototype an AI-augmented version, and report what changed. The portfolio funds the winners and kills the rest. Fast, cheap, compounding.
