( 03 ) — Innovation ceremonies

Embedded in SAFe events — not bolted on.

Four lightweight ceremonies that plug directly into the PI cadence you already run. No new framework, no new committee, no new budget line — just better outcomes from the events you have.

Mutation Workshops

↺ 1 IP DAY

Teams identify external mutations — market, tech, regulatory — and design a small experiment to test their org’s response. Embedded in the IP iteration so it never competes with delivery.

INNOVATION PLAYGROUND · QUARTERLY

Failure Forums

↺ 1 PI RETRO

Teams share a failed experiment, the hypothesis it tested, and what the org learned. Leaders attend — and budget the next experiment based on what they heard. Kills the “failure is career-limiting” reflex.

MUTATION READINESS · EVERY PI

Mental Model Challenges

IN I&A

A leadership-only session inside Inspect & Adapt. Each leader names one mental model they hold about the business and invites the room to challenge it. The output: one assumption tested next PI — with budget.

MUTATION READINESS · EVERY PI

AI Experimentation Sprints

2-WK SPRINT

One ART sprint each PI is reserved for AI experimentation. Teams pick a workflow, prototype an AI-augmented version, and report what changed. The portfolio funds the winners and kills the rest. Fast, cheap, compounding.
