## Who we are, and who handles your data

Report AI is published by Agile Agilist. **Report AI publishes measurement; Agile Agilist
sells consulting and remediation services.** We keep those two things apart deliberately,
and that separation extends to your data.

## What happens when you subscribe or contact us

Your details are stored in a HubSpot CRM operated by Agile Agilist. Every Report AI
contact is tagged at the point of submission as originating from report-ai.org, and:

- **Report AI subscribers are excluded from all Agile Agilist marketing.** We maintain a
  suppression list for this. Subscribing to Report AI's newsletter will not result in
  sales contact from Agile Agilist.
- **Contacting us is not consent to be emailed.** If you write to us, we use your details
  to reply. You join the newsletter only if you separately tick the box.
- **The newsletter is its own subscription type.** You can leave it without affecting
  anything else, in one click, from any email we send.

## What we collect

- **Newsletter:** your email address, the date you subscribed, and what you consented to.
- **Contact form:** your name, email, optional organisation, enquiry type and message.

We do not load HubSpot's marketing tracking script on report-ai.org, so subscribing or
contacting us does not place marketing cookies in your browser.

## How long we keep it

Newsletter subscribers: until you unsubscribe, plus a record of the unsubscribe so we
don't email you again by accident. Contact enquiries: two years, then deleted, unless the
exchange is an ongoing correspondence or a published correction.

## Your rights

Ask us for a copy of what we hold, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it —
info@report-ai.org. If you're in the UK or EU you can also complain to your data
protection authority.

## Corrections

If you tell us a published figure is wrong, we treat that as editorial correspondence,
not marketing. We publish corrections with the previous value still visible.

## Note for the site owner (do not publish this section)

This text asserts three things that must actually be true, or it becomes a
misrepresentation rather than a disclosure:

1. The suppression list exists and is applied to **every** Agile Agilist marketing send —
   not most of them, every one. (`integration-plan.md` §1, control 4.)
2. `ra_source` is set on every submission from day one, so the exclusion has something
   to key off.
3. The HubSpot tracking script is genuinely not loaded on report-ai.org.

If any of those three stops being true, this page has to change the same day.
