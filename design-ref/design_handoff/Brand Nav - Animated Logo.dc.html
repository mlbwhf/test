How the logo & menu behave
01

Logo entrance

On load, the mark wipes in left-to-right and settles upward (~0.9s) — like it's being drawn. A light sweep then glides across the letterforms every few seconds, and the accent dot twinkles. Subtle, brand-coloured, never gimmicky.

02

Two-tier menu

A quiet mono masthead carries trust signals (Gold Partner, rating, languages); the main bar stays focused on navigation. Links use an underline that draws in on hover. Right side holds the two actions only — consult + enroll.

03

Training mega-menu

19 certs are too many for a flat list, so Training opens a hairline grid of the five tracks — SAFe tracks tinted teal, the AI-Native flagship on its own navy/purple row. Counts are shown so visitors self-route fast.
