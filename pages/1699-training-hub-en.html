02 · Coaching

Certified is not hired. We coach you to the role.

A certificate is a keyword filter you get past, not a job you get offered. This section is the part most training pages leave out: which roles these credentials actually open, what those roles pay, and the coaching that turns the badge into the offer.

Where these certifications lead
SA · SSM

Delivery Manager / Agile Delivery Manager

Owns delivery across teams — people, process, dependencies and the blockers in between. In US postings a SAFe credential is one of the signals that gets a CV past the filter; senior listings ask for RTE or SPC outright.

$140K – $235K
SSM

SAFe Scrum Master

The first rung most people climb. Runs the team's events and flow inside a train, rather than a single Scrum team in isolation.

$103K – $138K
POPM

Product Owner / Product Manager

Owns the backlog and the value it delivers. The role where the certification evidence is strongest: in Scaled Agile's 2024 Careers Snapshot, 42% of product-owner postings asked for SAFe experience and 40% required or preferred the certification.

$94K – $130K
RTE

Release Train Engineer

Chief of staff to an Agile Release Train — five to twelve teams, one cadence. The step where pay stops tracking the team role and starts tracking the programme.

$108K – $175K
LSSP · RTE

Solution Train Engineer

The same job one level up: several trains building one integrated solution, usually with suppliers and regulation in the mix.

$102K – $141K
SPC

SAFe Practice Consultant

Licensed to teach SAFe and to launch trains. This is the credential that turns a practitioner into someone an enterprise hires to run the change, internally or as a consultant.

$134K – $207K
SPC

Agile Coach

Coaches teams, leaders and trains rather than running one. Telecommunications, financial services and aerospace pay this role the most.

$156K – $231K
SPC → ASPC

Enterprise Agile Coach

Works across portfolios and with the executive layer. ASPC is the credential that separates this from a team-level coaching title.

$184K – $276K
LPM

Lean Portfolio Manager

Funding, governance and strategy rather than delivery. The highest-paying destination on this list, and the one furthest from where most people start.

$186K – $300K

Typical US ranges — roughly the 25th to 75th percentile — read from Glassdoor and ZipRecruiter in September 2026. Treat them as a shape, not a quote: the sources disagree by as much as $50,000 on the same job title, and pay moves with industry, location and employer far more than with any certificate. These are what the roles pay. They are not what our courses cost, and they are not a promise about your next offer.

How the ladder actually works
01

Inside a team

SSM or POPM. You are certified for the role you already do, and the badge clears the filter on the next application.

02

Across a train

RTE, or Large Solution if your programme spans several trains. Pay moves with scope, and this is where the biggest single step usually happens.

03

Across the enterprise

SPC, then ASPC or LPM. You are no longer running delivery — you are teaching, funding or redesigning it.
