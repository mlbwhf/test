The substantive change — two structures

The Solution Train is no longer the only answer. A value stream network is a loosely coupled alternative where each value stream delivers standalone value, holds its own funding and roadmap, and collaborates only for as long as the shared solution needs it to. Knowing which of the two you are looking at is most of the diagnosis.

Solution Train — tight couplingValue stream network — loose coupling
What it isMultiple ARTs collaborating to build one integrated solution.Multiple value streams collaborating to deliver a coordinated solution.
Where value comes fromIndividual components are worth little alone. The value is in the integration.Each value stream delivers value on its own, and more through the shared solution.
Strategy and fundingSupport for strategy and the funding rationale are linked — same people, same discussions.Each supports strategy independently and is typically funded separately.
Vision and roadmapOne shared vision, intent and roadmap driving every ART.Each has its own, aligned to deliver the shared solution.
Release cadenceTypically released as a single unit, in large batches.Released independently to their own customers, coordinated for the shared solution.
RelationshipParent and child — nested value streams reporting up to a shared solution.Customer and provider — each is a supplier to some and a customer of others.
And when not to use any of it. Not every dependency needs a Solution Train or a network. Product Managers aligning their backlogs directly, an Epic Owner shepherding one shared epic, or a portfolio coordinating its own dependent value streams will all carry a surprising amount of coordination on their own. Scale only as far as you actually need to — a large solution structure imposed where a conversation would have done is its own kind of failure, and recognising that is part of the course.

( 03 ) — Career outcomes

The people who hold more than one train.

Scaled Agile names four groups for this certification. What they share is responsibility for delivery that spans more than one Agile Release Train — and none of them needs another credential first.

Roles this is written for

Change agents & SPCs

Guide enterprises in organising, roadmapping and coordinating large multi-train solutions across complex environments.

Solution Train Engineers

Facilitate large-scale events, sync multi-train execution and streamline delivery dependencies across value stream networks.

Solution managers & system architects

Establish architectural runways and Lean systems engineering practice that let teams innovate inside a system of systems.

Managers & business owners

Align multi-stream investment with corporate strategy, keeping the customer journey unified and supplier collaboration compliant.

Where large solutions usually come from

  • A new system of systems — something large-scale being built for the first time.
  • A change of strategy — a new vision that needs alignment far beyond one train.
  • A merger or acquisition — unifying strategies, consolidating systems, integrating delivery.
  • One customer experience across many products — harmonising a journey that spans several systems.
  • A major technology change — a significant legacy replacement, run in parallel with the old.
  • Delivery that has stopped working — missed deadlines and cross-organisation execution problems.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need another certification first?

No. Scaled Agile lists this course at Foundational level with no prerequisites required. You do not need SPC, RTE or any other credential first. Most people arrive already running a train or coaching an adoption, but neither is a gate.

What are the exam details and passing score?

45 multiple-choice questions, 90 minutes, passing score 80%. Accreditation is awarded on passing, and the exam fee is included in the tuition.

What credential do I earn?

The Large Solution SAFe Practitioner (LSSP) certification, with a verifiable digital badge you can share on LinkedIn. Certified professionals also earn PMI PDUs and Scrum CEUs.

Who is this course for?

Scaled Agile names four groups: SAFe Practice Consultants and change agents; Solution Train Engineers; solution managers and system architects; and managers and business owners. What they share is responsibility for delivery that spans more than one Agile Release Train.

Does the course cover AI?

Yes, and it is a substantial part of the material — AI in advanced systems engineering (model-based engineering, digital twins and threads, predictive maintenance), the SAFe CoPilot assistant, AI-assisted evidence-gathering in the value stream workshops, and how to keep human oversight over AI-generated strategic insight.

Solution Train or value stream network — what's the difference?

A Solution Train is tightly coupled: several ARTs building one integrated solution, where the value is in the integration. A value stream network is loosely coupled: each value stream delivers standalone value, holds its own funding and roadmap, and collaborates only as long as the shared solution needs it to. The network is the substantive addition in this material.

Can we run this privately for one organisation?

Yes, and it is the common case — Scaled Agile expect this class to run mostly as a private cohort, and so do we. The two facilitated workshops are far more useful when everyone in the room shares a solution. Ask us about a private cohort and we will schedule it around your PI boundaries.

How long is it, and what if the dates stop working?

Two days, live online, capped at 18 seats, taught by a Gold SPCT. If the dates stop working we move you to another cohort — no deadline and no fee.
