## What works for them
1. **The entry level is about volume at a low price.** Leading SAFe and SSM are both companies'
   flagships, with the most dates, badges and city pages. Skillbook sells them at $515-599 against
   your $850.
2. **SPC and RTE are their money courses.** Both companies build landing pages, badges and
   in-person or Toronto pages for them.
3. **The long tail is neglected.** LPM, APM, DevOps, ASM, Architects and Business Owner have no
   dates, "Announcing Soon" labels, or 3-year-old pages.
4. **AI is their growth bet, but not AI-Native SAFe.** They rename courses "AI-Empowered" and sell
   their own Gen-AI credentials. KnowledgeHut's AI-Native Foundations isn't scheduled, and
   neither company offers Launching AI-Native ARTs, Value Architect or Leading the AI-Native Org.
