## Microsoft Ads: don't upload
Your rules keep Microsoft Multimedia Ads at the minimum with **no image assets uploaded**. That's
the real opt-out, since the Microsoft UI blocks a negative bid adjustment. So this pack is
**Google only**.

## The gap: you need 3-5 more real photos
Only two library photos actually *show* career progression. Google rotates images and rewards
variety (aim for 3+ landscape and 3+ square). Real, un-staged photos convert best and fit your
"verifiable claims only" rule. For example:
- a graduate holding their certificate after class
- you teaching (in-person AI-Native Foundations, Toronto)
- a participant presenting or facilitating at PI Planning
- a small team at a whiteboard (for the "lead the team" step)

Don't use AI-generated people. They tend to read as fake, which undercuts the trust you're
building with buyers.
